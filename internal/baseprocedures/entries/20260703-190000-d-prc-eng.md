The engage procedure is the universal entry move for any session anchored on a graph entry — explore it, implement it, evaluate it, supersede it, check other work against it. One flow regardless of intent: establish the anchor, receive its chains in full, brief the user, and put the candidate moves to them at the junction. The engagement ends by dispatching — whatever move the user picks runs as its own procedure (capture entered with `anchor`, `supersedes`, or `closes` start inputs covers most acting-on moves), or concludes with an explicit "nothing further".

## unit: anchor

Establish what this engagement is about — a resolved anchor is what advances this step. A seeded anchor (dispatched from another move, or passed as a known entry ID) auto-advances past this prompt; you see it only when the anchor still needs resolving.{{if .anchorHint}} The user's pointer: "{{.anchorHint}}".{{end}} Report `anchor` (one entry — the subject the brief and the junction key on), `targets` for connected entries engaged alongside (their chains are served too), and `goal` when the user has voiced what they want out of it.

- **Entry ID given**: report it directly.
- **Pointer or phrase given**: search the graph from several different angles — different phrasings, plus any known term or ID — and dialogue toward picking the centre with the user. The candidates are theirs to choose from, not yours to assume.
- **Action verb only** ("keep going", "let's evaluate"): if recent dialogue or an active WIP marker resolves to a clear entry, propose it; otherwise ask which entry. Recency ("the last thing we shipped") does not resolve an anchor — it breaks in a shared graph; ambiguity resolves in dialogue with the user.

## unit: brief

The anchor and its surroundings, served in full — read them; summaries elsewhere are pointers, this is the material:

{{.entryChains}}

**Widen before you brief.** Search the graph from several different angles for what the chains above do not already name — a concept phrase from the anchor, the goal phrase, a key term from the chain. Record the searches and what they surfaced in `widenReport`; inspect any promising result in full before it shapes the brief. When the surroundings would overwhelm this dialogue's context, dispatch the explore procedure to a disposable context (a sub-agent running against this server) with the targets and a goal, and fold its briefing in.

Then synthesize `brief`, shaped by the anchor's kind and the goal{{if .goal}} (the user's goal: {{.goal}}){{end}}:

- **Plan (or plan-shaped directive) + implementation intent** — an AC-status table: ✓ done (evidence: partial done signals in the chain), ◐ partial, ☐ remaining. The contract is the union of the entry's acceptance criteria and every open augmenting directive in its downstream chain — fold each augment in as a row. When prior work paused at a slice boundary, the most recent partial done signal usually carries pickup notes; surface them.
- **Signal + orientation intent** — a short narrative: what this is about, current status, what's happened since, what's around it, closing with the orienting question — what does this need?
- **Contract or aspiration + check intent** — a divergence list over the named target work: alignments, divergences, ambiguous items. Surface alignments as well as divergences — a divergence-only list is a hunting expedition, not a fair check.
- **Done signal + evaluate intent** — what landed (from the body and what it closes) against what was committed, inner lens (did it meet the target?) and outer lens (what did it surface?).

These are the recognized shapes, not a closed set — when kind and intent pair differently, adapt: name what the user is engaging on and what they want as outcome; that pair selects the shape. Present the brief in the user's language.

## unit: moves

Put the junction to the user: what should happen with {{.anchor}}? Surface the candidate moves that fit the anchor's kind and current state — a short prioritized offer, not a menu dump. Two flavors:

- **Acting on it** — the outcome is a graph change against the entry: capture a decision on a gap or question, implement a plan or directive, supersede, close via done, decompose, refine, augment mid-implementation.
- **Using it as a lens** — the outcome is findings about other work: evaluate against a closed commitment, compliance-check against a contract, alignment-check against an aspiration, progress-check against a focus.

Closed entries stay engageable — closure ends the open-status derivation, not the dialogue; a closed plan still anchors a retrospective. Wait for the user's explicit pick and relay their answer verbatim: **move** carries `selectedMove` (the move in their words); **conclude** ends the engagement with an explicit nothing-further; **abort** abandons a mis-anchored engagement. When the picked move starts a follow-up procedure (a capture against the anchor, an explore over the neighborhood), start it with this instance as `parent` — the lineage belongs in the session log.
