The evaluate procedure treats evaluation as real work, not a verdict appended to a done: scope what this run judges, carry the judgment out with evidence, and record the evaluation as work the graph can see. Two lenses, generic postures whose concrete meaning comes from the project: **inner is verification** — is the work sound, judged against the project's own guidelines — and **outer is validation** — is it the right thing: working in use, serving the people it's for. A run may cover one lens or both; completeness is a coverage property computed across the graph — other runs and participants cover the rest. Anchor on the done signal (or the closed commitment — its closing done and downstream arrive with the chains either way).

## unit: anchor

Establish what is being evaluated — a resolved anchor advances this step, and a seeded one (dispatched from an engagement, or passed as a known entry ID) auto-advances past this prompt.{{if .anchorHint}} The user's pointer: "{{.anchorHint}}".{{end}} Report `anchor`: the done signal, or the closed commitment whose closing done and downstream arrive with the chains either way.

- **Entry ID given**: report it directly.
- **Pointer or phrase given**: search the graph from several angles and settle the centre with the user before scoping. Recency does not resolve an anchor — ambiguity resolves in dialogue.

## unit: scope

The evaluated work and its surroundings, served in full:

{{.entryChains}}

**Widen before you scope.**{{if .widenReport}} Grounding was inherited from the dispatching move:

{{.widenReport}}

Extend it toward the evaluation's own questions — what has happened since the work landed, and what evaluation is already recorded against it — only where the inherited reads fall short, appending to `widenReport`.{{else}} Search the graph for what has happened since the work landed — signals it produced, regressions or surprises captured near it, neighbors that strain or confirm it — and for evaluation dones already recorded against it: which lens is covered, what is still unjudged. Record the searches and what they surfaced in `widenReport`; inspect promising results in full before they shape the plan.{{end}}

Then scope the run in `plan`: which lens(es) this evaluation covers, and what each concretely checks — **inner is verification**: is the work sound, judged against the project's own guidelines; **outer is validation**: is it the right thing, working in use, serving the people it's for. What the lenses mean for this work type — code, docs, a design, a story — is the project's to define: read its guidance (`AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`) for what *sound* and *right* mean here; the framework fixes only the postures. Covering one lens is a valid scope — say plainly what this run leaves uncovered rather than stretching a thin check across both. When the coverage choice isn't self-evident, settle it with the user before carrying on.

## unit: carryOut

Carry the scoped run out — evaluation is work done now, part automated and part human, not a summary of impressions:

- **Inner — verification.** Judge whether the work is sound under the project's own guidelines: what the done signal claims against what its commitment demanded — acceptance criteria, augmenting directives in the chain, deviations named or omitted — and the project's standards for this work type. Inspecting and running is host work: do it. What was inspected and run, and what came back, goes in `innerEvidence`; the judgment in `innerEvaluation`.
- **Outer — validation.** Judge whether it is the right thing: working in use, serving the people it's for. Run it end to end where that answers the question; where the judgment needs a human — the user, the people the work serves — gather their read: a human attestation counts as evidence. What was observed and attested goes in `outerEvidence`; the judgment in `outerEvaluation`.

Report the lens(es) the plan scoped — at least one judgment must land, and evidence belongs beside it: a judgment without evidence is an impression. Present the read to the user in their language: what held, what didn't, what it taught.

## unit: junction

The evaluation outlives this dialogue only as graph entries — recorded, it is work the graph can see; unrecorded, it evaporates with this session. Put it to the user: record this evaluation, and which findings with it?

Relay their answer verbatim. **record** carries `selectedFindings` — the findings they want kept, in their words, or none at all: a clean pass recorded is still coverage. Capture the evaluation itself first — enter capture with `kind` done and `anchor` the evaluated entry: `grounded-in` this move (20260703-200000-d-prc-evl), `builds-on` the evaluated work, work-shape `topics` naming each covered lens as `evaluation/<lens>` (specifics welcome: `evaluation/outer/user-feedback`), the body carrying plan, evidence, and judgment. Then each selected finding as its own capture, `surfaced-by` that evaluation done and anchored where the finding's subject lives. Start every capture with this instance as `parent` — the answered dispatch seeds your `widenReport` into each child, so you draft without re-widening. **conclude** records nothing — for the evaluation that shouldn't enter the graph at all; when the work simply passed clean, prefer record with no findings over conclude, and never invent findings to justify the run.
