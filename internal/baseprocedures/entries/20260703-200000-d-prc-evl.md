The evaluate procedure closes the learning loop on completed work: judge it through both lenses, present the read to the user, and turn the findings they want to keep into signals against the evaluated work. Anchor on the done signal (or the closed commitment — its closing done and downstream arrive with the chains either way).

## unit: anchor

Establish what is being evaluated — a resolved anchor advances this step, and a seeded one (dispatched from an engagement, or passed as a known entry ID) auto-advances past this prompt.{{if .anchorHint}} The user's pointer: "{{.anchorHint}}".{{end}} Report `anchor`: the done signal, or the closed commitment whose closing done and downstream arrive with the chains either way.

- **Entry ID given**: report it directly.
- **Pointer or phrase given**: search the graph from several angles and settle the centre with the user before assessing. Recency does not resolve an anchor — ambiguity resolves in dialogue.

## unit: assess

The evaluated work and its surroundings, served in full:

{{.entryChains}}

**Two lenses — always both.** A pass on one is not a pass; reason about them together in `evaluation`:

- **Inner** — is it sound on its own terms: code, architecture, concepts. Read what the done signal claims against what its commitment demanded — acceptance criteria, augmenting directives in the chain, deviations it names or omits.
- **Outer** — does it work in use: an end-to-end or smoke check, and does it serve the user and the product. Did it meet the target? What surprised? What gaps remain?

**Widen before you judge.**{{if .widenReport}} Grounding was inherited from the dispatching move:

{{.widenReport}}

Extend it toward the evaluation's own question — what has happened since the work landed — only where the inherited reads fall short, appending to `widenReport`.{{else}} Search the graph for what has happened since the work landed — signals it produced, regressions or surprises captured near it, neighbors that strain or confirm it. Record the searches and what they surfaced in `widenReport`; inspect promising results in full before they shape the evaluation.{{end}} When the evidence needs running code — a smoke test, a build — that is host work; do it and fold the outcome into the outer lens.

Present the evaluation to the user in their language: what held, what didn't, what it taught.

## unit: junction

Each finding worth keeping becomes a signal refed back at the evaluated work — that is how the evaluation outlives this dialogue. Put it to the user: which findings should be recorded, and is there follow-up work to raise?

Relay their answer verbatim: **record** carries `selectedFindings` — their pick in their words; capture each as its own move — start capture with `anchor` set to the evaluated entry (or the finding's own subject when it centers elsewhere) and this instance as `parent`, so the capture inherits this evaluation's grounding and you draft without re-widening. **conclude** ends the evaluation with nothing worth recording — a clean pass is a valid outcome; say so plainly rather than inventing findings.
