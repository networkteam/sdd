# Recording a deviation — the gap kind

A gap records a deviation: the actual state of something in the project set against the expected one, with a distance between them that demands further action. It is a signal — something noticed, not something committed to — and the open gaps are the project's working surface: together they show where attention is owed. Its reader arrives asking three things — what was actually observed and how, what was expected and where that expectation comes from, and what the deviation is — and a good gap is the shortest record that answers all three.

**Both sides, each with its source.** The actual side is evidence: state what was observed and how the observation came to be — what was done, where the information came from — concretely enough that the observation could be reproduced. The expected side names its source, and that source takes more shapes than a pointer: committed somewhere in the graph (point at it); a natural expectation nobody wrote down (state it plainly — demanding a recorded commitment behind "the service should be running" is ceremony, not rigor); or new information entering the project through this very gap (name where it came from — the customer's ask, the regulation, the call). Never prop a side up with an invented pointer.

**The world input is the substance.** The entry's irreplaceable content is what it carries in from the world with no other record — most often the observation, and the expected side's source whenever nothing else holds it; the deviation itself can be re-reasoned later from two well-stated sides. So the weight of the writing goes into framing the world input right and recording it truthfully — the deviation needs stating, not elaborating. And when a newly arrived expectation stands beyond this one deviation — it will govern more work, or the project adopts it as a commitment — it becomes its own entry through dialogue, with the gap pointing at it.

**Reasoning enters only under its own name.** Everything past the two sides is reasoning: suspected causes, candidate remedies, weighed trade-offs. What the dialogue surfaced may enter — marked as what it is, unsettled candidates rather than observations — and nothing is invented beyond the stated deviation to make the entry fuller. The cost of skipping the mark is asymmetric: an inherited observation can be re-checked against the same world, while an inherited unmarked conclusion becomes the floor a later reading reasons from, and if it is wrong, nothing in the entry reveals that. Marking is enough; competing candidates may stay side by side, and none needs deleting.

**Observed, never decided.** The moment the entry commits to one remedy — this will be done, by then, this way — a decision is being smuggled in a signal's clothes. If the situation cannot be described honestly without stating a choice and its justification, the choice is its own entry; the gap keeps the deviation. The same line runs through resolution: a closing record that had to make a choice on the way reveals a decision was skipped between the gap and its closure.

**Record the act, not the readings.** Stating the actual side does not license precision: cite the observation and its result, not the figures left over from checking — numbers in the body bind every later reader to reconcile them, and belong there only when a figure is itself the claim. Both rules hold at once and pull in different directions by design: the two sides govern what a gap records, the craft of stating them governs how — an appeal to one never waives the other.

**Frame the level that needs to change.** A gap framed around a surface symptom invites a closure that fixes the underlying piece while the symptom's own half quietly lives on. Frame the deviation at the level where, once resolved, nothing of the concern remains — and write it sharply enough that a claimed resolution can be checked against it, because the gap's own framing is the checklist its closer is measured by. A gap too broad for anything to demonstrably close it fails its purpose at the moment of writing.

**How a gap resolves.** It stays open until something closes it: normally a decision addressing it — the deeper the layer, the firmer that expectation, since a strategic deviation is never resolved by execution alone — or a completed act directly, when resolving it genuinely required no choice. A gap whose context has moved on is retired by a done saying why it no longer applies — moot is a resolution, silence is not. Sharpening the understanding of a still-open deviation is supersession by a refined gap, not closure. And a gap never closes anything itself: noticing is not resolving.

**Choosing gap at all.** The neighbor tests, each a single question. Remove the expected side: if nothing is left unmet, it is knowledge — a fact ("the delivered timber is grade C16") informs, a gap ("the spec calls for C24 and the delivered stock is C16") demands. Could new knowledge alone resolve it? Then the expected side itself is unknown, and it is a question. Does the entry connect what is already recorded without leaving anything owed? An insight.

**Honesty in the small fields.** Confidence grades the two sides: with both named and detailed, high is the natural grade; low or medium says one side is uncertain and needs further exploration — and the body names which. The layer names the depth of the deviation, not its urgency. Topic labels reuse the project's existing vocabulary; the first sentence stands alone, because overview surfaces lead with it.
