# Dispatching a piece of work — the activity kind

An activity records a commitment that a specific piece of work happens: a batch roasted, four bents raised, a review held, notes written. It is a decision — something committed to, not something noticed: the work lies ahead of the record, and that it happened is a separate record, the done that closes the loop. Its reader arrives asking three things — what work is being dispatched, when it counts as finished, and what it leaves behind — and a good activity is the shortest record that answers all three.

**Dispatch, not deliberation.** An activity makes no choice against alternatives — whatever choosing the work needed happened before it, and carrying the work out is mechanical: no design is left to make while doing it. That is exactly its license. When one piece of work is being sent on its way and no route is being picked, activity is the right record — reaching for a choice-shaped entry instead, to be safe, documents a deliberation that never took place, and a future reader will go looking for a why that was never there.

**The shape lives where the reader can reach it.** An activity works only because the work's form is already known — from the plan it executes under, from the entries it references, or from its own words when the work is self-evident. That is what lets the body stay lean: the references carry the context, and the activity need not retell why the work matters — it says what happens; the chain behind it says why.

**Judge the form of the work, not of what comes out.** Emergent outputs do not disqualify an activity: "run Thursday's cupping with the new roaster" has a well-known form even though which lots make the cut cannot be known in advance — the session is the work, the verdicts are its outputs. Measuring an activity by whether its outcomes can be specified in advance applies the wrong axis; what is asked of it is not a slate of outcome criteria but a completion condition.

**Say when it is finished.** The activity states its completion condition, and its closer is measured against exactly that — "finished when every sample lot has a verdict" hands the later reader the line the closing record must meet. And when the work will wait its turn while the world keeps moving, the condition says so rather than freezing a snapshot: scope that will only be knowable at execution is stated as resolving then, because a list written today would read as complete when it is not.

**Name what the work leaves behind.** The expected landing — the artifact or state the work ends in — turns "did it happen?" into a question the closer can answer concretely, and the closing record confirms that exact landing. When the purpose bounds the work, the activity says what it is for: "a trial batch roasted to learn whether the profile holds, not to commit to it" keeps a deliberately non-committing piece of work from being read as more than it is.

**Sequence by prerequisite, not by calendar.** When the work waits on something, the activity names what it waits on — "after the frame inspection passes" — not a date. A named prerequisite stays true when the schedule slips; a date quietly goes stale and tells the reader nothing about why the work has not started.

**Research is dispatched, not enshrined.** Looking into something — research, an evaluation, a trial — is an activity like any other, not a kind of its own. What the looking finds comes back as entries in their own right — facts, insights, deviations, open questions, each pointing where it belongs — and the done that closes the activity records only that the looking happened.

**How an activity resolves.** It closes like work: the done recording the act closes it, and that done covers the whole remit — each part of what the activity required either delivered or its deviation named with reasoning; silent omission is the failure. Work that advances it partway points at it and leaves it open — partial progress references, it does not close. When the work will not be done, a directive closes the activity carrying the why; a replacement activity may supersede it — a documented path, rarely the one taken. And the closing edge runs one way from the activity itself: it may close a signal it resolves — a deviation whose remedy was plain and only needed dispatching — but it never retires a decision; that instrument is the directive.

**Choosing activity at all.** The neighbor tests the type-system introduction does not run, each one question. Is a route being chosen, with a why to carry? Then it is a directive — the ruling "load-bearing joints are pegged, not screwed" chose among ways; "peg the joints on the east wall bents" sends work already ruled on its way. And an entry that turns out to carry a non-obvious choice inside the dispatch stays a directive: the boundary between the two is lossy in that direction, and a why lost crossing it cannot be recovered later. Has the work already happened? Then it is a done — "roast and cup the new lot" commits; "roasted and cupped the new lot" records. Is the commitment about the present period — who attends to what through it — rather than a piece of work? A focus: "through March, Jun carries the discovery line" is a focus; "write the March discovery notes" is an activity. Will the same work run again and again by the same steps? A procedure — "the weekly cupping review" is a procedure; "this week's cupping review" is an activity. And whether a record defines outcomes or dispatches work — the line between plan and activity — is a test the type-system introduction owns; pull it there.
