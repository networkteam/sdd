# Recording a synthesis — the insight kind

An insight records a synthesis: an understanding reached by connecting what is already observed and recorded — a pattern across entries, a cause read behind them, a bound on an earlier claim. It is a signal — something noticed, not something committed to: it changes the reading of the project, not the project's obligations. The open insights together are the project's current understanding of itself, which is what makes retiring a reading that has stopped holding a duty rather than tidying: left open, it makes the picture wrong. Its reader arrives asking three things — what is now understood, what that understanding rests on, and whether it changes anything ahead — and a good insight is the shortest record that answers all three.

**The connection is the substance, and the reasoning is the part only this entry holds.** What the entry adds is the relation among the records it reasons over — the referenced entries are its ground, each one pointed at, none of them restated: repeating what a referenced entry already says is duplication debt, the copies drift, and a later reader cannot tell which telling governs. But the material is only half of what makes the synthesis. The dialogue that reached it is not kept anywhere, so the entry is that reasoning's durable representation: the body carries why these records connect and what the connection means, because the ground can be re-read later and the thinking that put it together cannot. The references are load-bearing, not decoration: remove them and the entry should visibly lose its ground.

**One synthesis, one entry.** Where the drafting fuses two things, split and cross-reference. The commonest fusion welds a deviation to the reading behind it: "the delivered stock keeps failing grade" is a gap demanding action, and "the failing deliveries all come from the merchant with the fastest quoted turnaround" is the pattern read across them — two entries, each pointing at the other, so the deviation can be resolved while the understanding stands, and the understanding can be corrected without reopening the deviation. The same split holds when one dialogue yields a reading and a question it raises, or a reading and the claim it corrects: each stands as its own entry, connected, because each retires on its own terms.

**Understanding, never commitment.** An insight may walk far into what-to-do territory — carry a candidate direction, weigh alternatives the dialogue surfaced, sketch what the understanding suggests — and all of it may stand in the entry as exploration. What it may not do is commit: the moment the entry acquires an owner and a horizon — this will be done, by someone, by then — a decision is being smuggled in a signal's clothes, and the choice belongs in its own entry with its own dialogue. The insight keeps the understanding and the explored space; the ruling, if one follows, points back at it.

**Captured because it changes something.** A connection earns its entry by making a difference — a coming choice it would sharpen, work it would redirect, an assumption it unseats. A relation that alters nothing when read is trivia, and trivia dilutes the record. But the difference need not be claimed now: because nothing is owed on an insight, recording it and deliberately not acting — banking the understanding until the work it bears on arrives — is a legitimate end state, and saying so in the body keeps the restraint readable as deliberate rather than as neglect.

**A correction names what it touches.** When an insight bounds or corrects an earlier claim, it says exactly which part it touches and which still stands. "That earlier reading went too far" forces every later reader to renegotiate the whole claim; "it holds for the morning handovers, and the afternoon pattern was read into it" leaves the surviving part usable. Precision here is what makes correcting cheaper than replacing: the earlier entry keeps carrying what it got right.

**The one close it makes.** An insight may close a question by answering it — the type-system introduction names this move — and a question is the only entry it closes. The craft of the dissolution is carrying the answer's context: the insight's own narrative restates the question's framing, or says which unknown it settles, so the close is readable without opening the question first. And what earns the close is that carried context, not correctness — the record shows what answered the question and where the answer came from; whether it was right is judged by whoever reasons from it later.

**Nothing owed after the reading.** An open insight demands nothing. It does not sit among the deviations and unknowns where attention is owed — it stands beside them as the current understanding, open because it still holds, not because anyone must act. That is also why it is retired rather than fulfilled: it has nothing intrinsic to resolve. A sharpened or corrected understanding supersedes it. Being acted on is not a retirement — a commitment that takes the reading up, or completed work that ran off it, points at the insight and leaves it standing, because the understanding is now load-bearing rather than obsolete. What retires an insight is that the reading stopped holding, and any entry may carry that as long as it says why: the material moved, or the reasoning had a flaw in it. Restating what the insight said, or merely noting its age, is not a rationale.

**Choosing insight at all.** The neighbor tests the type-system introduction does not run, each a single question. Strip the connection: does a standalone observation survive intact? Then it is a fact — "the January lot cups 86" reports one thing, checkable by looking; "every lot we scored above 85 this year came through a direct trading relationship" adds a relation no single observation carries. Does the entry leave something owed? Then it is a gap — an insight connects what is recorded and leaves the attention surface untouched. Is it naming an unknown rather than settling one? Then it is a question — the insight is the knowledge a question waits for. Did the world change, or only the reading of it? The act is a done, the reading an insight — one working session commonly produces both, as two entries, the reading pointing back at the act that surfaced it. Against the decision kinds, the deciding test — noticed, not committed — is the type split itself; the type-system introduction runs it.

**Honesty in the small fields.** Confidence grades the evidence behind the synthesis, not the conviction it is held with — a lower grade says the connection needs more instances or a check, and the body names what would firm it. The layer names the depth of the synthesis, not its importance: a reading about direction is strategic even when it fits in one line. Topic labels reuse the project's existing vocabulary, and the first sentence stands alone, because overview surfaces lead with it.
