# Dispatching a piece of work — the activity kind

An activity records a commitment that a specific piece of work happens: a batch roasted, four bents raised, a review held. It is a decision — something committed to, not something noticed: the work lies ahead of the record, and that it happened is a separate record, the done that closes it. Its reader arrives asking three things — what work is being sent, when it counts as finished, and what it leaves behind — and a good activity is the shortest record that answers all three.

**The choosing happened before the entry.** A dispatch presupposes a dialogue already held: the route was weighed and settled somewhere behind this record — in the plan it runs under, the decision it carries out, the exchange that shaped it — and what remains is to send the work on its way, with no design left to make while doing it. That is the kind's structural limit as much as its license. An activity has no place to keep a why — so the moment a non-obvious choice turns up inside the dispatch, the entry stays a directive: the boundary is lossy in that direction, and a why lost crossing it cannot be recovered later. But where genuinely no choice is left, activity is the right record, not a cautious fallback short of one: a choice-shaped entry written to be safe documents a deliberation that never took place, and a future reader will go looking for reasoning that was never there.

**The shape lives where the reader can reach it.** An activity works only because the work's form is already known — from the plan it runs under, from the entries it references, or from its own words when the work is self-evident. That is what lets the body stay lean: the references carry the context, so the activity says what happens and lets the chain behind it say why — and it commits nothing those references do not support.

**Judge the form of the work, not of what comes out.** Emergent outputs do not disqualify an activity: in a coffee roastery, "run Thursday's cupping with the new roaster" has a well-known form even though which lots make the cut cannot be known in advance — the session is the work, the verdicts are its outputs. Measuring an activity by whether its outcomes can be specified up front applies the wrong axis: what it owes is not a slate of outcome criteria but a completion condition.

**Say when it is finished.** The whole of an activity's validation is one question — was it done? — and the stated completion condition is what makes that question answerable: in a coffee roastery, "finished when every sample lot has a verdict" hands the closer the exact line its record must meet. When the work will wait its turn while the world keeps moving, the condition says so rather than freezing a snapshot: scope that will only be knowable at execution is stated as resolving then, because a list written today would read as complete when it is not.

**Name what the work leaves behind.** The expected landing — the artifact or state the work ends in — turns "did it happen?" into something the closing record can confirm concretely. And when the purpose bounds the work, say what it is for: in a coffee roastery, "a trial batch roasted to learn whether the profile holds, not to commit to it" keeps a deliberately non-committing piece of work from being read as more than it is.

**Sequence by prerequisite, not by calendar.** When the work waits on something, name what it waits on — in timber framing, "after the frame inspection passes" — not a date. A named prerequisite stays true when the schedule slips; a date goes stale and says nothing about why the work has not started.

**Research is dispatched, not enshrined.** Looking into something — research, an evaluation, a trial — is an activity like any other, not a kind of its own. What the looking finds comes back as entries in their own right — facts, insights, gaps, open questions, each pointing where it belongs — and the done closing the activity records only that the looking happened.

**How an activity resolves.** It closes like work: the done recording the act closes it, and that done covers the whole remit — each part of what the activity required either delivered or its deviation named with reasoning; silent omission is the failure. Work that advances it partway points at it without closing it. An activity whose work will not be done is retired by whatever entry carries the reasoning why it stopped holding — the stated rationale is what qualifies the retirement, not the kind carrying it — and a replacement activity may supersede it, a documented path rarely taken. In the other direction, an activity may close a signal its dispatch plainly resolves — a deviation whose remedy needed only sending. But closing an entry means saying why it stopped holding, and an activity carries no reasoning of its own — so a retirement that needs arguing belongs to an entry built to carry the why.
