# Querying for input — the question kind

A question records a demand for input: something the project needs to know and does not, put into the record so later work — a session, a meeting, a dialogue — can find it, reason toward answers, and explore the space it opens. It is a signal — something noticed, not something committed to: the project is not ready to decide, and says so by asking, because an unasked question gets answered anyway — silently, by whatever the work happens to do. Open questions sit beside the open gaps on the project's working surface, carrying the demand into every future session until something meets it — that the unknown becomes explicit along the way is a consequence of asking, not the purpose. Its reader arrives wanting three things — what input is being asked for, why it is needed, and what could settle it — and a good question is the shortest record that answers all three.

**As specific as the demand that raised it.** Input is asked for because concrete work needs it, and the question's scope follows the scope of that demand. A roastery shaping a marketing campaign gains nothing from "what is the life-goal of our customers?" — interesting, and attached to nothing; "what joy can our products bring into our customers' lives?" asks for exactly the input the campaign needs. And the framing carries more than focus: the question's own words are the handle a later answer must take up — whoever settles it names it by what it asks, and a claimed resolution is checked against that framing — so an unknown posed too broadly for any answer to demonstrably settle it fails its purpose at the moment of writing.

**Name the demand and where it arose.** Questions arrive out of concrete work — a review that surfaced an ambiguity, a piece of work accepted with a doubt attached, a customer's ask nobody could answer. Say which work needs the input and point at the entry that raised the question: candidate answers can be re-reasoned later, but where the demand came from cannot be reconstructed once forgotten. The layer names the depth of the unknown itself, not of the occasion — a doubt surfacing in day-to-day handovers may open a conceptual question.

**A question exists to be brought into dialogue.** The answer is rarely lying somewhere waiting to be looked up — it is reached in dialogue, when a later session picks the question up and works it out between the people at the table. So a question may seed that dialogue: candidate answers, directions worth weighing, a proposed move — "think about a parent survey" — and, when the path to an answer is already visible, the move that would settle it: a trial, a measurement, an unknown answerable only through a real attempt. All of it enters marked as what it is, unsettled. What the question may not do is pre-empt the dialogue: the moment it commits to a route — an imperative with an owner and a horizon — it is a decision in a signal's clothes. Committing to the survey, after that dialogue, belongs in a decision of its own; the question's job is to make the dialogue happen.

**One demand, with its strands.** The unit is the unknown, not the theme: two questions that different resolutions would answer are two entries. But one demand can have several angles that stand or fall with the same resolution, and those travel together, each stated. The line need not be perfect: a question is temporary, and whatever answers it can split what turned out to be two — the guard is against multiplying entries into ceremony, not against bundling. When the question is one face of a larger unknown the graph already holds, it says so and points at its siblings, so the answering work sees the whole shape.

**How a question resolves.** It stays open until something meets the demand: most often a decision — the answer is a choice, recorded with its why — and sometimes knowledge alone, when a fact or insight dissolves it by answering it and no choice was required. Learning that the timber yard stocks only one grade dissolves "which grades can we source?" without anyone deciding anything. A decision may also close a question that will not be pursued, carrying the why. Sharpening a still-open unknown is supersession by a refined question, not closure. In every path the record owes traceability, not certification — the answering entry can be checked against the question's words; nothing adjudicates whether the answer is right. And a question never closes anything itself: asking is not answering.

**Choosing question at all.** The neighbor tests, each a single question. Does the entry assert something the project now knows, or ask for something it does not? "A courier ships a 500-gram package for under six euros" informs — a fact; "what should shipping cost the customer?" asks — a question. Is there an expectation already held that the world fails to meet? Then it is a gap — a question lacks the expected side itself, which is why knowledge alone may answer a question but never close a gap. Does the entry connect what is already recorded, leaving nothing owed? An insight — "children settle faster when handover happens at the door" owes nothing; "does handover work better at the door or in the room?" owes an answer.
