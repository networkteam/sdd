# Recording a synthesis — the insight kind

An insight records a synthesis: an understanding reached by connecting what is already recorded — a pattern across entries, a cause read behind them, a bound on an earlier claim. It is a signal — something noticed, not something committed to: it changes the reading of the project, not its obligations. The open insights together are the project's current understanding of itself, so a reading that no longer holds and stays open makes the picture wrong. Its reader arrives asking three things — what is now understood, what that understanding rests on, and whether it changes anything ahead — and a good insight is the shortest record that answers all three.

**The connection is the substance, and the reasoning is the part only this entry holds.** What the entry adds is the relation among the records it reasons over: they are its ground, each one pointed at, none of them restated — repeating what a referenced entry says is duplication debt, the copies drift, and a later reader cannot tell which telling governs. The references are load-bearing, not decoration: remove them and the entry visibly loses its ground. But the material is only half of the synthesis. The dialogue that reached it is kept nowhere else, so the entry is that reasoning's durable representation, and the standard it is held to is re-derivability: a later session should be able to reach the entry's claims again from its writing, its attachments and its references alone.

**One synthesis, one entry.** Where the drafting fuses two things, split and cross-reference. The commonest fusion welds a deviation to the reading behind it: "the delivered stock keeps failing grade" is a gap demanding action, and "the failing deliveries all come from the merchant with the fastest quoted turnaround" is the pattern read across them — two entries pointing at each other, so the deviation can be resolved while the understanding stands, and corrected without reopening the deviation. The same split holds for a reading and the question it raises: each retires on its own terms.

**Understanding, never commitment.** An insight may walk far into what-to-do territory — carrying a candidate direction, weighing alternatives the dialogue surfaced — and all of it may stand in the entry as exploration. What it may not do is commit: the moment the entry acquires an owner and a horizon — this will be done, by someone, by then — a decision is being smuggled in a signal's clothes, and the choice belongs in its own entry with its own dialogue. The insight keeps the understanding and the explored space.

**Captured because it changes something.** A connection earns its entry by making a difference — a coming choice it would sharpen, work it would redirect, an assumption it unseats. A relation that alters nothing when read is trivia, and trivia dilutes the record. But the difference need not be claimed now: because nothing is owed on an insight, recording it and deliberately not acting — holding the understanding until the work it bears on arrives — is a legitimate end state, and saying so keeps the restraint readable as deliberate rather than as neglect.

**Narrowing an earlier reading.** Understanding sharpens by narrowing as often as by replacing: new material read against a recorded reading yields not a contradiction but a more specific one — the claim holds, inside bounds nobody had found yet. "The sourdough proofs in four hours" does not become false when winter shows six; it becomes a claim about the summer kitchen. What the narrowing entry owes is that boundary, stated precisely: "that reading went too far" makes every later reader renegotiate the whole claim, while "it holds in the summer kitchen" leaves the surviving part usable — and the earlier entry stays open, still true where it was observed.

**The one close it makes.** An insight may close a question by answering it, and a question is the only entry it closes. The craft is carrying the answer's context: the narrative restates the question's framing, or says which unknown it settles, so the close is readable without opening the question first. What earns the close is that carried context, not correctness — the record shows what answered the question and where the answer came from; whether it was right is judged by whoever reasons from it later.

**Nothing owed after the reading.** An open insight demands nothing: it does not sit among the deviations and unknowns where attention is owed, but beside them — open because it still holds, not because anyone must act. That is also why it is retired rather than fulfilled: it has nothing intrinsic to resolve. A sharpened or corrected understanding supersedes it, pointing at the material that changed the picture and saying why the reasoning moved; closing is for the reading that goes entirely. Being acted on is not a retirement — a commitment that takes the reading up, or completed work that ran off it, points at the insight and leaves it standing, because the understanding is now load-bearing rather than obsolete. What retires an insight is that the reading stopped holding, and any entry may carry that as long as it says why: the material moved, or the reasoning had a flaw in it. Restating what the insight said, or merely noting its age, is not a rationale.

**Honesty in the small fields.** Confidence grades the evidence behind the synthesis, not the conviction it is held with — a lower grade says the connection needs more instances or a check, and the body names what would firm it. The layer names where the reasoning landed, not where its inputs sit: a reading drawn from several operational deviations and one survey result is strategic if that is where the thinking arrived.
