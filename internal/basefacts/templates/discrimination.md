# Telling the kinds apart — choosing a kind

When two kinds compete for one entry, one test settles it; the work is finding that test without walking all fourteen. The type split comes first and the type-system introduction owns it, along with several deciding tests named below where they apply. Everything else sorts by the situation you are in: something was noticed, something is being committed, a person is entering the record, or structure is being laid over entries already written. Find the situation — its first question drops most kinds before any pair needs testing.

## Something was noticed

**Does the entry leave something owed?** A gap and a question demand: they sit on the project's working surface until something meets them. A done, a fact and an insight owe nothing. If it demands, ask: **is an expectation already held that the world fails to meet?** Then both sides exist and it is a gap; when the expected side is itself what the project lacks, it is a question. In timber framing, "the delivered timber is grade C16" informs — a fact; "the spec calls for C24 and the delivered stock is C16" demands — a gap; "which grades can we source at all?" asks — a question. The split governs resolution too: knowledge alone may answer a question, while a gap's demand is only met by a decision or a completed act; knowledge closes a gap only by showing it no longer applies.

**If nothing is owed: did the world change, or only what is known of it?** An act performed is a done; a claim standing on the world regardless of any act is knowledge. In a coffee roastery, "re-labelled the bags and reprinted the batch" records an act; "the new label stock absorbs ink unevenly below eight degrees" is true whether or not anyone acts. Between the two knowledge kinds: **checked against the world, or reasoned out of the record?** Strip the connection and see whether a standalone observation survives. In a coffee roastery, "the January lot cups 86" survives — a fact, checkable by looking; "every lot we scored above 85 this year came through a direct trading relationship" is the relation itself, held only against the project's own record — an insight.

## Something is being committed

Work already performed is a done — in a coffee roastery, "roasted and cupped the new lot" records; "roast and cup the new lot" commits. Past that, one question fans out every work kind: **how does the commitment end?**

- **It never ends — it pulls, and no single piece of work conforms to it or violates it: an aspiration.** The deciding test against a directive — force, not completion — lives in the introduction, and a guard travels with it: the absence of a completion criterion cannot pick between them, because both kinds may lack one by design. In a village bakery, "every loaf we sell was baked that morning from grain whose farm we can name" pulls; "nothing leaves the shop without a second person's sign-off" pushes.
- **It keeps applying as a rule work must conform to, a violation observable: a guiding directive** — including every standing constraint, which the introduction owns. A rule outlives every period: in timber framing, "load-bearing joints are pegged, not screwed" keeps holding after November; the barn-frame commission does not.
- **It holds for the present period, naming who attends to what, and is replaced when priorities shift: a focus.** In a village bakery, "this quarter the bakery attends to the wholesale accounts, with Rosa on ordering" ends with the quarter. Replacement when priorities change is the lifecycle no dispatched piece of work can have.
- **It runs again and again by the same steps: a procedure** — the one commitment that is not only read but run. In a coffee roastery, "the weekly cupping review" recurs; "this week's cupping review" happens once, an activity. A guiding rule also keeps applying, but it binds work; it does not run.
- **It is delivered once and closed by the act that delivers it: a plan, an activity, or a pending directive.** Which of the three, two tests and a pointer settle.

**Route or end-state — directive against plan.** Can what counts as finished be enumerated and checked item by item, or does the commitment state a route the work must conform to? In a coffee roastery, "no batch ships uncupped" directs; "the launch is finished when the blend is on the shelf and the first orders have gone out clean" plans.

**Choice or dispatch — directive against activity.** Is a route being chosen, with a why to carry, or is work already ruled being sent on its way? In timber framing, "load-bearing joints are pegged, not screwed" chose among ways; "peg the joints on the east wall bents" dispatches. Two guards travel with this test. The boundary is lossy in one direction: an entry carrying a non-obvious choice inside its dispatch stays a directive, because a why lost crossing that boundary cannot be recovered — so ambiguous cases stay directives. And read the shape of the work, not of its outputs: in a child-care group, "run the handover redesign with the parent group Thursday" is an activity even though what emerges cannot be known in advance — the session is the work, its findings are outputs.

**Plan against activity** is the introduction's, by WHAT versus THAT.

## A person is entering the record

The deciding test — outside versus here — lives in the introduction. Past it:

**Is the description a position held in another frame?** In a village bakery, "chairs the millers' cooperative" is identity here even though it is a role there: what someone is elsewhere grounds who they are, and only what they do within this project is a role.

**Is the name just someone who took part once?** In a child-care group, a visiting inspector quoted in one meeting's minutes is listed among that entry's participants; an actor entry is for a name the graph will attribute work to from now on.

**Is it about the world, or about who this is?** A participant's stable identity and the background they bring belong in that actor's entry, not in a fact — in a coffee roastery, "the new apprentice trained four years in Lyon" reads like discovered knowledge and is actor material all the same.

**Does the sentence stay true with a different name in it?** Then it is a guiding directive, not a role: in timber framing, "material substitutions are confirmed before ordering" holds whoever orders; "material substitutions come to Jonas before order" does not.

**Is that the usual part, or just this stretch's work?** A single period's pattern is not yet a role: in a child-care group, "Marta covers the toddler group this month" belongs in a focus; "Marta holds the settling-in period for new children" is her standing part.

## Structure is being laid over entries

The introduction's cut — structure, not narrative — has an operative form, and it is the strongest single test here: **strip the references away and ask whether anything left is still worth reading.** If yes, it is not an annotation. In timber framing, "the shop knows exactly as much as before; it just could not find the three entries in one pull" marks an annotation; "deliveries keep arriving below spec" claims something new and owes something — a gap. One neighbour needs its own line: **does the entry commit attention — who attends to what, for this period?** In a child-care group, "this month the team watches settling-in at handover" is a focus; a label laid over last term's handover entries is an annotation.
