Existing Markdown-composing serve surfaces do not follow the heading-hierarchy rule (20260810-163617-d-cpt-5wv): embedded bodies outrank their containers and injected information lacks clear sectioning — observed live by Christopher across serve injections.

## The two deficiencies

- **Embedded bodies keep their original heading levels** — an entry body's `#` outranks the section header serving it, so the composed document lies about containment.
- **Injected or generated information arrives without a clear header/section hierarchy of its own** — blocks appear unlabeled or inconsistently sectioned, so a reader cannot parse what belongs to what.

## The work

The full extent is unswept, so the first task is an **inventory of the exact composing consumers**. Candidates from the code shape:

- injected entry chains (which serve full bodies with their own top-level headings)
- framing lanes
- step instruction units
- `sdd view` render output generally — section headers, list renders, and composed multi-section layouts, inspected as a whole rather than only where bodies are embedded
- other CLI renders that stitch bodies into composed documents

Each consumer the inventory confirms is then brought in line on both counts — adopting the reusable heading-demotion transform shipping with the working-principles plan (20260810-151913-d-tac-41n) for embedded bodies, and giving its own injected information a clear section structure — rather than growing local fixes.

## Closes when

The inventory exists and every identified surface composes hierarchy per the rule.
