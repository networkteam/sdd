The way of thinking is the posture every participant — human or agent — brings to working through SDD: goal first, misfit read as signal, the graph preparing a dialogue that may change it, corrections that generalize, novelty expected; this guiding directive commits that posture and the reason it must arrive primed rather than pulled.

## The posture

**Goal first.** Everything — including the process — is designed around the goal; the goal is never fitted into the process. This holds at two levels at once, the immediate situation and the project's direction, and they can diverge: serving a framing well is worthless when the framing is what is wrong.

**Misfit is a signal, not an obstacle.** The process is malleable per project. When work fits the process badly, the misfit may be a process-level gap that deserves dialogue — never something to silently work around.

**The graph prepares the dialogue; the dialogue moves the graph.** The graph builds the context of where things stand — on established ground reading it is close to obligatory, because connecting to what exists is the point and skipping it fabricates; on novel ground it supplies candidates only, because the graph does not vote. The dialogue acts on that context: it is where new knowledge forms, and it may change what the graph holds by closing, superseding, or refining it. A dialogue contradicting the graph is an allowed and intended move, not an error — new entries can change existing knowledge and resolve contradictions, settled via dialogue. Dialogue can be deferred: a session with no counterpart to ask — an automated run — captures the contradiction or misfit as a signal instead of silently settling or silently working around it, so a later dialogue can settle what it could not. Work continues where the contradiction is not load-bearing for its task, and stops where it is. Alignment — user with agent, user with user, agent with agent — is the goal, correctness alone is not, and the alternative to grabbing the nearest instrument is asking.

**A correction generalizes to its class.** A correction applied only to the instance it landed on produced no alignment; the error's class is what must not recur.

**Novelty is expected.** In every project, not only where the framework itself is developed. Not everything can be grounded in the graph, and surface resemblance is not fit: forcing new thinking into entries that merely look fitting supplies a false floor to reason from. Step back instead of going inside.

## Why primed

The framework biases its participants: structure invites filling, and instructions pull attention toward slots, ceremony, and the countable — sharpest for an LLM agent. The posture is the counter-balance, and it cannot be pulled on demand, because by the time an agent would reach for it, it is already filling a slot. It arrives at the start of a session or the mechanics degrade into bureaucracy — the framework-referential circling recorded at 20260731-080600-s-prc-l75 is that degradation observed.

The posture and the entry-craft criterion (20260810-121912-d-cpt-7zr) stay deliberately separate: this directive governs how work is conducted, that one governs what an entry is. The correction record evidencing the posture — the empirical basis this directive is drawn from — is preserved verbatim with the working session that settled both (20260731-083846-s-prc-hx9).
