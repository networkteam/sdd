A gap earns its keep by saying two things in plain words: what is expected, grounded in the graph wherever the graph holds material for it, and what is actually observable, together with how that observation came to be. The kind is already defined as the delta between target and actual state (20260412-114647-d-cpt-omm); what a later session needs is that delta written so both sides can be checked — the target traceable to whatever committed it, the actual traceable to the act that produced it. This is the most valuable thing a gap carries forward, and the part no later reader can reconstruct if it is missing.

Everything past those two sides is reasoning, and reasoning belongs in a gap only when explicitly marked as an unsettled candidate. The cost is asymmetric. An observation a later session inherits is checkable against the same world; a conclusion inherited unmarked becomes the floor it reasons from, and if that conclusion was wrong the work built on it is wrong in a way nothing in the entry reveals. Marking is enough and deletion is not required, which is how a specimen here kept two competing repairs while stating both as unobserved and unsettled (20260811-233331-s-tac-bjn).

Stating the observable side does not license precision. It is the act and its result that belong in the body, not the figures: the observation is that completion-side counts did not move while embedding-side counts did, never the counts themselves, because a number carried into prose invites a later reader to treat a single reading as knowledge. This is the general evidence rule of the entry-craft criterion applying unchanged (20260810-121912-d-cpt-7zr) — and the checker that reads each draft cold applied it correctly, flagging exactly those figures as dilution, while the agent drafting dismissed the finding by appealing to the observation rule. Both hold at once: one governs what a gap records, the other how it states it, and the specimen was corrected to the general form (20260811-234304-s-tac-ual).

This is the gap kind's counterpart to the done-kind discriminator that a kind's purpose decides what counts as its own content (20260811-174502-s-cpt-ayb), banked under the same sweep discipline that requires every discriminator to carry the specimens it was read from (20260811-141038-d-tac-rvu).
