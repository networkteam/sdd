The CLI write surface is transitional: entry creation has outgrown what CLI interaction carries, and once the skill retirement (20260820-162456-d-tac-o2v) ends skill-driven use with v0.18.0, engine capture is the primary write path and `sdd new` remains a manual escape.

Entry creation now spans typed fields, resolved refs, playback confirmation, staged attachments, and procedure specs; a flag-driven CLI cannot carry that dialogue, which is why skill-driven capture routed agents through `sdd new` and why the engine replaced it. This reading orders investment on the surface: routing `sdd new` through the composition root (20260830-114446-d-cpt-xc3) is worth its one wiring change, since both write surfaces then produce identical pre-flight findings, while deeper CLI capture work would improve a path that loses its main consumer with v0.18.0. That wiring change is part of the bounded-serves architecture work (20260830-112915-d-tac-qwc).

What becomes of `sdd new` after the transition, a maintained manual escape or a removal, is deliberately left open: the retirement's outcome will show whether anything but the engine still writes entries in practice.
