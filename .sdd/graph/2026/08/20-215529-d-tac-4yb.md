Pre-flight routes a signal's close checks by the closed target's kind: a fact or insight whose targets are all questions keeps the dissolution rubric, and every other signal closure, a gap's included, runs a new closing-signal rubric that checks the stated why. This sharpens the close-validation rework (20260820-151100-d-cpt-304) on its pre-flight side. The rework made these closures mechanically legal and assigned pre-flight the judgment, but left the dispatch untouched: every fact or insight carrying a closes edge is sent to the dissolution rubric, which assumes the target is a question and can emit an off-target finding for the very cases the rework allows, while a gap carrying a closes edge falls through to the plain signal-capture rubric and gets no closure check beyond the generic unusual-close pattern flag. The inner review of the shipped rework surfaced both misroutes (20260820-162242-s-tac-677).

The new rubric checks exactly what the rework made the test of a valid close. Medium when the narrative only states the new content and never says why the target stopped holding; low when the why is implicit; no finding when it is explicit. Its worked examples come from the unusual-close partial so the two rubrics stay consistent, and it states only the check, never which kinds may close what: that knowledge lives in the validator's rule constant and the kind facts, and repeating it in a prompt template would reopen the drift this line of work closed.

Fulfilled by the routing branch in the dispatch, the new rubric template, and tests pinning both routes. Calibration repeats what the earlier widening of close validation did when directives were first allowed to close any decision: no dedicated eval run, the first live captures are the evidence, and an eval is added only if they misfire.
