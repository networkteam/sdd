A gap's irreplaceable content is the world input it carries in with no other record — most often the observation of the actual state, and the expected side's grounding whenever nothing else in the graph holds it — while the deviation itself can be re-reasoned later from two well-stated sides.

This sharpens the gap discriminator (20260811-235500-s-cpt-qyo), which held the delta itself to be the part no later reader can reconstruct. Reviewing the gap authoring fact showed the boundary sits elsewhere: an expectation may legitimately enter the project through the gap — a customer's ask, a regulation's demand — and then its grounding has no other record either; conversely, when a side already lives in the graph, that side is recoverable and the deviation statement is derivable — the gap's real contribution is then whatever the graph did not yet hold, and the visibility. The unreconstructible core is exactly what the entry brings in from the world, on whichever side it arrives.

The resolution behind it is the type split doing its work: noticing enters through signals, committing follows dialogue. A stakeholder's expectation is something noticed — structurally at home inside the gap with its provenance (who said it, when, where it came from) — and demanding a prior committed entry for it would run the loop backwards; the commitment adopting that expectation is what comes out of the dialogue the gap triggers, not what must precede it. The line for a separate entry is standing on its own: an expectation that will govern more than the one deviation, or that the project adopts as a commitment, becomes its own entry through dialogue, with the gap pointing at it. A natural expectation nobody wrote down is stated plainly — demanding a recorded commitment behind it is ceremony, not rigor.

The drafting consequence: the weight of gap writing goes into framing the world input right and recording it truthfully — the deviation needs stating, not elaborating. The discriminator's other claims stand unchanged: both sides in plain words, reasoning only as marked candidates, the act rather than the figures.
