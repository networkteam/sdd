Engine abilities could become an extension point: a plugin mechanism through which a deployment registers new predicates, queries, and commands, so procedures gain capabilities without a framework release.

**What it would change.** The registry is closed Go today — the ability-model ceiling already on record names that as the limit on graph-local extensibility. An extension mechanism would move the boundary: what a procedure can check, inject, and do becomes deployment-defined rather than release-defined.

**The concrete pull.** The engine itself could reach external knowledge and systems — connector-backed queries over outside APIs or knowledge sources — so a step injects what today only arrives if the running agent chooses to call an external tool on its own initiative. Dependable, declared access replaces agent initiative.

**Status.** An idea, deliberately uncommitted: any such mechanism belongs to the ability-model redesign's design space, and a hosted deployment (its own repository, out of scope here) would be its first obvious beneficiary. It neither gates nor is gated by the registry-fact direction.
