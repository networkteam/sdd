Kind discrimination is factored out of the per-kind authoring facts into one fact of its own, referenced from the type-system introduction, because holding it per kind writes the same test twice from opposite sides and the duplication grows with every kind added.

The overlap is structural, not accidental. The directive fact tells the reader when the entry is really a plan; the plan fact tells the reader when it is really a directive. Across fourteen kinds the "choosing X at all" paragraphs come to roughly 1,800 words of discrimination held as one view per kind, with those reciprocal pairs restated inside it. A claim that lives in more than one place drifts out of step with itself, and the repair is to state it in one home and point at it from everywhere else.

Two things settle the direction rather than merely favouring it. First, the plan being refined here already assigns discrimination to the introduction — its acceptance criterion has that fact state how the types and kinds play together and how to discriminate the correct one (20260812-134549-d-tac-9be). The per-kind paragraphs grew because the introduction could not hold the whole set, not because the kind facts were ever the right home for it. Second, the arrangement already ships: the procedure kind has a companion reference fact beside it, pulled when a drafter needs depth the kind fact does not carry. A discrimination fact is that same arrangement applied to a concern that spans kinds instead of sitting inside one.

The cost is real and accepted. By the time a per-kind fact is served the kind has usually been chosen, and the choosing paragraph sitting right there is what can make a drafter reconsider. Moving it one hop away makes that reconsideration depend on the drafter noticing they should pull it. What answers this is that the reconsideration path stays served rather than remembered. The introduction is read before the kind is chosen, so it can point at the discrimination fact plainly. And the check that reads every draft before it is played back — which is told which kind was drafted — can be given the discrimination fact too, so a draft in the wrong kind still meets the tests even when the drafter never went looking for them.

Recorded as pending: the discrimination fact itself, the removal of the choosing paragraphs from the facts already shipped, and the introduction's pointer at it are all still owed. Kinds still unwritten when this lands carry no choosing paragraph from the start.
