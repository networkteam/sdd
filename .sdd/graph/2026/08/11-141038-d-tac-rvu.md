The capture writing guide rolls out by wiring a first general version into the capture procedure immediately — running on real captures between assemble and playback — rather than after a manual dogfooding period, with the legacy pre-flight narrowed to graph-facing checks and per-kind guidance developed through specimen sweeps over existing entries.

## Wire now, calibrate live

Manual clean-room runs before wiring were the hedged alternative and are rejected: they depend on agent discipline at every capture and so are skip-prone, and they produce less of exactly what calibration needs. The capture-guide architecture commits calibration against real capture traffic (20260810-141555-d-cpt-20r), and wiring is what produces that traffic. The plumbing cost that motivated the hedge is thin here — the LLM runner already exists for pre-flight, and this repository iterates its own binary daily, so guide iterations are cheap commits, not releases. The first version renders the entry-craft criterion (20260810-121912-d-cpt-7zr) with general style guidance; findings arrive as drafting input before playback, and per-kind sections stay absent until specimens earn them.

## The legacy pre-flight narrows

While the graph-fit guide does not yet exist, the write-path pre-flight keeps only its graph-facing checks; its writing-quality checks are dropped where the writing guide covers the same ground, so LLM judgment never runs twice over one draft. The write gate goes fully mechanical only when both guides are proven, as the architecture already commits.

## No served experiment note

A served note marking the guide experimental and inviting feedback was considered and dropped on the audience test (20260730-101601-d-cpt-r99): an adopting project cannot capture entries for this repository, so the invitation would ask its reader for an action they cannot take. The served step is identical everywhere; the harvest expectation is project-local and lives in this decision.

## Per-kind guidance by specimen sweeps

Each sweep starts by finding fitting specimens: two to three existing entries per kind, selected for contrast — an exemplary case, a suspected spoiled one, an ordinary middle case — never at random, and extended past the initial sample only while specimens keep teaching; when a further specimen surfaces nothing new, the kind is settled. Ground truth comes first: for each specimen, dialogue settles the optimal shape — the corrections it actually needs — and the guide is then checked backwards against that judgment: would its notes have surfaced these corrections? Missed corrections expose what the kind's guidance must say; extra findings expose miscalibration; findings that confirm genuine spoilage validate the guide and incidentally mark grooming candidates. Sweeps also cover kinds that live capture traffic reaches rarely. Every discriminator or misfire learned this way is banked as an insight with the specimen attached, the pattern the aspiration-versus-directive specimen demonstrates (20260810-144251-s-cpt-jz0), so guide iterations rest on a corpus rather than intuition.
