Commits 719420c9 and 30adcce2 ship the close-validation rework that 20260820-151100-d-cpt-304 owed, closing it; follow-up commits be43b8a6, 8a7b9dd9, f234d15f, 58159080, 69f9e4a4, and fcfd4ce5 tightened the wording of the changed sentences and their surroundings. validateCloses now checks only the directive's three refusals. The clause that only done signals may close is gone, and the exception that let a fact or insight close a question is no longer needed, since the general rule covers it. The rule constant printed by the kind facts states just the refusal for the three no-findings kinds, so every fact that renders it updated itself. The two hand-written sentences the directive named are rewritten: the gap fact now says any entry that states why a gap no longer applies may close it, and that a gap may itself close an entry its reported deviation shows no longer holds. The fact fact's retirement sentence excludes the three no-findings kinds. The framework-concepts reference, the third change the directive named, now states the refusals and routes the close paths in its retirement table by stated rationale instead of kind. Pre-flight needed no code change; its unusual-close check already runs on every capture and flags weak rationale instead of blocking.

One fix the directive did not list came up mid-run, and Christopher chose to take it in the same run: the insight fact claimed a question is the only entry an insight closes, stricter than the new validator, so it now allows an insight to close an entry its reading overturns. Christopher corrected the wording several times along the way: the rule constant states only the mechanical refusal and no longer mentions pre-flight, and the changed sentences lost their added em dashes, mid-sentence colons, and metaphors in an audit against his unslop checklist. Validator tests cover the new refused and allowed cases, and both installed skill renders were refreshed.
