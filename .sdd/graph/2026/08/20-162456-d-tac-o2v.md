The `/sdd` skill family retires in two releases: v0.17.0 recommends the engine while the skills stay installed and marked deprecated, and v0.18.0 removes them and renames `/sdd-engine` to `/sdd`. This settles the deprecation mechanics the engine-primacy directive (20260715-175731-d-cpt-p3k) demanded as follow-up, adopting the two-release split from the release-readiness triage (20260815-000432-s-tac-xlw).

In v0.17.0 the skills stay installed and their prose stays frozen, with one named narrow exception: the `/sdd` skill opens with a short deprecation note the agent relays to the user, saying to work in `/sdd-engine` because `/sdd` will be removed in v0.18.0. The note is messaging at the top, not a change to how the skill works, so the frozen-prose rule of the parity plan keeps its meaning. `sdd init` additionally emits an info-level log line communicating the same, reaching users who upgrade without invoking the skill. The README leads with engine use and moves the skill instructions into a collapsed section marked deprecated. Renaming `/sdd` to `/sdd-skill` now was considered and rejected: it would break existing setups twice, once at the rename and again at the removal, and adds nothing over the notes.

In v0.18.0 the bundled skill renders are removed and the thin `/sdd-engine` skill is renamed to `/sdd`. `sdd init` must then delete installed skill files whose bundled source is gone, with the safety the orphan-cleanup gap (20260507-174656-s-tac-zaz) sketches so user-modified copies are never silently deleted. Without this cleanup every existing install keeps stale skill copies and the dual-flow confusion the deprecation exists to end comes back. The removal is committed for v0.18.0 and ships only after the bootstrap parity evaluation (20260726-195300-d-tac-fim) records a positive verdict; that evaluation sits on the release's critical path.

Existing users learn of the transition through the skill's deprecation note, the init log notice, and the release notes.
