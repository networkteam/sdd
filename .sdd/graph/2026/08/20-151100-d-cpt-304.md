Mechanical close-validation stops checking which kind of entry may close which, keeping only three cases that are wrong whatever the entry says; what makes a close valid is that the closing entry states why the target no longer holds, and pre-flight checks that statement and flags a weak one instead of blocking it.

**The kind cannot tell you whether a close is honest.** A close stops the graph asserting something, so that what is left standing models the project better than before. That goes wrong in two ways. Something true is dropped: the target still held, and the graph is now silent about it. Or something owed is falsely settled: the target demanded action or input, never got it, and the picture now reads calmer than the project is. Both depend on what the closing entry says. A rule written over kinds cannot read that, so it can only refuse the combinations that usually go wrong, which refuses the honest ones along with them, and each of those then has to be allowed by hand.

**Otherwise a second entry has to be written that records no work.** When the entry reporting the news is not allowed to close, someone must add another entry whose only content is that the first one no longer applies. A recorded deviation says deliveries keep failing grade. The supplier then goes out of business, and the new gap already carries the whole story of why the old deviation describes nothing any more. Requiring a done in between, to declare the old gap moot, makes the graph longer without making it truer, and puts the reason for the retirement in a different entry from the retirement.

**Allowing one case at a time never finishes.** In June the gate blocked a fact or insight dissolving a question, a path the framework had designed and documented. The fix allowed that one target and recorded that other non-done signal closures stay blocked (20260617-170241-s-tac-4j7). Three months later the same problem came back with a fact or insight as the target. Meanwhile pre-flight's unusual-close check offers `fact` closing `fact` with stated rationale as a worked example of a valid, low-severity close — an entry the gate makes impossible to write. The framework holds both rules at once, and the stricter one wins because it runs first and blocks.

**What stays refused, whatever the entry says.** A settled directive is finished when it is written, and only a superseding entry retires it. A question, an actor and an annotation state no findings, so none of them can report that something stopped holding. And a decision other than a directive does not close another decision, because `supersedes` records that with its lineage and costs no extra entry. What goes is the blanket rule that only done-kind signals may close, which today refuses a gap retiring a stale reading, a fact retiring a fact where no corrected version exists, and a gap answering whether something was a real deviation.

**Nothing blocks a careless close any more, and that is accepted.** Pre-flight can be skipped, and the unusual-close check flags rather than blocks, so a close with no reasoning in it will now land where the gate used to stop it. The trade is taken because the aim is not a perfect graph but one that models reality better than the state before it: a wrong close can be corrected by a later entry that says why, using the same freedom this directive grants. Raised in dialogue as an idea and deliberately not committed here: a check over graph changes that spots moves which look wrong and flags them for a person to review, since both people and agents make mistakes.

**What this owes.** The blanket clause comes out of the close validation, leaving the three refusals above. Every kind fact prints that clause in its mechanics section from one declaration in the code, so all of them update themselves when it changes, and only two hand-written sentences need rewriting: the gap fact (20260815-100000-s-prc-gpk) says a gap never closes anything itself, which this overturns, and the fact fact (20260816-110000-s-prc-kno) says any kind may close a fact, which is looser than this rule and must exclude the three kinds that state no findings. The retirement table in the bundled framework-concepts reference still sends a corrected insight through a directive, and is the last leg to reconcile. A done recording those changes closes this directive.

**Why the gap it closes retires.** That gap (20260608-004727-s-prc-4kh) recorded documentation, validator and practice disagreeing about how an insight is retired, and offered two options: sanction a done closing an insight as first-class, or call the practice wrong. Both were narrower than the disagreement. The question was never which kinds are allowed to close, but whether the kind decides anything at all, and with that answered neither option needs choosing: the practice it worried about was sound, the validator is the part that moves, and the documentation row is the outlier.
