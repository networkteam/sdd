SDD gains `distill`: the move that turns material arriving in dialogue into an aligned picture of the entries the graph should gain, handing to a shared slicing procedure — factored out rather than written a third time — once that picture is clear enough. This chooses a route for the gap that has stood since April in one framing or another (20260826-003829-s-prc-1s1).

**The arc.** Input arrives in the dialogue — a pasted transcript, a document, the findings of work done elsewhere. Distill takes it in, grounds it against the graph (what is already recorded, what is genuinely new, what contradicts), and aligns with the user on what the material means before proposing any shape. When the picture looks clear enough the agent says so and proposes moving on; whether it is clear enough is the user's call, made in the dialogue. Then slicing runs: the constellation is proposed as a set, with each entry's kind and layer, the references between them, and what is deliberately left unminted; the judgment calls go to the user; the agreed set is then captured in dependency order through parented capture sub-moves. Research and alignment are the weight of distill, and they are why it is a move rather than a preamble to capture.

**Why slicing is separate.** Bootstrap already proposes a cluster and materializes it in dependency order (20260719-140000-d-prc-bst); interview already turns a synthesis into user-picked captures (20260706-180000-d-prc-int). Writing the step a third time inside distill is the duplication axis of the entry-craft directive (20260810-121912-d-cpt-7zr) at procedure scale. Factored out, one procedure carries it, and the split criterion that directive commits — content that is its own concept or carries its own lifecycle earns its own entry, calibrated by whether the resulting constellation serves future pulls — becomes a step the engine runs rather than a rule the drafting agent is expected to recall unprompted. That is also why this addresses the structure-time knowledge gap (20260826-002704-s-cpt-mas) in part: a named step is something a pull can attach to. It does not resolve that gap, which also needs the vocabulary to exist as a fact.

**Slicing plays back structure, not only meaning.** Bootstrap's cluster playback is deliberately meaning-level — kinds and references stay the agent's craft — because its user may be meeting SDD for the first time. Distill's user is deciding the shape of the record, and getting the kinds and edges right is the point of the step, so its playback shows them and asks for the calls. The shared procedure therefore needs a register its caller sets, not one fixed posture.

**Composition with intake, not absorption.** The intake model (20260603-172628-d-cpt-fbi) stands as decided: channel items are captured as intake signals and surfaced for grooming. Distill neither replaces that surface nor folds into it — once the intake kind exists, one or several groomed intake signals become an input a distill run anchors on, exactly as a pasted transcript is. Intake keeps ingestion and triage; distill owns the promotion into an aligned entry set. Folding the two together was weighed and set aside: it would rebuild intake's grooming surface inside distill for no gain, where composition leaves each with one job.

**The name.** Chosen over `mine`, the April framing's word: mining reads as extraction from inert ore, while the material is usually someone's work. Distill names the act — reducing a large and partly redundant volume to the entries that carry it, in the context of what the graph already holds.

Pending: the procedure spec follows, and settles the slicing procedure's canonical name, its register parameter, and whether bootstrap and interview migrate onto it or keep their own steps for now.
