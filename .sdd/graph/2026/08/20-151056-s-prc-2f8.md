SDD v0.17.0-alpha.2 is published as the second prerelease of v0.17.0, and writing its notes forced a standing change to how this project writes them. The tag sits at `0aa39172` and carries the type-system knowledge work: the per-kind authoring facts, capture serving the type-system overview before drafting, the capture writing guide, sessions priming with the working principles, one construction boundary for every write surface, and capturable procedures. Notes, four platform archives, checksums, build provenance and `install.sh` are attached.

**Why a second alpha rather than a promotion.** The readiness triage (20260815-000432-s-tac-xlw) holds that the next stable is the release carrying "work in the engine now", gated by six blockers. Two were checked against the source during this cut and are still open: search over MCP suppresses citations when the caller omits `max_citations`, and the binary still directs users to `.sdd/config.local.yaml`. The deprecation call itself is also unmade. So the delta shipped as a prerelease and v0.17.0 stays free for the release that carries the message.

**The notes standard, settled while drafting.** The instruction file's ban on em dashes in outward-facing prose was recorded after the release skill was written, and the skill's notes format was built on them: every highlight bullet led with a bolded name joined to its narrative by an em dash. Raised as a conflict rather than resolved silently, Christopher ruled the instruction file right. Bullets now close their bolded name with a period and continue as a plain sentence, the ban is stated in the skill's writing rules, and the skill's own prose was swept too, since banning a mark while using it throughout reads badly. Two span rules were settled in the same dialogue, on Christopher's question about whether alpha.2 should carry only its delta: a break announced in an earlier prerelease is repeated in every later prerelease of the same version, because a reader installing the later tag arrives from the last stable and never saw the earlier notes; and a stable release carries the complete span since the previous stable, not the delta since its last prerelease. Alpha.2's notes therefore repeat the module-port break under a line saying it is unchanged. All of this lands in the release skill, following the precedent that release-process learning is absorbed there (20260508-102334-s-prc-lep).

**Evidence.** The release is at https://github.com/networkteam/sdd/releases/tag/v0.17.0-alpha.2; the skill changes are commits `0aa39172`, `6beed6a2` and `ac80b9c8`. The post-ship verification the skill prescribes came back clean on all four checks. The prerelease flag held at true, so the `release.prerelease: auto` fix the previous cut made is doing its job where that cut's flag had been overwritten; `releases/latest` and the Homebrew tap both stayed on the previous stable.

Applying the new standard to the releases already published is a separate sweep, recorded on its own.
