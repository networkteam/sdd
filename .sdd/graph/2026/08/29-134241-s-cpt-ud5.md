The dialogue layer itself can carry process: the engine already structures moves, and the same machinery, extended by an explicit goal hierarchy and a register of open leads, would shape the conversation between moves that today runs unshaped.

The boundary this reads against is deliberate: the engine drives move instances, never the conversation, and the weave between instances stays conversational (20260702-174833-d-cpt-3yw). The steering gap shows the cost of that unshaped weave: a concept-level challenge has no served place to land, so it gets absorbed at the level of whatever move is running (20260829-133504-s-prc-qd5).

The reading: two session-level registers would bound the weave from both sides. A goal hierarchy places the dialogue's goal above each move's goal; it is aligned with the user in the early turns and stays renegotiable, including widening when the dialogue was framed narrower than what surfaced. A leads register holds the threads a dialogue notices but does not follow, in the engine instead of agent context: one line each, droppable without ceremony, nothing owed on any of them, which is what separates it from a task list. At junctions the two meet: the agent puts the open leads against the goal, may recommend against leads that do not serve it, and offers ending the session as a first-class outcome. Whether leads hang as leaves of the goal tree or sit flat beside it stays open; the observed relation is promotion, a lead taken up becomes a sub-goal, while a dormant lead need not serve any goal. Both registers are session scaffolding under the standing calibration (20260727-224047-d-cpt-u8o): they die with the session, and a lead worth keeping crystallizes into a graph entry before the end. A cross-session lead store would be a parallel record and is exactly what this must not become.

The same structure gives the planned, not yet built rule system its attachment surface. Routing designated user interactions through the engine turns the dialogue into a sequence of observable interaction points, and the rule plan's in-flow delivery (20260622-084244-d-tac-eho) can propose applicable rules exactly where the dialogue passes a point they govern, framework defaults shaped per project, the way hooks attach to a lifecycle. Which turns pass through the engine and which stay host-side is the unexplored distinction this depends on.

Two bounds on the reading, marked. Registering must stay near-free, one line maintained by the agent; a register that demands its own mode of interaction costs the very flow it exists to protect. And a goal register alone would not have prevented the observed failure: the analysis behind the steering gap records explicit goal statements that held while the concept challenge was still absorbed at move level. What it adds is a served place where a challenge to the ground can land and be measured, instead of competing with a move's local goal. Medium confidence accordingly: the connection rests on one analyzed session and the architecture; live junctions run against a declared goal would firm or break it. Nothing is owed on this reading; it is the explored space the decision addressing the steering gap draws from.
