When the user questions whether a concept is right at all, the agent answers at the level of the move it is running, adjusting the artifact in front of it instead of stepping up to survey the concept.

Observed in the dialogue of 2026-08-26/27 that produced the delegate-session directive and the superseded resume directive (20260827-233925-d-tac-9om), reconstructed from the session transcript by a delegated analysis, attached as [steering analysis](./29-133504-s-prc-qd5/session-steering-analysis.md). Steering as such held: of 31 corrections across the arc, 25 landed on first serve and none were ignored. The failure is localized and specific. Six goal-level asks, all forms of one question, whether a dialogue may be derived from a transport connection, drew local answers across ninety minutes: a reworded draft paragraph, a code review pronouncing the mechanism sound, a pattern-insight proposal, a fresh abstraction, before the first plain no. Every miss occurred with a capture draft at playback or a wrap-up on offer; every steer in free design dialogue landed. The concept's uses surfaced one round at a time instead of through one sweep, and its lifecycle use was never found that night; it failed in the field the next day (20260828-164140-s-tac-mss). The survey that resolved the question ran only when the user dictated its method: asked how an agent can get a handle at all, the agent enumerated the routes and the design fell out in minutes, preserved in the capability directive (20260828-165352-d-cpt-aen). Separately, the resume directive asserted a delegate could no longer find the parent handle while probe output refuting it, from the same conversation a day earlier, stood in context; the contradiction sat inside a single turn, the agent caught it itself ninety seconds after the write, and the correction was recorded by supersession (20260827-235350-d-tac-d15).

Expected, stated by the user in this dialogue: session goals form a hierarchy, the dialogue's goal above each move's, and a concept-level challenge belongs above the move that happens to be running. The working shape it demands: park the running capture, make the survey the governing goal, as a sub-goal beneath the dialogue's, or by widening the dialogue's own goal when it was framed narrower than the concept under question, then explore the ground, lay out the whole picture, align on it, decide, and only then return to capturing. The served working principles carry the first half of this, when work stops resolving, step up; their arrival at session open is validated while their effect on behavior was left open (20260811-122422-s-tac-aqg).

That open question now has a negative data point: the posture was in context throughout and the step-up never happened unforced. Candidates from this dialogue, marked as interpretation, not observation: an open draft supplies a cheap legitimate local move, adjust, exactly when the survey is owed, so the move's narrow goal absorbs the challenge; park existed as the escape and nothing served names it; and no goal level exists between the session shell's standing goal and a move's step goal where a survey sub-goal could be set and aligned. The shape extends the recorded research-failure family, pushback answered at the same depth rather than deeper, held evidence unconsulted (20260528-170354-s-prc-uq8).
