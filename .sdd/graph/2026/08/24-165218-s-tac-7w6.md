Pre-flight reads its ref-kind vocabulary from the legacy skill render and continues silently when the read fails, while the v0.18.0 plan removes that render from the binary. The actual state, read from the code: refKindVocabulary in internal/llm/preflight.go loads references/ref-kinds.md through the bundled-skills store and ignores the error, so a failed read renders the ref-meta rubric with an empty vocabulary and no message. The expected state has two sources. The v0.18.0 retirement plan (20260821-123832-d-tac-ip1) commits that the binary carries no legacy skill render and that no bundled surface still points at the skill-driven flow, which leaves this read with nothing to read; from v0.18.0 on the only rendered skill is the thin wrapper pointing at the engine, so pre-flight becomes the vocabulary's only consumer. The repository's engineering rules (AGENTS.md, fail loud) require a failure to surface to a caller that can act, never to degrade into silent continuation. The kind-knowledge directive points the same way: kind knowledge lives in the graph as facts and the capture writing guide already renders the type-system overview and the drafted kind's fact from the graph (20260811-184407-d-cpt-x7a), leaving pre-flight as the remaining consumer whose framework knowledge comes from a skill file.

Candidates, unsettled: move the ref-kind vocabulary into a base fact and have pre-flight render its framework knowledge from the graph as the writing guide does; independently, make the vocabulary read fail loud so a missing source blocks instead of quietly weakening the rubric.
