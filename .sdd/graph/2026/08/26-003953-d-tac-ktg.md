Capture's play-back must deliver its committed completeness as a delta after the first round, and must let a correction reach the fields it actually needs — two constraints added to the fourth boundary of the plan that commits play-back to render the complete entry (20260821-105247-d-tac-q1x). Both rest on a measurement taken after that plan was drafted (20260825-234504-s-tac-jro), so neither overturns a judgment made on the evidence now available.

**Completeness, bounded.** The plan commits play-back to render the entry as it will be written, replacing today's fixed field subset. That is right for fidelity and, as stated, wrong for volume: play-back already re-serves the whole draft every revision round and is the heaviest single item in a capture-heavy session (20260826-000339-s-tac-fyi), so making it complete multiplies the largest payload by the round count. The refinement leaves the fidelity goal untouched and bounds its cost — the entry arrives whole at the first play-back, and later rounds carry only what changed, which is already what the step's own instruction tells the agent to show the user.

**The adjust surface, reopened.** The plan excluded this deliberately: play-back was to gain visibility, not correctability, and making every field adjustable was named out of scope. That call followed the release triage's severity rule — what matters is not how bad a gap looks but whether a fallback exists (20260815-000432-s-tac-xlw) — and for an uncorrectable field a fallback does exist: abandon the draft and rebuild it. What the measurement adds is what that fallback costs on both axes. It spends the time twice over, and it frees none of the context it consumed: the discarded serves stay in the window for the rest of the session, which on the axis that decides how long a session can run at all makes the fallback a permanent charge rather than a detour. Two such abandons in one session discarded and re-paid a substantial share of both. Read against context and not time alone, the triage's own rule now argues the other way. So the correctable set widens toward what a revision needs, and the deviation that records it (20260811-232108-s-tac-kus) has its second half addressed rather than left standing after the plan ships.

**The three fields are not equal, and the plan should not assume they are.** `attachments` is gate-neutral — adding it to the revision paths is the declaration-only repair that gap already describes. `entryKind` and `layer` change which gate applies and which authoring guidance is live, but that re-evaluation is the assemble step's ordinary behaviour, since it already re-serves what is missing until a draft is valid; it is cheaper than abandoning on every axis. One condition rides with them: state licensed only by the superseded kind — an intent collected while the draft was a directive, say — is cleared or refused at the gate, or a kind change silently writes a field the new kind never licensed.
