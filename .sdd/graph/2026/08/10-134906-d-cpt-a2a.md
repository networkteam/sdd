Always-on priming principles live in the graph as fact entries and are served in full at session open through a shell-declared topic selector — the shell owns selection and placement, never the words. This realizes the primed-rather-than-pulled commitment of the posture directive (20260810-123226-d-cpt-ei0) with no new metadata concept and no hand-copied shell prose.

## Mechanism

Priming content is authored as facts — base facts when it ships with the framework, project facts when local — carrying an agreed topic from a priming family, with `priming/interactive` as the first. Each shell procedure declares in its spec a selector over active facts by topic, and the opening serve renders the selected bodies in full, positioned before the process core and the move enumeration: the counter-balance instructs before the structure begins to bias, which is what primed rather than pulled means mechanically. The user-dialogue shell (20260704-100000-d-prc-dlg) is the first consumer; automated shells opt in by declaring their own selector.

Selection stays shell-side and topic-based on purpose: alternatives that put the declaration on the fact — a `prime` metadata block naming session modes — were rejected because they invent a selection vocabulary the graph already has. Topics remain the only vocabulary, so per-shell targeting, automated-session opt-in, and per-project priming families need no further mechanism, and graph-resident project procedures can declare their own priming.

This deliberately inverts the declaration side relative to the fact index (20260719-133529-d-cpt-qhn), where enrollment lives on the fact itself — and the discriminator is one rule, not two: the fact declares what it is, the shell declares what it needs. Index enrollment is intrinsic and consumer-independent — a fact either is indexable reference with a retrieval cue or it is not, identically for every shell. Priming is a consumption choice that varies per shell and is paid for at every open of that shell — so it lives on the consumer. The mechanism also extends qhn's derivation pattern from pointer-strength index lines to serve-in-full for the selected few: the deliberate exception to the index's never-duplicate-bodies discipline, allowed because a pointer cannot prime, and safe because the content renders from the graph at open — the fact owns the words, supersession and project override flow through ordinary resolution, and the authority boundary of the knowledge homes (20260730-110228-d-cpt-rh6) holds with a single home.

## Guard

The priming set stays small by discipline rather than mechanism: every priming fact is paid for in context at every session open — the dilution cost of the entry-craft criterion (20260810-121912-d-cpt-7zr) at its maximum — so the priming topic is applied rarely and deliberately, and a fact that primes is written tersest of all.
