A guiding directive is not an aspiration, and a live pre-flight specimen supplies the discriminator the aspiration-shape check lacks: the two kinds differ in the force they exert, not in their lack of a completion criterion. An aspiration exercises a pull — strategic direction that tells the work which way to go, with no single piece of work conforming to it — while a guiding directive pushes: it binds concretely, and work and entries are expected to conform to it. Absence of a completion criterion is shared by both kinds by design and therefore cannot discriminate.

The specimen: at the capture of the way-of-thinking posture (20260810-123226-d-cpt-ei0), the directive-reads-aspiration-shaped check fired a medium finding, reasoning from the missing completion criterion and the standing-orientation language alone. Christopher's judgment names two reasons the directive kind stands, and both generalize. On nature: an aspiration carries a higher, unreachable strategic quality — in his words, something like "all information in an organization is captured in entries that conform and can be used to build the best specific context for each situation" — it orients on the way, pull, without ever binding, where a guiding directive binds in every session, push, and its violation is observable. On structure: within the framework, the aspiration layer belongs to each adopting project's own strategic pulls, so a framework-level meta-posture parked as an aspiration would occupy the slot projects are meant to fill; `intent: guiding` exists precisely so a directive can carry standing, non-completing character without changing kind.

The distinction also earns its keep operationally: the active aspirations serve as the base constellation against which the graph's movement is checked for direction — the very property the capture guides' aspiration exception relies on (20260810-141555-d-cpt-20r) — and as preparation for dialogue: where should dialogue lead? In a novel situation the graph does not back, the aspirations are the constellation to check it against. Which also means aspirations are not fixed doctrine — they can be extended over time, and change when a misalignment is observed. A strategic guiding directive could not absorb this role without collapsing the direction check into conformance.

This is a concrete calibration case for the capture guides, whose tuning is committed to run against real capture traffic: the aspiration-shape check needs the pull-versus-push discriminator — direction versus conformance — not the completion-criterion heuristic alone.
