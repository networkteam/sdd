Entry checking at capture splits into two scoped LLM guides that run before the user's confirmation — a writing guide judging the draft in isolation and a graph-fit guide judging it in graph context — while the write step keeps only mechanical validation, so LLM findings become drafting input resolved by judgment in the dialogue and a confirmed entry writes without oscillation.

## Two scopes

**The writing guide sees the entry in isolation.** A fresh LLM context holds the draft, the entry-craft criteria (20260810-121912-d-cpt-7zr), and kind-specific style instructions — and not the dialogue that produced the entry; that absence is the instrument. The craft criterion requires an entry to stand alone, and its fit axis names stranding as the one failure the author structurally cannot detect — the author fills gaps from context without noticing — so only a reader outside the dialogue can run the test, and a fresh context is that reader by construction. The guide calibrates against the full criterion — both fit directions, conflation, form as the kind requires — and its context needs are small: the draft plus instructions on what a good entry is.

**The graph-fit guide sees the entry in the graph — a bounded view of it.** It carries the judgment the current pre-flight attempts — does the draft duplicate or contradict what its neighborhood holds, do its refs fit what the body asserts — with context bounded for scale: the draft's declared targets (refs, supersedes, closes) served in detail, plus retrieval-selected semantic neighbors of the draft's content, never the whole graph. Duplication beyond the declared refs is caught by the retrieved neighbors, and blanket-loading whole classes of entries — every active guiding directive, say — is rejected as brute force with unknown applicability. The one exception is the active aspirations, included at summary strength regardless of refs: small and stable by kind semantics, and the one class where contradiction matters globally — with only contradiction of an aspiration ever a finding, never missing alignment (the strategic-grounding-stifling anti-pattern the corpus documents). Everything below the aspiration layer stays retrieval-bounded, and global coherence stays with dialogue and grooming. The bounded-but-graph-facing context is the exact inverse of the writing guide's requirement, which is why the two are separate invocations with separate instructions and separate calibration; both return findings that prompt the agent toward a change, per axis.

## One timing

The flow through the capture procedure: assemble → both guides → reassemble, the agent folding findings in by judgment and surfacing the substantive ones in dialogue → playback for the user's confirmation → write. Findings arrive while the draft is malleable — drafting input, never post-approval rejection — and neither guide is a hard gate: returning a substantially changed draft to the guides is the agent's judgment. This fits the existing state machine — assemble already re-serves until the draft is complete and playback already loops on adjust; the guides sit between the two as an engine capability.

## The write step is mechanical

After confirmation only deterministic validation remains: every reference resolves or capture blocks (20260628-130400-d-cpt-uh0), and the entry is structurally valid. Moving LLM judgment out of the write path removes the post-confirmation findings loop — the oscillation the pre-flight failure corpus documents (20260415-112708-s-prc-dix): find-something bias, editorial rewriting, prose-completeness policing, and marginal verdicts of the a-better-ref or not-worth-a-supersede sort bouncing entries the user had already confirmed, until the gate got skipped. Those judgments belong in the dialogue before the confirm, and the user override that existed to escape them dissolves together with the loop. This refines the capture procedure (20260703-094500-d-prc-cap): assemble, playback, and summary verification stand; the gate between confirmation and write goes mechanical.

## Open

The kind-specific style instructions the writing guide consumes are future work — the per-kind crafting guidance is deliberately being developed against real specimens rather than settled in theory — and both guides' calibration is expected to be tuned against real capture traffic, with the corpus as the evidence bar.
