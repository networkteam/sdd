A done signal's provenance follows its act: a done that performs a delivery carries the evidence, while a done that only retires a commitment delivered elsewhere carries a pointer to the record that has it. Both meet the same demand — a reader must be able to trust the claim and find the work — so requiring evidence of the second misreads what the entry did.

This was settled against a thin retirement specimen (20260614-134025-s-tac-wts): one paragraph, no evidence of its own, grounded in the earlier done that carries the delivery. Judged in dialogue that is the right shape, not a gap — the act was retirement, and reproducing the delivery record would be the fault. The general form already exists as a validator rubric written for the checking side (20260617-155740-d-prc-kqx); what is new is the reader-facing form, that the act decides where provenance lives, so the same absence is correct in one done and a defect in another. It bounds the provenance half of the first done-kind discriminator (20260811-160518-s-cpt-igk), which established evidence as load-bearing without saying which dones owe it.

The discriminator is not yet earned as guide text. Three runs of the live writing guide over this specimen never demanded evidence — the misfire predicted in dialogue did not occur — and each returned exactly one finding, on the commitment being retired, calling it a mention the entry never anchors. The entry does anchor it: the commitment is named in the frontmatter field recording what this done closes, and that field is not among those the guide is shown. The finding is therefore sound reasoning on input the guide never received, and it measures what the guide is given rather than absent per-kind guidance. Because the rollout directive keeps per-kind sections absent until specimens earn them (20260811-141038-d-tac-rvu), this rule stays banked rather than written; what would earn it is a later specimen where a delivery done omits the evidence its act owes. The verbatim findings of these runs are kept with the sibling discriminator from the same sweep (20260811-174502-s-cpt-ayb).
