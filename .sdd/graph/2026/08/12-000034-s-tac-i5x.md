The four gaps the session-subsystem rebuild absorbed are verified resolved and closed together: the relocation-core simplification gap (20260726-195959-s-tac-wg5), the structural reclamation gap (20260728-004924-s-tac-gtx), the post-conclude lifecycle gap (20260729-084833-s-tac-3be), and the superseded-identity discovery gap (20260724-145631-s-tac-vxs). The rebuild's closing done (20260811-234201-s-tac-dq7) holds the criterion-by-criterion evidence, so none of it is restated here.

They stayed open because no delivery signal in the chain claimed anything beyond its own slice, and the closing done retired only the plan — leaving four gaps whose substance had been answered still reading as open work. Each was checked against the current code and behaviour before closing, not inferred from the plan having landed.

**s-tac-wg5** listed seven simplification items and every one holds in the tree, checked individually: the ancestor-pinning containment apparatus is down to a single `os.OpenRoot`, the parallel stores are collapsed to one session store and one blob store, both transaction engines are replaced by a single publish helper, the Windows-only build files and the transition marker are gone entirely, the routing policy has moved out of `cmd` into `local`, and branch precedence is consolidated into `effectiveTarget`, documented as the sole rule shared by reads and writes.

**s-tac-gtx** asked for a port that could express a sweep at all. It can, and as of the rebuild's final run an external composition is proven to reach collection through that published surface rather than through local-only code — which is the specific thing this gap doubted, since its own diagnosis was that sweeping had been assigned to composition-side work no composition ever built.

**s-tac-3be** is met on both halves of its minimum ask, exercised live in the dialogue that closed the plan: concluding returned the statement that continuing means opening a new session, and the door then minted a new handle rather than returning the spent one.

**s-tac-vxs** closes on a decision already taken rather than on a mechanism built, and the distinction matters because the gap's real question was which of the two it would get. It asked for a dialogue on detection, user-facing availability, and any A-to-B migration policy, explicitly refusing an implementation-time registry, mutation block, or rekey state machine. That dialogue happened in the rebuild plan (20260730-093011-d-tac-k4q), which settled the question as an observable acceptance and carried it as one of its acceptance criteria — a session whose only copy lies under a superseded repository identity is absent from listings, with no surface offering to move it. That criterion is among those verified met. What this entry records is therefore that the decided acceptance is in force, not a fresh choice to accept.
