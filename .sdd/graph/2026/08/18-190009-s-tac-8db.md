The vision landscape is built and live on the documentation website as its new Vision section. Two pages: "The SDD Landscape" with the maturity legend, the region map, the six why-first region texts, the collaboration view, and the work-lifecycle visuals with the before/after contrast against the email/meetings/documents/tickets lifecycle; and "How will that feel?" with three role scenes (product owner, project lead, UX designer), each carrying an honest note on what works today and a closing line lifting the pattern beyond the agency example. The work landed in commits 1a6a6c0a (two MDX pages, five SVG components, sidebar entry) and 4263b7f7 (wording fix), with rendering verified on the local dev server. This records progress on the landscape plan (20260818-133112-d-tac-okx) without closing it: refinement and iteration are still needed before the material can be shared outside the project with confidence.

Production followed the plan's attachment: two context-inheriting writing agents produced region texts and scenes in parallel, a third built the visuals, and assembly plus a style pass happened inline after repeated service overloads stopped the delegated integration. Christopher reviewed on the rendered site and one feedback round is folded in. Its themes matter for the next iteration more than the file-level changes the commits already carry: the agent's role stepped down from "counts like a colleague's" to contributing under rules and guidelines the graph itself carries; meetings elevated rather than demoted, with assistants preparing participants and humans curating what feeds back; dialogue presented as the work itself rather than only capture; and a standing writing rule recorded in the project instructions, no em dashes in outward-facing prose. Four of the plan's five acceptance criteria are met; the review criterion is only partially exercised (a browse and one feedback round, no full read yet), which is why the plan stays open for the next iteration.
