The capture friction recorded in 20260731-083611-s-cpt-wag produces two distinct failure shapes, not one, and only the shape it happened to observe is beyond mechanical reach — which means the unbuilt body-to-edge enforcement would catch half the population rather than none of it. A second instance turned up independently while implementing supersede-aware ranking: 20260729-092849-d-cpt-tm1 states in both its body and its generated summary that it supersedes a predecessor, but carries no `supersedes` key, so the predecessor still derived as active and the two entries stood as live duplicates of one rule until a correction was written. That correction cost a third full capture of a long directive, because immutability means the edge cannot be added after the fact.

This is wag's mechanism on the other half of the field pair — lifecycle fields are start-only params, so an author who settles the supersede framing while drafting has no legal field for it — and it confirms the diagnosis wag reasons to without having observed it: wag names `supersedes` alongside `closes` and proposes moving both to `state:`, on evidence drawn only from the `closes` side.

The correction it forces is to wag's detection analysis. wag concludes the failure is edge *kind* rather than edge presence, because its reported instance put the target in `refs` under `addresses`, which satisfies the body↔edge consistency rule and survives a diff against refs ∪ closes ∪ supersedes — so it reads the missing coverage as an absent category. The second instance fails differently: the predecessor's ID appears in the body and summary and in *no* edge at all. That is edge absence, exactly what the enforcement recorded as never built in 20260614-225650-s-tac-dxh would have flagged. So the population splits — authors who reach for a plausible `refs` kind produce the uncatchable shape, authors who simply write the prose produce the catchable one — and building the long-promised check is worth more than wag's framing implies, without being sufficient on its own.

One further connection neither entry draws: 20260731-083311-s-tac-4vx is the same family. wag says the lifecycle fields are start-only; 4vx says the `supersedes` param is additionally single-valued. Both are the lifecycle params being under-powered, and the declaration move wag proposes is where arity would be fixed too — so a redesign should treat them together rather than as two patches.
