No move turns a body of input — a pasted transcript, a document, a debugging session's findings — into an aligned picture of the entries the graph should gain.

**Observed.** The act recurs and has no named home. It was recorded in April against a podcast transcript: load the source, find where it touches the graph, dialogue to filter for relevance, capture what survives. It recurred this week in another project, where a debugging session produced a body of connected findings and the agent laid out a four-entry constellation — kinds, layers, the edges between them, two judgment calls put to the user, residuals named rather than minted — before any capture ran. Both times it was improvised from scratch.

Two procedures now carry the step in narrower forms. Bootstrap (20260719-140000-d-prc-bst) proposes a coherent cluster and materializes it in dependency order through parented captures, but only to orient a fresh project, and its playback is deliberately meaning-level — kinds and references stay the agent's craft rather than the user's to check. Interview (20260706-180000-d-prc-int) carries the tail: a synthesis whose crystallized outcomes the user picks, dispatching a capture for each. Neither is reachable for material that simply arrives. The intake model (20260603-172628-d-cpt-fbi) reframed the mine move as ingest-as-intake-then-groom and already names the act — distilled entries captured through dialogue, with a triage ladder by complexity — but assumes the material arrived asynchronously through a transport channel and was parked as an `intake` entry first; that kind is not in the type system and the channel agent is unbuilt, so the in-dialogue case has sat behind an unbuilt program since June.

**Expected**, stated plainly rather than pointed at: a way of working that recurs has a named home, or it stays improvised and undiscoverable. Under the engine that home is a procedure.

**The deviation**, sharper than its April framing: what is missing is not capture, which takes one entry at a time and does that well, but the step before — designing the entry set as a set, with the kinds, the edges and the judgment calls surfaced for the user, so what gets written is a considered picture rather than an accumulation. That step is also where the sibling gap (20260826-002704-s-cpt-mas) lands: at the moment kinds and reference kinds are actually chosen, no framework knowledge reaches the agent, so the picture gets designed without the vocabulary it is drawn in.

The April gap is superseded rather than left standing because its framing names the Claude skill as the thing lacking the move, and that skill is retiring.
