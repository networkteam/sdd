The first three slices of the bounded-serves plan (20260829-173342-d-tac-rzi) shipped on main in nine commits (6b1d0b37, 16c5b8c2, a38651c0, befa3280, 931690a3, e8e17666, d09ed520, 655ac649, cee67707), realizing the settled architecture (20260830-112915-d-tac-qwc) in code: after them, every measured automatic serve on this repository's live graph is under the provisional 36000-byte budget.

Slice one built the measurement: per-part byte accounting on every serve, a realistic proctest fixture populating each surface the audit found scaling, a harness walking all ten shipped procedures to their heavy serves with unmeasured steps logged as coverage debt, and a wire test replacing the fixture-blind door test, which now documents the door's 25KB contract breach instead of hiding it. Calibration set the fix order: the four chain-serving steps (engage, evaluate, implementation, explore) measured 42 to 43KB, catch-up compose 31KB.

Slice two delivered the mechanism: internal/truncate (cuts as data separated from meta), internal/serveview (part kinds, caps, the calibrated default budget), inject caps declared in the spec and resolved against the query's registration Bound at the engine's two serve seams, cut meta routed out of band into an engine-owned cuts lane so authored text is untouchable by construction, and the three previous cut styles collapsed onto one mechanism, deleting the silent one.

Slice three bounded the pipeline: skip(N) composes with n(N) and sections keep their raw source, so a cut's pull is the expression the user wrote; served entry chains are budgeted per direction through the existing truncated-ref frontier, dropping the four chain serves to 27 to 28KB; served views cut focus groups, participant groups, WIP markers, whole bodies, and per-entry ref lines at whole units, each with its dropped count and a runnable pull; and catch-up's blanket cap became six per-lane caps. Explicit pulls pass no budget and arrive complete.

Two things surfaced in the doing. Splitting catch-up's lanes exposed that pending recovery notices were appended per view call, so a multi-lane serve would repeat them; recovery is now served once per session and viewLayout lanes opt out. And the view-grammar base fact needed no hand edit for skip(N): it generates from the executor's vocabulary table, which held.

This is partial progress and closes nothing. Open before the plan closes: bounds on MCP response line lists, interpolated store values, and the draft lane; the worst-case authoring arithmetic with its pre-flight and lint findings; the lint reshape on the application; and the live verification on a roughly 10K-token host. The plan keeps its WIP marker: the parked implementation run it marks carries the working notes, and the session listing resumes it.
