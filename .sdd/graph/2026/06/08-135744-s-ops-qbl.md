`sdd init` omits `.sdd/stats/` from the gitignore set it writes, so the machine-local LLM/embedding stats sink (`.sdd/stats/llm.jsonl`) shows up uncommitted in projects upgraded to the stats-bearing release. The sink shipped (s-tac-bmw) without the matching gitignore entry — the same kind of miss as when `.sdd/index/` was left off this list and caught against an external project (s-ops-626). A fresh instance of the systemic gap where a change ships without its dependent surfaces updated (s-prc-guf).
