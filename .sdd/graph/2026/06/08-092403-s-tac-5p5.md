General refs to superseded entries resolve only one hop, not transitively to the chain head. The model computes a ref target's status as superseded-by its immediate successor (`SupersededBy[id][0]`), so following a ref to a multiply-superseded entry means hopping chain-link by chain-link to reach the live entry — and tooling never resolves the ref to the current head for the reader. Actor-identity chains already do the opposite: they walk to a head for role-status and canonical derivation. The capture-time norm of reffing the current head cannot carry this alone — under concurrent work the head an author refs may already be superseded by the time the entry lands, and immutability means that ref can never be rewritten — so the mechanical fix is the durable one: resolve a ref to its transitive supersede-chain head at read time, in rendering (`sdd show` / `sdd view` ref sub-lines) and in pre-flight's ref context, generalizing the actor-chain head-walking the type-system contract (d-cpt-ni0) already defines for identity chains. Grounded in the immutability contract (d-cpt-e1i), which makes read-time resolution necessary rather than rewriting historical refs. The superseded-type-system-contract ref in the closure-mismatch gap (s-prc-4kh) is the ground-zero instance: once transitive resolution lands, it should surface the live head (d-cpt-ni0) without a manual hop.
