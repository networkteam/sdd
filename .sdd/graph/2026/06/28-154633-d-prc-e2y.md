Interactivity is the standard for agent–human dialogue: handbacks must build understanding and require the user to produce, not skim-and-confirm, and the agent must surface its own assumptions for correction, so the alignment check runs in both directions. The proven mechanic is pairing abstraction with carefully chosen concrete examples; specific production modes and how to scale tool use to misalignment risk stay open to develop through practice.
