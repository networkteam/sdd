Confirms the realized prompt-cache win that the pre-flight restructure (s-tac-sk1) was left open to verify. With Sonnet 4.6 carrying SDD's traffic exclusively, cache reuse stepped up sharply the day the byte-stable preamble shipped. Local stats (.sdd/stats/llm.jsonl) and the Anthropic console agree: before the restructure (Jun 5) pre-flight calls almost never reused the prefix — 177k cache-creation tokens against only 6.3k cache-read across 12 calls (read/create ≈ 0.05×); after it, reads matched or exceeded writes — 70.7k read vs 63.6k created across 19 calls (Jun 7), then 99.0k vs 71.9k (Jun 8) — a 0.05× → 1.0×+ shift in cache-read efficiency that has held since. The structural cause (volatile, type-specific content in the system block) was removed by the restructure; the cache is now demonstrably hit across sequential session captures.
