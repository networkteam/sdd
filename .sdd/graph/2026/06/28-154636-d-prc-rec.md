When a directive carrying active `refines` refinements is superseded rather than closed, those refinements go stale and must be resolved deliberately — either folded into the successor and closed, or superseded by a fresh entry re-anchored on the successor (promoted to standalone when scope no longer fits). Doing either automatically is rejected, because an auto-sweep may close still-valid refinements and an auto-repoint mis-anchors when scope has shifted. This is concrete SDD process content — a graph-authoring rule a participant follows, destined to become a graph-resident rule — so it is an ordinary development decision: pending until that ships, then closed, not standing development guidance.
