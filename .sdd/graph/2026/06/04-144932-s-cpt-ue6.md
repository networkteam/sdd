Analyzed Hermes (NousResearch's standalone autonomous agent) and Honcho (Plastic Labs' peer-memory infrastructure) against SDD's agent direction. Both surfaced in talks Christopher reviewed; Honcho also ships inside Hermes as its user-modeling layer.

**Hermes is the architecture we chose against.** It *is* the agent — it brings its own channels (Telegram/Discord/Slack/WhatsApp/Signal/email via a separate gateway process), its own tools, and its own code-execution sandbox (six backends, namespace isolation, serverless hibernate). That is the standalone-agent path d-stg-6za weighed and r57 rejected in favor of being a connector to whatever agent the user already has. So its sandbox and channel layers route around problems our connector architecture doesn't have — reference material for the self-hostable path, not drop-ins, and its core is Python against our Mastra/TypeScript runtime choice.

**Honcho's peer ≈ our actor, with inverted epistemics.** A peer is any participant, human or AI — our actor unification exactly. But Honcho *infers* an evolving, observer-relative representation of each peer (vector observations keyed by observer/observed pairs, background-derived conclusions, theory-of-mind), where our actor is a declared, canonical, write-once identity. Honcho's peer system is the implicit-learning mechanism pointed at people.

**The line we already drew holds.** Hermes' closed learning loop and Honcho's background derivation both commit knowledge without review, against dialogue-first (d-stg-beb) and reasoning-not-recording (s-stg-ob9). We rely on the human alignment step for now; the mining door (s-cpt-bn1) stays candidates-for-later, with Hermes+Honcho as proof the loop is viable when we open it.

Two new insights: (1) **perspectival memory as derivation** — Honcho stores observer-relative conclusions; SDD could *derive* a per-actor commitment/engagement footprint on read (what X decided, questioned, grounded in, which topics X touches — not personality), staying inside "the graph is the sole record" (d-stg-3k0) and seeding per-participant catch-up. (2) **a "reasoning-first" collision** — Honcho markets the exact phrase SDD uses but means automated inference, not human-AI dialogue; same word, opposite mechanism, which sharpens rather than blurs our differentiator.
