Insight-closure semantics are inconsistent across the framework's documentation, its validator, and its actual practice. The retirement guidance in framework-concepts routes insights through a directive ("noted, no action needed"), and pre-flight routes a done signal closing any non-gap signal to the short-loop check whose unusual_close partial flags it — yet in practice done signals are the most common way insights actually get closed: five of the nine closed insights in the graph were closed by done signals, versus four by decisions. Rule, validator, and behavior disagree. Either done-closes-insight should be sanctioned as a first-class closure path — sweeping an addressed insight into the done that completes its driving decision — or the practice is wrong and should route through a decision. Grounded in the type-system contract that makes insight a distinct, observational signal kind (d-cpt-ydf).
