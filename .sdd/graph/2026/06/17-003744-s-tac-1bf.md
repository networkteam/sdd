SDD has no way to emit its graph as an Open Knowledge Format (OKF) bundle, leaving it unable to feed the emerging vendor-neutral interchange ecosystem (Google's Knowledge Catalog, the static OKF visualizer, future OKF consumers) standardizing the markdown-frontmatter "agent knowledge" substrate. Grounded in the OKF v0.1 spec (20260617-001734-s-cpt-bhu), the export looks low-cost and largely a pure read-side render: OKF's conformance bar is minimal (a single non-empty `type` field), it already shares our substrate (markdown + frontmatter, path-as-identity, which our IDs already derive), and its rule that consumers preserve unknown frontmatter keys means our richer semantics (typed refs, closes/supersedes, confidence, layer, derived status) could ride along in extension keys while generic OKF tools ignore them. The field mappings are open design questions for the export work, not settled here — e.g. what to emit as OKF's `type` (our `kind` alone, or a composite like `[layer] [kind] [type]`), and how to render refs as links. Positioning: SDD becomes a producer feeding the substrate layer while keeping its strict, immutable canonical format and the dialogue-and-verification layer OKF deliberately omits. Separate from, but a sibling of, the Obsidian-mirror export idea (20260416-113122-s-tac-bta) — Obsidian stays its own idea; this is the vendor-neutral, spec-backed target. Grounded in the agent-neutrality direction (20260413-160358-d-stg-574): adds a neutral consumption channel without compromising the canonical format. Touches non-developer access (20260422-122523-d-stg-x0l): OKF's zero-backend static visualizer yields a free read-only browse view, orthogonal to whatever UI the runtime work builds. Value is real but contingent on OKF adoption — optionality to sequence behind the live runtime decision, not critical-path work.
