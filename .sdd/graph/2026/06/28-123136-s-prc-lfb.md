During engage, the agent read an anchored entry's summary and body — including the body's own pointer to an attached classification — and skipped loading the attachment itself, treating the pointer as the record. The body framed the attachment as "illustrative, not the full set," and the agent took that summary as license not to open it; the backfill scoping that followed rested on a premise (most directives are pending) that the skipped attachment had already settled the other way (architecture/policy directives are guiding). This is a fresh instance of the mentions-as-facts failure mode (s-prc-uq8): the pointer is treated as content and following it to the source is skipped. It is specifically a residual hole in the Inspect move (s-prc-lud): the Widen→Inspect restructure made grounding mandatory for chains and bundled IDs but does not bind on reading an anchored entry's own attachment, where "the description is the summary, the attachment is the record" should apply. Sibling to the describe-then-ID skill-discipline gap (s-prc-pkq); the fix direction is to make Inspect treat the anchored entry's attachment as part of the record to load, not optional context a summary can stand in for.
