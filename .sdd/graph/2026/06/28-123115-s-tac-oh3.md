Pre-flight gives no feedback on whether a directive's declared `intent` matches its narrative shape, except for the settled-justification check. A `pending` or `guiding` value can be assigned arbitrarily: nothing flags a `guiding` directive whose body reads as a concrete bounded task (looks pending), or a `pending` directive whose body reads as perpetual standing orientation (looks guiding). A symmetric intent-framing-consistency rubric — low/medium and non-blocking, the same family as the existing directive-reads-aspiration check — would close this so intent can't be set with zero validator feedback. Surfaced while evaluating the shipped intent attribute (s-tac-o30); grounded in the finding that instruction-carried discipline doesn't transfer across model families (s-cpt-h5c), which is why a structural calibration check rather than skill prose is the right fix.
