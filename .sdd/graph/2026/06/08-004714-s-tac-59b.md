`sdd init`'s skill-install commit fails when the embedded bundle carries OS junk. The `//go:embed all:claude` directive's `all:` prefix force-includes dotfiles — meant for a hypothetical skill-local `.gitignore`, but it also bakes in any `.DS_Store` macOS Finder drops into the source tree at build time. `sdd init` then installs that `.DS_Store`, stages it for its auto-commit, and `git add` aborts the whole commit because `.DS_Store` is conventionally gitignored ("paths are ignored by one of your .gitignore files"). Surfaced during a manual `sdd init` smoke after the charm v2 upgrade. No skill ships a dotfile today, so `all:` buys nothing.
