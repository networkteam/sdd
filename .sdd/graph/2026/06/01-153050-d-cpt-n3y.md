Build SDD's LLM access on a fork of gollm with proper retry rather than adopting the official Anthropic Go SDK. We inspected the official SDK's `go.mod` directly: it requires the full `aws-sdk-go-v2` tree (for Bedrock) and `google.golang.org/api` (for Vertex), so importing even its core client drags those heavyweight trees into our `go.sum`, `go mod download`, and supply-chain surface — grossly disproportionate for a CLI that needs correct retry on a single endpoint. This directive therefore adopts a dependency-hygiene principle — reject dependencies that pull in heavyweight transitive trees like `aws-sdk-go-v2`, even when the offending packages would not themselves compile into the binary — as binding for this decision and as a candidate to later harden into a standing contract governing all dependency choices. Applying that principle, we fork gollm and port the proven retry approach — parse `Retry-After`/`retry-after-ms`, exponential backoff with jitter, retryable-vs-permanent error classification, quiet logging — into its HTTP layer, where the response and headers are already in hand, keeping one unified multi-provider path (Anthropic, OpenAI, Ollama) with no new heavyweight dependencies. This addresses the no-retry deficiency and resolves the backoff work s-tac-a4s deferred (gollm's built-in retry having been disabled, per s-tac-a4s, to avoid batch-log flooding). Fork location, version-pinning, and the maintenance model are specified by the implementing plan.
