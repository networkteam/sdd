Stats inspection gets a dedicated `sdd stats` command — the analytics surface across both graph activity and LLM/embedding usage — kept distinct from `sdd view`, whose purpose stays surfacing live graph entries for working and catch-up. The two answer different questions from different sources: `sdd view` composes over immutable entries; `sdd stats` aggregates the per-call `.sdd/stats/llm.jsonl` sink (usage) and the graph's own creation history (activity). `sdd stats` may borrow `sdd view`'s composable pipeline design (d-tac-uww) where it fits, but ships as a separate surface and conforms to the dual-audience terminal architecture (d-cpt-mvb): a managed TTY view for humans, clean structured stdout for agents. This resolves the surface-shape question in the read-surface gap (s-tac-j4u); v1 scope and acceptance criteria are carried by the accompanying plan.
