Empirical prompt-cache behavior, measured via the new LLM stats sink: pre-flight calls cache well — two identical captures recorded a 6163-token cache_creation, then a 6163-token cache_read on the second — but summary calls register no cache at all (read and creation both 0). The cause is Anthropic's minimum cacheable prefix (~1024 tokens): the summary system prompt sits well below it, so cache_control is silently ignored, while the larger pre-flight system prompt clears the floor. Implication for the byte-stable-prefix restructure (d-tac-fah): the cache win is real for pre-flight but will not extend to summaries unless their stable prefix grows past the floor — so scope and measure that restructure against pre-flight, and treat summaries as out of cache scope.
