Bounds the scope of the rule system, correcting a wrong turn: rules are always project-local — there is no framework-rule-distribution mechanism to design. The base framework process (capture discipline, the type system, the loop) ships via bundled skills and the binary, never as rules and never via CLAUDE.md, which does not travel to projects using SDD. Rules cannot drive the base process; they layer project-specific depth on top of the shallow generic base, accumulating over time — which is the per-project customization the graph-resident-rules gap (s-cpt-k8i) was reaching for, achieved by local rules rather than by modifying the base. This dogfooding repo is not special in mechanism — its rules are project-local too — but its domain is building SDD, so they are meta-rules for building the tools and bundled skills: the output travels to other projects, the rules do not. Practical consequence for the rule corpus: build-SDD lessons (gitignore-on-new-.sdd-path, cli-reference-on-new-flag, CQRS-on-plan, skill-overshoot, pathspec-scoping) are this project's rules, whereas candidates that are really base capture discipline (body-edge consistency, topic reuse, commit-hash citation) belong in the bundled skill, not as rules. Reasoning from the rule directive (d-cpt-30v), it bounds that directive's scope — discovery and capture operate within one project's graph, with no distribution layer — and clarifies the discovery synthesis (s-cpt-smn).
