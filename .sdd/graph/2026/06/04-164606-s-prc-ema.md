Refined the /sdd skill's ref-capture guidance from a provenance-only opener ("link to what led to this entry") to a connectedness principle plus a body↔edge consistency rule. Principle: connect the entry's reasoning to the graph — wherever the body draws on, responds to, contrasts with, or leads to other entries, those become edges, with the shape depending on the entry's type and scenario rather than a fixed checklist. Rule: the entry is self-contained — every entry-ID typed in the body is one of its edges (a ref, or a closes/supersedes target), and every edge is reflected in the body's narrative; no dangling prose mention, no edge the narrative doesn't support. Two carve-outs are made explicit: closes/supersedes targets stay in their own fields (explained as retirement rationale, never repeated as refs), and universal graph-mechanics contracts named in passing stay unref'd. The gap surfaced during the perspectival-insight capture (s-cpt-dnh), whose body named two entries in prose without reffing them and nothing caught it. Edited the bundled SKILL.md (commit 4782778), rebuilt, and reinstalled (installed copy refreshed in bb58f9a); see attachment for before/after and rationale.
