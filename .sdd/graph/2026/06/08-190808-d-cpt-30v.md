Commits to making process learnings first-class, graph-resident `rule` entries — captured once, then rediscovered at the relevant phases of the working modes — and unifies the existing `contract` kind into them via an `enforcement` attribute (binding blocks, advisory surfaces). This keeps the kind set at 7+7 (rule replaces contract in place), but because the active type-system contract (d-cpt-ni0) enumerates the kinds, the change requires superseding it with a revised set — captured when the change ships, not here; d-cpt-ni0 stays in force until then. A rule self-describes when to apply, what to consider, and how to calibrate; the "when to apply" doubles as the text discovery matches against. This is the committed response to the systemic gap where changes ship without their dependent surfaces updated (s-prc-guf): every learning becomes a rule, surfaced again the next time its situation recurs. Discovery is a "widen"-style retrieval over the rule corpus at junctures (plan, implement, evaluate, close) — the skill supplies angles (feature, layer, topic), vector search returns candidates, and a sub-agent sifts them to the applicable few so poor candidates never reach the main context. This realizes the graph-resident-rules direction (s-cpt-k8i) and generalizes phase-aware guardrails (s-prc-ljg) from contracts to discovered rules, with nothing hard-pinned to a juncture so a rule can later hook into any seam. The move is text-driven for v1, structural gating a later hardening; calibration guards against the alert-fatigue that makes pre-flight over-eager (s-prc-vvd), and the retrieval seam also points at eventually replacing pre-flight's load-all-contracts path (s-tac-osb). Making the whole surface consistent — capture through discovery — is deferred to a follow-up plan with acceptance criteria; the identified surfaces and the contract→rule migration (de-risked by supersede-chain resolution, s-tac-4ly) are recorded in the attached design record ([design.md](./08-190808-d-cpt-30v/design.md)), alongside open questions on rule provenance, discovery precision, and enforcement phasing.
