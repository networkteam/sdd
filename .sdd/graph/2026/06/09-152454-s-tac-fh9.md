Live evaluation of the on-branch interactive terminal output (the d-tac-5g9 work) shows its alt-screen progress view is poor UX and diverges from the captured terminal-experience requirements (20260605-182704-d-cpt-mvb). That directive's requirement 1 calls for transient progress as a *spinner / status line*, and its per-command matrix marks `sdd search` indexing as transient with only ranked results persisting — but the implementation takes over the whole terminal with an alt-screen viewport. It is worst for search: the screen clears to a near-empty void showing only a lone `searching` spinner for ~10s (query embed + lazy-fill, with no progress signal), then a burst of indexed log lines, then it drops back and renders results — roughly 19s of jarring full-screen takeover, confirmed from a screen recording. Root cause: the plan resolved the directive's open coordinator-design question ("pause/redraw around log emission vs a dedicated log region") toward a dedicated region, and reached for alt-screen to make that region erase cleanly. Separately, the no-work summary line and the indented background-sync warning render unstyled, not yet on the CLI palette (20260609-105101-d-cpt-n0f).
