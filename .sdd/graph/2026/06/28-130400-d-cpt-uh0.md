Every reference must resolve at capture, or capture is blocked with a high-severity precondition finding — one rule for local and cross-repo refs alike. Because references point backward at entries that already exist (immutability), a ref that does not resolve is a typo or a broken edge, never a state worth persisting; cross-repo refs resolve against the connected-repo cache, fetching fresh on a miss before deciding. The forward-class kinds (surfaces, required-by) are exempt, since they point at an entry captured first. This is a standing capture-time invariant every entry must satisfy — hence guiding. Re-stated from the prior identical directive (d-cpt-ba7) under the one-time intent backfill (d-tac-9lv); the superseded entry carries the full rationale and refs.
