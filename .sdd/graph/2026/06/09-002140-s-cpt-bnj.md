Adjusting pre-flight calibration is cumbersome, and for downstream SDD consumers it is impossible. The rubrics and severity calibration are baked into the bundled skill and code, so improving them means the heavyweight loop of filing a gap, re-reading the skills, and hand-editing rubric text — and a project that merely uses SDD has no lever at all to tune pre-flight to its own work, short of forking the framework. This leaves calibration base-only, even though the scope model (s-cpt-ov7) expects projects to layer local depth on the bundled base — calibration is the one place that depth cannot reach. It is a concrete instance of the graph-resident-process-customization gap (s-cpt-k8i), specifically its "extend pre-flight validation" level, sharpened by a recurring symptom: pre-flight's ref-kind over-eagerness and oscillation (s-prc-vvd), which this session hit repeatedly. The response — how calibration becomes adjustable, including per-project — is a separate directive; this entry records the problem only.
