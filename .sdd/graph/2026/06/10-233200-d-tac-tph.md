Make pre-flight's ref-kind validation reliable by moving the deterministic work out of the LLM and disciplining what remains. Four leak instances in six days — cross-run oscillation (s-prc-pex), intra-run self-retraction (s-prc-lbv), and confidently-wrong single-run highs (s-prc-2lm) — showed that rubric-text patching does not converge, as diagnosed in the patch-loop insight (s-prc-xr9): the blocking tier was gated on a stochastic judgment that is actually a table lookup. Three structural changes, in regression-first order.

First, eval-corpus discipline: the verbatim leak transcripts already captured (s-prc-l2d, s-prc-2lm) become pinned eval cases before any behavior changes, and the harness gains N-run pass-rate assertions so flakiness is a measured number instead of an anecdote. Second, the finding schema is reordered so the model writes its reasoning before committing a severity — today severity is the first field emitted, which is the literal mechanism behind self-retracting blockers. Third, a ref-kind applicability matrix is declared in Go (ref kind × target kind × target derived status → applicable/inapplicable, with per-cell notes): an inapplicable chosen kind becomes a deterministic mechanical high with no LLM call, and for applicable kinds the matrix renders per-ref admissible-kind lines into the user prompt, keeping the system prompt byte-stable for caching. This gives the applicable-never-high guarantee (d-prc-v0h) mechanical enforcement instead of prompt-requested compliance, and settles the applicability half of the canonical-definition drift question (s-tac-uer) by making the matrix the single source for both the mechanical check and the prompt fragment. The LLM's remaining ref-kind job is the genuinely semantic part: kind-vs-body fit among admissible kinds, and desc-vs-body contradiction.

Calibration of the remaining advisory tier is revised: a kind-sharpness finding may be medium only when it cites the body phrase supporting the other admissible kind, otherwise low — a low-only cap had made the advisory ignorable, while ungated mediums historically caught zero real errors (s-tac-4h7). This revises the never-medium sharpness rule from the selection-and-calibration directive (d-prc-2is); that rule's supersession lands with implementation. The supersede-check eagerness gap (s-prc-vvd) is partially acted on: its attached oscillation evidence is pinned and measured, with disposition decided by data at close time. A mechanical withdrawal guard (post-parse retraction detection) was considered and rejected as a brittle text heuristic made unnecessary by the schema reorder and the matrix. This plan closes s-prc-pex — its proposed convergence criterion is resolved by removing the fuzzy judgment from the blocking tier — and s-prc-lbv, whose mechanical-determination-ahead-of-the-LLM direction is what the matrix implements.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The leak transcripts attached to s-prc-l2d and s-prc-2lm are pinned as eval cases and reproduce the reported failures pre-fix, covering both directions of the terminal-done shape; the debatable-medium findings attached to s-tac-4h7 are pinned as advisory-precision cases.
- [ ] The eval harness supports per-case N-run pass-rate assertions, with stricter rates on blocking-tier cases than advisory-tier ones.
- [ ] The finding output contract emits reasoning before severity across all check types, and the parser accepts the new field order.
- [ ] A Go-declared applicability matrix (ref kind × target kind × target derived status, with per-cell notes) drives a mechanical check: a chosen kind that is inapplicable yields a deterministic high finding without an LLM call; soft cells are applicable-with-note, never inapplicable.
- [ ] Per-ref admissible-kind lines are generated from the matrix into the user prompt; the system prompt remains byte-identical across captures.
- [ ] The LLM ref-kind rubric no longer scores applicability: kind-sharpness findings are medium only with a cited body excerpt supporting another admissible kind (low otherwise); desc-vs-body contradiction may remain high.
- [ ] The never-medium sharpness rule is retired: a successor directive supersedes the selection-and-calibration directive (d-prc-2is), carrying the evidence-gated medium calibration forward while preserving body-derived kind selection.
- [ ] A supersede-check oscillation case built from the per-run findings table attached to the eagerness gap (s-prc-vvd) is pinned in the eval corpus and measured at N runs post-fix; the closing done signal records a verdict — the gap is either closed as resolved by the schema reorder or re-scoped in a fresh entry naming what remains.
- [ ] All pinned eval cases pass at the agreed rates post-fix, and the full vet/test/lint gate is clean.
