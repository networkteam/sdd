Dropped the `all:` prefix on the skill embed: `//go:embed all:claude` → `//go:embed claude`. Plain embed recurses the whole tree but excludes `.`/`_`-prefixed files (Go's default), so a build-time `.DS_Store` can no longer ride into the embedded FS, get installed by `sdd init`, and abort its commit on the gitignored path. Chosen over a runtime junk-filter or a commit-time gitignore guard because it removes the cause at the single embed chokepoint rather than the symptom; 16/16 bundle files are normal (no dotfiles), and a future skill-local dotfile would get an explicit pattern instead. Verified end-to-end: built with a planted `.DS_Store`, `sdd init` installed 16 files with none present and committed cleanly. Commit ec7d2c8.
