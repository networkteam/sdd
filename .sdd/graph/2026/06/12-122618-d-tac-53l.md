Add `surfaced-by` as a ninth ref-kind — the inverse of `surfaces` — capturing on a surfaced entry that it was raised/produced by an earlier surfacer when that surfacer was written first. This makes the `surfaces` pair order-independent the way `depends-on`/`required-by` already is, but `surfaced-by` is the *backward-class* partner (edge on the produced entry, pointing back at the producer) — the opposite side from where `required-by` completed its pair. It resolves the surfaces-inverse gap (s-cpt-seg) by choosing the new-kind option over documentation-only, on the grounds the gap establishes: the backward fallbacks are lossy (`grounded-in`/`builds-on` understate "raised by") and mis-target (when the surfacer is a terminal done, `addresses` is mechanically blocked, so authors re-anchor onto a convenient live decision and spawn capture-time disputes). It extends the eight-kind vocabulary directive (d-cpt-s9j) to nine: that directive's growth principle stands — add an inverse only when the other direction has no adequate home — and this corrects its one mis-judgment, since the reverse of `surfaces` was found inadequate, not absent. The kind adds one selection boundary to police — `surfaced-by` (the target's work produced this) vs `grounded-in` (this merely reasons from the target) — pinned by an eval case so it does not regress.
