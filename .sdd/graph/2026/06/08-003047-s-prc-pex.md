Pre-flight fired a `[high]` ref-kind-inapplicable finding on a ref whose kind (`addresses`) was actually applicable to its target — an open insight — then on retry with `grounded-in` flagged `addresses` as the sharper kind at `[low]`, so the verdict oscillated across runs on the same ref-meta input. Because `addresses` is applicable to an insight (its precondition holds), the high-severity fire violated the recalibration's own rule that applicable ref-kinds can never reach `[high]` (d-prc-v0h). This is a fresh instance of the contradictory-findings-across-runs pattern (s-prc-vvd), now on the ref-meta advisory surface and after the recalibration shipped — evidence that the "applicable means never high" guarantee is leaking on the ref-kind dimension and warrants a convergence or tie-break criterion for ref-meta verdicts.
