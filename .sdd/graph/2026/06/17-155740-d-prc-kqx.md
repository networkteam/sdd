Generalize pre-flight's artifact-durability check from a fixed commit-or-attachment requirement into a "cite the most specific durable artifact the act produced" rubric. A done signal's claim is durable when it points at evidence appropriate to its act: a commit (most specific and in-band, for in-repo file/code changes), a URL (merged PR/MR, CI run, deployed page, external doc), or an attachment; and an act that changed nothing — a manual or behavioral verification ("confirmed X works in a fresh session") — is satisfied by a human participant's attestation in the body, with the playback-and-confirm step as the safeguard rather than any new machinery. "Most specific" is author guidance, not validator-enforced: the check accepts any durable artifact that corresponds to the act and must not reject a valid URL because a commit also exists, guarding against the validation rigidity that forced bypasses (s-cpt-ix1). The one hard line stays high-severity — an in-repo code or file change with no commit cited blocks; other missing-evidence cases stay advisory (medium) so the broadened rubric does not re-introduce blocking rigidity. URLs are accepted but are a weaker durability guarantee — they rot and sit outside the graph — so the rubric nudges authors to bring evidence in-band as an attachment when they can, honoring the graph-as-record pull (d-stg-3k0); a URL is the honest choice only when the artifact genuinely lives elsewhere. Builds on the three-path durability check delivered earlier (s-prc-0ka) and resolves the attestation-and-artifact facet of the evaluation-capture gap (s-prc-10m) without closing it — the marker, sibling-relation, and finding-kind facets remain open.
