A research-grounded synthesis of how rule discovery could work, reasoning from the rule directive (d-cpt-30v) and developing the discovery half it deferred to planning — candidate design with experimental parts flagged, meant to feed the follow-up plan rather than commit it. It draws on three converging literatures (LLM agent memory, IR retrieval, pre-LLM rule systems) plus our own dialogue. Rules self-describe in sections — when-to-apply (activation), what-to-consider, how-to-calibrate; because the text splitter chunks on headings, the activation section is its own embedding chunk, enabling situation-to-situation matching rather than situation-to-whole-rule. Discovery moves away from a hard structural gate (which would drop applicable-but-mistagged rules, and our topics are imperfect): semantic search over the full corpus drives recall, lexical adds identifier hits, and topic/kind/layer act only as a ranking boost rather than a recall angle that would union in irrelevant topic-siblings — fusion weight is an empirical knob at our scale. The bootstrapping gap — the agent must know which activations exist to search well — is met by an auto-generated activation index: a compact section compressed from all rules' activations, regenerated on change and injected into the skill (always-loaded), which is the tiny top-level index the memory literature recommends (MemGPT core memory, H-MEM routing, Claude Code's MEMORY.md) and a concrete instance of the graph-resident-rules seam (s-cpt-k8i), reusing the catch-up dynamic-injection pattern (d-tac-k4l). Division of labor: the outer agent (full context plus the index) compresses the situation into activation queries; a sub-agent searches activation chunks, reranks, and returns the top-n with bodies, absorbing poor-candidate noise; the outer agent judges applicability. Usage would be positive-only — a `grounded-in` ref from each resulting entry to the rules that shaped it turns heat into recency-weighted usage, rank(heat) surfacing recently-useful and rank(in-degree) the most-applied, with decay as the only demotion and no negative signals. Provenance reuses the existing dialogue-first capture discipline, prompted at evaluate/close with recurring-or-verified as the rule-worthiness test, mirroring discovery (discover at plan/implement, capture at close). Consolidation is grooming over the rule corpus, the surface the topic-grooming plan builds (d-tac-dxa); hierarchical auto-clustering stays a later accelerator since topics are already the curated buckets. Enforcement (binding/advisory) is treated as the consequence, not the matching method, which is uniform. The activation index plus the discovery/capture wiring would be the genuinely new build; the rest rides on existing primitives. The attachment carries the full followable write-up and sources.
