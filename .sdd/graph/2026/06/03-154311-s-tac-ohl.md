`Graph.DerivedStatus` (internal/model/status.go) reports an entry's closer as `ClosedBy[e.ID][0]` — documented as "the first closer by graph insertion order" — without skipping closers that are themselves superseded. So when an entry has more than one closer and the earliest-inserted one is later superseded, the derivation reports the stale, superseded closer. Observed: `d-cpt-lgs` is closed by both `s-tac-mxi` (inserted first, now superseded by `s-tac-uo2`) and `s-tac-uo2` (active, which re-closes it), and renders `{status: closed-by s-tac-mxi}` — pointing at a superseded entry. Open/closed computation is unaffected (the entry is correctly closed either way); this is an attribution defect only. Fix direction: when choosing the reported `By`, prefer a closer whose own status is not superseded — i.e. skip superseded closers; the adjacent `SupersededBy` branch uses the same `ids[0]` pattern and may warrant parallel treatment. A deeper, separate question — whether a superseded done's closure should count at all when it is the *sole* closer — is out of scope here. Surfaced while consolidating two decisions under one execution record via done-supersession.
