Added a capture-time mechanical check that blocks superseding a non-head, closing s-tac-4s7 (commit 2327fd7). When a new entry's supersedes targets an entry whose chain already resolves to a different, still-active head, pre-flight now emits a high-severity supersede-non-head finding pointing the author at the live head to supersede instead — enforcing the supersede-the-head prevention rule mechanically. Settled branches (the existing successor has since closed) are exempt, so reviving a dead chain is still allowed. Verified end-to-end on the live graph: superseding a twice-superseded entry is now rejected with guidance to supersede its live head, resolved transitively through the chain. Tests cover the block, the linear-head allow, and the settled-branch allow; the full vet/test/lint gate is clean.
