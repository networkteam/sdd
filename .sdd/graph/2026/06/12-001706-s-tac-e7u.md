The first real Codex (gpt-5.5) run against SDD's rendered skills carried the full discipline loop end to end: catch-up against the live graph, grounding via search-plus-inspect, dialogue-before-capture with playback-and-wait, re-confirmation after a mid-flight correction, full-ID refs, canonical-only participants, pre-flight, and post-capture summary self-verification. This materially nuances the prior cross-agent finding that instruction-carried discipline does not transfer (s-cpt-h5c): on gpt-5.5 the procedural loop transferred via the thin port with no structural enforcement. The ceiling is finer SDD judgment, not the loop — ref-kind precision occasionally over-reached (proposing `related` as `refines`) and one-idea-per-entry slipped (two unrelated gaps bundled into one), both the kind of thing playback and review catch. Two runtime caveats surfaced and are captured as separate gaps: Codex's sandbox first-run approvals and the gollm `max_tokens` incompatibility. This is a single smoke-grade run — it partially addresses the behavioral-baseline gap (s-tac-qy8) and the Codex-eval criterion (AC 8 of d-tac-4ln) without closing either; a rigorous baseline needs broader coverage and a second independent capture.
