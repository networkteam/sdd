The `/sdd` skill fails, most of the time, to introduce an entry before naming its ID in dialogue — the agent bare-name-drops short IDs a human reader cannot identify, treating IDs as if they carried meaning rather than as precise handles behind a description. The skill's vocabulary-register guidance permits short IDs in narrative, but in practice that licenses unintroduced name-drops; the discipline should be describe-then-ID (name what the entry is, then attach the ID) unless the entry was given context in the immediately preceding turn. This is the textual-interface counterpart to the reader-facing-surfaces insight (`20260625-143326-s-cpt-9dp`): out-of-text surfaces (hover gloss, references pane) mitigate it from the UI side, but the prose discipline stands on its own and the skill text needs sharpening to enforce it.
