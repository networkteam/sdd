Establish the terminal-experience architecture for the sdd CLI, serving two consumer modes that stay distinct: interactive TTY humans and non-TTY consumers (agents, pipes, scripts). For TTY humans a single output coordinator owns the terminal so transient progress, leveled observability logging, and styled feedback never corrupt each other or the TUI; non-TTY mode emits clean structured results on stdout and structured slog on stderr, with no progress animation or decoration. It commits to four capabilities — transient progress distinct from log lines, leveled slog routed through the coordinator, reusable styled feedback components, and a per-command UX design — and honors the CQRS contract by exposing progress/feedback reporter callbacks on command and query structs, wired to a renderer only at the CLI layer. This is the standing terminal-experience architecture every command's output wiring conforms to — hence guiding.
