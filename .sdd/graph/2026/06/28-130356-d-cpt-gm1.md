The ref-kind vocabulary is defined by principle rather than by allowed target-types, with the skill, framework-concepts, and pre-flight rubric structured to match. A kind names why a contextual, non-status pointer exists, chosen from what the entry body asserts, with `related` as the floor; kinds split into mostly-backward refs and a small forward class captured on the source with no back-ref needed; and an inverse kind is added only when the other direction would otherwise be homeless. This is the standing vocabulary every ref is chosen from and that pre-flight polices — hence guiding.
