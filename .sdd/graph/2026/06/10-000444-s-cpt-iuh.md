The upcoming rule plan — deferred from the rule directive (d-cpt-30v) — coordinates with the guiding-intent plan (d-tac-n9k) on two points a future rule-plan drafter should account for. First, the Directives read-surface split — pending vs guiding, plus the `{status: settled}` rendering and the new `intent()` filter — is delivered by the guiding-intent plan, so the rule plan's `Contracts`-to-`Rules` rename builds on that rendering work rather than redoing it. Second, the rule directive's contract-dissolution triage routes always-on commitments to guiding directives, which requires the `intent: guiding` value to exist — so that branch of the triage can only complete once the guiding-intent plan ships. The rule plan should therefore sequence after or alongside guiding-intent and carry an explicit dependency then.
