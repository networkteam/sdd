SDD's summary generator infers an entry's language from its content instead of the configured graph locale, making the multilingual language guard one-sided: pre-flight enforces the configured language on the entry body, but the summary that `sdd new` generates is unconstrained and can come out in the wrong language. Observed in external use — an English body on an `en`-configured graph produced a German summary because the body carried heavy foreign-language content (domain names, organization, domain-specific terms) and the summarizer's prompt rule says "follow the entry's language." The two sides use different sources of truth: `Preflight` receives the configured language and checks the body against it, while `Summarize`/`RenderSummaryPrompt` never receive it — the summary prompt context carries only the entry and its related entries. Because summaries are what status, list, and catch-up render, readers consume the wrong-language summary while the correctly-authored body sits unread, and `sdd summarize --all` would reproduce the drift across the graph with no human in the loop. This is a fourth summary-body drift mode beyond the three catalogued in the drift gap (s-cpt-tdp): actor hallucination, graph-positioning commentary, and identifier-casing override. Candidate fix: thread the configured language into the summarizer (mirroring the pre-flight signature) and pin the summary to it, falling back to content-follow only when no language is configured; gating the new instruction on a set configured-language keeps the rendered prompt byte-identical for default-English graphs, so existing summary hashes don't churn and no forced re-summarize is needed. Grounds in the multilingual configured-language mechanism (d-cpt-x38), whose guard this shows covers the body but not generated summaries.
