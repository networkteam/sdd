Commit to interactivity as the standard for agent–human dialogue: handbacks must build understanding and require the user to produce, not skim-and-confirm, and the agent must surface its own assumptions for correction — so the alignment check runs in both directions, not just agent-checks-user. The proven mechanic leaned on is pairing abstraction with carefully chosen concrete examples, because a concrete case is what lets a reader's instinct catch a plausible-but-wrong framing where abstraction slides by. The specific production modes, and how to scale tool use to the risk of misalignment, stay open to develop through practice. This is a standing dialogue practice refining the alignment architecture — hence guiding.
