At the lightning talk, the audience's first-contact questions were about SDD's boundary model rather than the loop or capture mechanics: (1) is the graph private or shared, and (2) is it one graph or many, and how do they connect. This suggests newcomers orient to SDD through visibility/ownership and cross-project federation before engaging with the signal→dialogue→decision mechanics. The federation question is already settled internally — cross-repository references via per-repo URL identifiers (d-cpt-v8t) — but absent from the public pitch, so surfacing the federated model could pre-empt it. The privacy/sharing question has no captured answer at all: the graph is shared git-style with no per-entry visibility model, so "is it private" currently has no story. Implication: public positioning should state the sharing/boundary model up front, and per-participant visibility may be a genuine product gap worth its own signal.
