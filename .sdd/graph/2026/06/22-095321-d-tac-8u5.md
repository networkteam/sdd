Retire the `sdd list` command, folding its filtered-listing role onto `sdd view`. This mirrors the `sdd status` retirement (20260610-000311-d-tac-oei): both consolidate hand-built overview commands onto the `sdd view` pipeline (20260506-151345-d-tac-uww) to remove parallel presenters and the drift risk they carry. Scope spans three surfaces. (1) Code: remove the list-specific stack — `listCmd` plus its `query.ListQuery`, `finders.List`, and list presenter — while preserving the shared `EntryLine` row format that `sdd view` and `sdd search` reuse. (2) Skills: rewrite functional `sdd list --kind`/`--topic` invocations to `sdd view` layouts, drop `sdd list` from the rendering-surface mentions, and remove its `cli-reference` entry — editing the bundled templates and reinstalling, never the installed renders; coordinate with d-tac-oei where it touches the same combined status/list lines. (3) Docs: the single references in README and AGENTS.md. Two `sdd view` parity gaps gate removal and must be closed or explicitly waived first: `sdd view` has no `type()` filter to replace `sdd list --type d|s`, and no equivalent for the `--missing-kind` migration helper. This closes the open enhancement gap that proposed adding tail-slice flags to `sdd list` (20260417-120309-s-tac-93s), which the retirement moots. Full reference map and per-file worklist: [migration scope](./22-095321-d-tac-8u5/migration-scope.md).
