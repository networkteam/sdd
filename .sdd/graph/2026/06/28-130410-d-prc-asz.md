Ref-kind sharpness findings are evidence-gated: a kind-vs-body mismatch may be flagged at medium only when the observation quotes the body phrase naming a different admissible kind; with no quotable anchor the ceiling is low. Body-derived kind selection stands — write the body first, then tag the strongest relationship it states, with `related` as a gated last resort. Severity for kind questions never reaches high on the LLM side; high is reserved for desc-vs-body contradiction, with precondition violations handled mechanically before the validator runs. This is the standing calibration governing how the advisory flags ref kinds — hence guiding.
