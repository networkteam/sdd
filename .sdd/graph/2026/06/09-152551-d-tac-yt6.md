Resolve the terminal-experience coordinator open question (from 20260605-182704-d-cpt-mvb) for the d-tac-5g9 work as inline rendering, not alt-screen, correcting the divergence captured in the evaluation gap (20260609-152454-s-tac-fh9). Progress becomes a small transient region — spinner plus a determinate bar plus count — that redraws in place at the bottom of normal terminal flow and is erased on completion, while leveled log lines scroll through above it as ordinary persistent output. This is the "pause/redraw around log emission" option the plan resolved the wrong way (it chose a dedicated log region in alt-screen, producing the full-screen takeover the gap records). Mechanism: bubble tea v2 inline mode (no alt-screen), with the ephemeral footer in View() and durable log lines emitted via tea.Printf, so the footer clears cleanly on quit and never scrolls. Per the directive's per-command matrix: `sdd index` emits its per-entry indexed lines as persistent scrolling logs with the footer below, clearing to the styled summary on done; `sdd search` keeps indexing transient — footer-only progress that clears, leaving only the ranked results with no indexed lines stranded above them. This sharpens plan ACs 1, 7, and 8 (which described an alt-screen scrolling-viewport view) and leaves the rest of the plan unchanged — the cliout core (log pipe, policy, recorder, reporter), the CQRS producer boundary, the launch-when-there-is-work gate, and the non-TTY/agent paths all hold. The styled summary and feedback lines also come onto the CLI palette (20260609-105101-d-cpt-n0f).
