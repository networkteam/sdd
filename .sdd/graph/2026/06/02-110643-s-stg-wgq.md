Delivered a 5-minute lightning talk introducing SDD to a conference audience of mostly web developers plus less-technical attendees; it landed well. The deck moves from the problem (reasoning lost when sessions end, state scattered across PRDs/specs/roadmaps/wikis/issues), through the core shift — one append-only graph where state is derived rather than maintained in parallel — the signal→dialogue→decision loop, the two entry types, a live `/sdd` walkthrough, the human/agent division of labour, what ships in v0.6, and a three-part forward vision (beyond the terminal, more stakeholders, self-running agents). During prep the positioning was sharpened to lead with SDD's differentiator — a confirmed record of what you decided and why, including alternatives considered, rather than a mechanical recording of what was said — drawing on the open positioning insight (s-stg-ob9) and tempered with the honest nuance that mining only ever produces candidates a human confirms.
