The pre-flight ref-kind consistency check fires debatable `[medium]` findings on correct ref-kind choices, pushing more-specific kinds that do not fit the strict vocabulary definitions. Across the four entries captured in one session (the worktree and sync work on 2026-06-01) it flagged three of four: it argued `builds-on`→`grounded-in`/`related` for a closed same-lineage target, `related`→`grounded-in` for a closed plan (but `grounded-in` is reserved for standing structure — contract, aspiration, or active directive — which a closed plan is not), and `related`→`refines`/`depends-on` for a guidance entry the directive will only later require updating (but `refines` splits lifecycle for the augmenting-directive pattern, and `depends-on` is backwards). In each case the chosen kind was defensible per the vocabulary and the suggestion was weaker, so the finding read as iteration-hunting rather than a real correction. This sharpens the pre-flight over-eagerness gap (s-prc-vvd) along a ref-kind-specific axis and provides concrete failing examples the calibration eval in the principle-based ref-kind plan (d-tac-6d4) should pin against, so correct `related`/`builds-on` choices stop producing debatable mediums. Verbatim findings plus the entry IDs to read the full bodies are attached.
