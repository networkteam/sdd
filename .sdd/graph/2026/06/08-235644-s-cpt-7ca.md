Prototype and contract-grounding evaluation that sharpens the rule design (s-cpt-smn) cheaply, without building the rule kind. (1) Mining recent done signals via a sub-agent produced clean candidate rules in the when/consider/calibrate shape — representation and one provenance vein hold. (2) The binding/advisory tier falls out mechanically: binding rules have a checkable trigger, advisory rules are judgment — "is there a checkable trigger?" is the enforcement axis empirically. (3) The dilution risk is real and writable-against: over-broad rules (trace every dependent surface) fire on everything while their specific, checkable specializations (a new .sdd/ path needs a gitignore entry) are what's useful — so the rule-capture procedure needs quality guidance favoring specific+checkable over broad, rules about writing good rules. (4) Topic reuse is already handled by the existing capture discipline; the mining sub-agent invents labels, but real capture researches existing topics, so it is not a new concern. (5) Rules should fill enforcement gaps, not duplicate checks already enforced elsewhere (pre-flight, the staging rule). (6) Provenance has two veins — capture-at-close (live) and mine-retroactively (batch, overlapping the mine-move gap s-prc-p6q); gap signals are a second source, and gaps that ref done signals ("doing X surfaced gap Y") are the richest seeds, but surfacing them needs ref-graph traversal current queries lack, for which the planned refs-of filter (d-tac-jqq) is the closest. (7) Grounding the seven active contracts showed `contract` was overloaded, and it dissolves cleanly into two existing homes by one discriminator: does the entry fire on a situation, or is it always-on background? Situational constraints (CQRS-on-plan, regression-test-on-bugfix, placeholders-on-external-eval, conversion-on-format-break, supersede-on-entry-change, and each per-kind structural requirement such as plan-needs-ACs) become binding rules. Always-on non-situational commitments — principles like structural-separation, and the bare kind-enumeration of the type-system schema — become guiding directives (d-cpt-cyx, intent guiding), which stay supersedable (the path by which the rule kind itself gets added). So the old kind-system schema decomposes: its per-kind requirements become binding rules, its enumeration a guiding directive, with code still enforcing the set as today. This preserves the directive's 7+7 framing (rule replaces contract; guiding directives add no new kind) and yields a crisp rule-vs-directive boundary on activation presence (d-cpt-30v). The eight candidate rules produced (attached) are the seed corpus and test fixtures, since the rule kind does not exist yet.
