Re-home the surviving merge-pull half of the worktree work that the scope directive (d-cpt-927) retired along with the bundled implementation plan. With worktree lifecycle out of the CLI, three items from the old plan are deliberately dropped — the content-equivalence `Brancher.BranchMerged` check, its orphaning-scenario regression test, and the `WIPMarker` base-branch field — because they existed only to guard CLI worktree teardown, which no longer exists. What remains is the background-sync correctness work that stands on its own merit: a sanctioned merge-pull so background sync never rewrites the shared graph's history, and rewriting the rebase-shaped conflict guidance (s-prc-jpx) to a merge shape. This realizes the still-active sync-strategy directive (d-tac-4ff), whose merge-pull half the scope directive flagged as surviving.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `sdd sync --pull` exists: on a clean working tree it runs `git pull` with merge (never rebase); on a dirty tree it refuses and defers with an actionable message.
- [ ] The `/sdd` skill's sync-reaction guidance invokes `sdd sync --pull` instead of instructing a raw `git pull --rebase`.
- [ ] The rebase-shaped conflict-resolution guidance (s-prc-jpx) is rewritten to a merge shape: resolve once, conclude with a commit, no per-commit continue.
- [ ] `go vet ./...`, `go test ./...`, and `golangci-lint run ./...` are clean; skill changes are made in `internal/bundledskills/` and the installed `.claude/skills/` copy is re-stamped.
