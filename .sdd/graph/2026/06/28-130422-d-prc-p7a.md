When a directive carrying active `refines` refinements is superseded rather than closed, those refinements go stale and must be resolved deliberately: either fold the refinement's content into the successor and close it (preferred at supersede time, and it shrinks the graph), or supersede the stale refinement with a fresh entry re-anchored on the successor — promoted to a standalone directive when the successor no longer covers the refined area. Doing either automatically is rejected: an auto-sweep closes refinements that may still be valid, and an auto-repoint mis-anchors when scope has shifted. This is a standing graph-lifecycle rule applied at every supersession — hence guiding.
