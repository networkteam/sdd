Refines the `sdd show` command surface from a single `--max-depth` (int) plus a boolean `--downstream` into two independent depth flags — `--up N` (upstream/grounding, default 2) and `--down N` (downstream/consumers, default 1) — with downstream now shown by default and `0` turning a direction off (`--up 0 --down 0` renders the primary entry alone).

The split is grounded in how the two directions behave. Upstream is an entry's grounding chain and reads well at depth 2 — the full "why" in 7–16 nodes on well-connected entries, where depth 3 roughly doubles the volume and depth 4 walls at 27–55. Downstream fans out far faster: a hub contract reaches 16 immediate consumers and 69 at depth 2, so it defaults shallow at 1. Both defaults were verified empirically against the live graph before being set.

This sharpens d-tac-z3k's show surface — its acceptance criteria assumed the prior `--max-depth` / `--downstream` shape; the child-level truncation behavior they specify is unchanged, only the flag surface and the now-shown-by-default downstream. Skill references were swept to match: `/sdd-explore` and the engage playbook widen past the defaults (`--up 3 --down 2`) to pull more of an entry's surroundings into view, the implementation playbook reads `--down 2` for the augmenting-directive chain, and groom, the release skill, and `cli-reference` (including its stale output-format section) were updated.
