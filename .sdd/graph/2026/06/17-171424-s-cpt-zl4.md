A sweep of the active graph shows topic coverage on active work is effectively complete: only 2 untagged active non-done entries (a process contract and an operational fact) against 167 untagged done signals. Capture-time tagging quietly did the job — the active gaps, questions, directives, plans, and aspirations that drive topic navigation already carry topics. So the topic-backfill premise behind the grooming directive (d-tac-nbp) and the /sdd-groom-topics plan (d-tac-dxa) is largely moot: there is no meaningful backlog of untagged active work to hand-tag. The real shape of any remaining grooming work is therefore narrower than backfill — done-signal topic derivation (d-cpt-b2x) and drift-label consolidation (gated on the rename/merge command, s-cpt-k1b) — not the untagged-backfill that d-tac-nbp and d-tac-dxa were framed around. Supersedes the prior capture of this finding, which closed with a prescriptive "rescope accordingly" instruction: an insight surfaces the implication; the rescope itself is a decision for whoever drives the topics focus.
