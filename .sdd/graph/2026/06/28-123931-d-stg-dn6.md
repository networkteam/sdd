SDD's core design — graph format, CLI abstractions, skill structure, and primary distribution channels — must remain agent-agnostic, with no single-agent assumptions baked into the foundations; the MVP feature surface may target Claude Code alone, because the neutrality discipline applies to what is expensive to change later, not to cheap-to-add agent-specific features. This is a standing constraint shaping every distribution, integration, and skill-authoring decision rather than a commitment with a completion point — hence guiding. Re-stated from the prior identical directive (d-stg-574) solely to record intent under the one-time intent backfill (d-tac-9lv); the superseded entry carries the full rationale: the cheap-vs-expensive-to-change framing, the distribution-research grounding, and the agent-platform scope boundary.
