A sweep of the active graph shows topic coverage on active work is effectively complete: only 2 untagged active non-done entries (a process contract and an operational fact) against 167 untagged done signals. Capture-time tagging quietly did the job — the active gaps, questions, directives, plans, and aspirations that drive topic navigation already carry topics. So the topic-backfill premise behind the grooming directive (d-tac-nbp) and the /sdd-groom-topics plan (d-tac-dxa) is largely moot: there is no meaningful backlog of untagged active work to hand-tag. The remaining topic-coverage levers are narrower than backfill — deriving done-signal topics from edges (d-cpt-b2x), and consolidating drift labels (e.g. catch-up-scaling into skill/catch-up, dev/ux into cli/ux), which needs the label rename/merge command (s-cpt-k1b). Re-scope d-tac-nbp and d-tac-dxa accordingly: a groom-topics surface should target drift consolidation and the done-derivation, not the untagged-backfill it was framed around.
