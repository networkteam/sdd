The mechanical close-validation (`validateCloses` in internal/model) forbids any non-done signal from using `closes`, which blocks the fact/insight → question *dissolution* path the framework explicitly designed and documents. A dedicated `dissolution.tmpl` pre-flight check exists for "fact or insight closes a question" — designed in the two-type redesign (20260420-113731-d-cpt-e7i) — and the retirement-primitives table in framework-concepts lists dissolution as the canonical low-ceremony way a question is answered into mootness. But the mechanical gate runs before pre-flight, so that template is unreachable for its intended case and the validator contradicts the framework's own design. The friction surfaces when a finding simply answers an open question: capture is forced into a directive (which falsely asserts a decision was made) or a done signal (which falsely asserts execution work), instead of letting the fact dissolve the question — the shape that matches what actually happened. Fix direction: relax `validateCloses` to allow a fact or insight to close a question, mirroring the existing carve-out that lets a directive retire a contract or aspiration. This sits alongside the insight-target closure inconsistency (20260608-004727-s-prc-4kh) and is a cousin of the born-done ceremonial-closure friction (20260603-172849-s-cpt-r8s).
