Catch-up's "active and hot" block under-surfaces unacted-on commitments along two distinct dimensions, and the intent fix it was pinned to covers only one. Dimension one (from the superseded gap): the block filters to plans and activities, so a fresh directive that redirects a work area — the workflow-MCP experiment turn (20260609-234656-d-cpt-afn) — never appears, expected to close via the intent pending/guiding split (20260610-000354-d-tac-n9k). Dimension two (newly isolated): an entry that *does* pass the kind filter — a plan or activity — still sinks, because heat counts only incoming refs and a freshly captured entry has none, so it ties at zero and the result cap drops it. The intent fix cannot reach dimension two: intent is directive-only, and even for directives it changes which entries are *eligible*, not how they *rank* — a zero-heat fresh directive still sorts last and falls off the cap. Empirically isolated on a downstream project: a just-captured plan was absent at check-in, then jumped to second place the moment one inbound ref landed, proving inbound-ref count and not relevance governs visibility. The carrying question — what have I committed to and not yet acted on — needs its own surfacing mechanism, not closure-by-association with the intent work.
