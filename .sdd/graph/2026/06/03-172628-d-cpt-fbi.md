SDD adopts an intake model for bringing external input into the graph: issue trackers, email, and chat are transport, and an autonomous agent reacting to channel events auto-captures each incoming item as a new `intake` signal kind rather than guessing it into a gap/fact/decision. The point of doing this agentically rather than dumping raw input mechanically: the intake entry arrives already connected to neighbours, topic-tagged, analyzed, and summarized — so it is searchable and usable in dialogue instead of dead raw material. An intake entry carries provenance (a generic channel + source — email address, sender name, URL, document title) and, as its primary payload, the agent's reasoning: why this shape might fit, why these connections, why these topics — the alignment-check a human reacts to when a grooming session converts the guess into durable entries. Candidate connections ride as `related` refs and candidate topics as inline `topics:`; the proposed kind/layer stays in prose, never machine-readable fields, so the kind keeps doing its real job — refusing premature classification. Promotion is by dialogue, not edit: distilled entries are captured through dialogue and `ref` the intake for provenance, and any one of them may close it — the simplest case is an intake decided to be a gap, where that gap closes it directly (the same shape as a fact dissolving a question). What matters is that the close is deliberate, not automatic on the first entry: triage follows a short ladder by complexity — a single entry (gap, fact, or decision) closing directly; several entries with a done signal as the deliberate closer and completeness record; or, for complex input, a direction-setting directive or activity that closes the intake and carries its own downstream work to a later done signal — noise is dismissed by a closing done signal (terminal, no lingering decision). The intake stays open — a visible open end — until that deliberate close lands, so a session that captured only part of the planned shape never silently disappears; the moment of closing is the completeness checkpoint. Intake entries live in the graph (not a parallel store, honoring d-stg-3k0), are held out of normal catch-up, and surface as a triage count feeding a dedicated grooming flow that works across every surface — dashboard, chat agent over MCP, or local agent — via select-then-start-a-session. The intake-processing agent runs a dedicated prompt that should draw on the generalized/graph-resident prompt direction (s-cpt-z7l) rather than being bound to one runtime. This directive sets the shape; follow-up work adds the kind and its frontmatter, the close-path rules (deliberate close; any distilled entry may close, with a done signal for multi-output triage), the presentation surfaces (status, list, show, catch-up exclusion + count, pre-flight rubric, skill rendering), and the autonomous channel-reactive intake agent itself. Chosen against alternatives weighed in dialogue: in-graph over an outside store, reasoning-prose over a structured candidate-shape field, and a deliberate close over auto-closing on the first distilled entry — the latter would silently drop planned-but-unmade outputs.
