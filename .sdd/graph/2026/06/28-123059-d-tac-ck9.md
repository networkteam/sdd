Collapse the five per-kind active-decision helpers — `Directives`, `Activities`, `Plans`, `Contracts`, `Aspirations` — into thin `Graph.Filter(GraphFilter{Type: TypeDecision, Kind: …, OpenOnly: true})` wrappers, so the active-set definition (closed, superseded, settled, role-cascade) lives in `Graph.Filter` alone. Chosen over a single `ActiveDecisions(kind)` accessor because the named helpers read clearly at call sites (e.g. pre-flight's `graph.Contracts()`). The duplication surfaced while adding the settled-directive exclusion to both `Filter` and `Directives` during the intent work — the next status-affecting concept should be added once, not six times.
