Rewrote the `viewHelpText` constant in commit 5e42e4d to enumerate the full implemented `sdd view` vocabulary — adding the `source()` section, the `participant`, `untagged`, `id()`, and `not()` filters, the `name-prefix` output modifier, the `as-counts`, `as-participants-block`, and `as-wip-list` render terminators, and the `participants` and `wip` macros — and refreshed the `expand`, `group`, and example sections to match. The built-in help printed by a bare `sdd view` now agrees with both the binary and the cli-reference skill doc, and a sync reminder was added to the constant's doc comment so the next added primitive lands in the help too. This closes the help-drift gap (s-tac-vil): a reader can no longer misread the help as evidence of an older binary, since the help and the implementation are back in agreement.
