The sdd CLI's styled (TTY) output uses a single color scheme keyed to concept, so every command's human-facing rendering reads consistently: each concept maps to one color and prominence is carried by weight (white-bold, white, body grey, faint) rather than by adding hues. Color lives only on the styled TTY path — it downsamples to the terminal and strips entirely for a non-TTY writer or NO_COLOR — and inactive (closed or superseded) entries recede wholesale so live ones draw the eye. This is the standing palette every styled surface conforms to — hence guiding.
