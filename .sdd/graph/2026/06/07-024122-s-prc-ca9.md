Skimmable output isn't enough to keep agent–human dialogue honest: engagement is enforced only by making each party produce rather than passively receive, and this holds in both directions. Passive output, however clear, invites skim-and-confirm ("looks plausible"), and that confirmation is hollow — the user nodded, they didn't decide — which quietly defeats the framework's premise that the record is confirmed. Agent→user: the handback must build understanding and demand a produced response. User→agent: the agent must state its own assumptions for correction, or it only appears aligned — the agent's own nodding (taking the first plausible answer) is the same failure from the other side. Reached through the teaching lens: you can't enforce engagement by explaining better, only by requiring production. A tidier "two kinds of failure" framing was considered and dropped — it's one thing, alignment breaking, not two axes.
