The summary-generation prompt (summary.tmpl) instructs the summarizer to describe an entry's `supersedes` relationship as a primary relational sentence, on equal footing with refs and closes, with no guidance that supersession can be bookkeeping subordinate to the entry's own substance. For a superseding entry whose point is its content — a re-statement, or a supersede solely to stamp an attribute — this foregrounds "supersedes X" over what the entry actually commits to. Observed during the intent backfill: a guiding re-statement was summarized as a restatement of the prior directive and an artifact of the backfill activity, rather than as the agent-neutrality commitment it carries. The overview surfaces (sdd view, catch-up) lead with the stored summary, so the bookkeeping crowds the lede readers actually consume. This is distinct from summary-body drift (s-cpt-tdp): the summary is faithful to the body, so it is an emphasis/instruction defect, not a fidelity divergence. It is a concrete instance of the prompt-hardening the summary-fidelity tooling left open (s-tac-233). Fix direction: instruct the prompt to lead with the entry's substance and treat supersession as subordinate context — naming it only when the relationship is itself substantive, and omitting or de-emphasizing it for re-statements and attribute-stamps.
