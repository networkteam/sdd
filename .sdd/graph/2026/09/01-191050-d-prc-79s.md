A choice settled in dialogue may be recorded inside the done that closes the gap, the short loop, when the done's body names that settlement and its participants; the kind knowledge and the pre-flight short-loop calibration must carry this rule so a drafting agent knows to state it and the gate reads stated provenance as the loop working rather than a smuggled decision. The rule holds because the short loop exists to avoid ceremony: when a gap pre-names the candidate remedies and dialogue picks one, a separate decision entry would restate that dialogue, and what the record owes is provenance, not a second entry. Today nothing carries this: the done authoring fact (20260812-170000-s-prc-dnk) ends its choice rule at 'capture the decision first', the gap authoring fact (20260815-100000-s-prc-gpk) reads a choice on the way to closure only as a skipped decision, and the short-loop pre-flight template blocks every approach-shaped done at tactical layer with no reading of provenance. The follow-up realizing this directive: extend both authoring facts and recalibrate the short-loop template with the settled form, and verify whether the per-kind checking facts the writing guide consumes (20260811-184407-d-cpt-x7a) need the same, folding it in if so. The worked example is the capture of 20260901-185333-s-tac-ckx, blocked at first playback and passing once the body named the dialogue settlement.
