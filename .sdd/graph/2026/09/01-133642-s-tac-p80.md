Judging 50 of this graph's stored summaries against the summary contract failed 29 — uniformly across template eras, so the bar is genuinely new and no generation of summaries was ever aimed at it.

The run covered the outer lens only — validation: is the commitment right, judged in use — measuring the day-old contract (20260901-112834-d-cpt-75a) against real data rather than the synthetic eval cases. Instrument: a wild-sweep harness (commit 2d12bad7) drew a deterministic, kind-stratified sample of 50 stored summaries from this graph and had the fixed judge compare each against the full rendered entry with its neighbors, axis by axis per the contract's own wording; mechanical checks ran beside it. Ballpark failure distribution: lede not standing alone or stuffed — roughly half the sample; ungrounded assertions — about a quarter; inflated or vague wording — about a fifth; relationship coverage and terseness — a handful; the [attached report](./01-133642-s-tac-p80/wild-sweep-report.md) carries the exact counts, the per-month split, and all 29 failures verbatim.

Judgment: the contract is validated — it discriminates on real data with concrete, endorsable reasons, and it covers five of the six failure classes the wild state shows. The uncovered class: detailed figures — CLI commands, token counts, durations — sitting in summaries whose bodies own that detail; its ready form is the entry-craft criterion's figure discipline (20260810-121912-d-cpt-7zr) applied to the summary, stricter because a summary is a pointer. Two mechanisms surfaced beyond the axis counts: positioning language transfers from neighbors' summaries into new generations through the prompt's related-entries block, so drift feeds drift; and tense inflation, a plan summarized as work already done.

Two implications follow from the measurement, marked as reasoning: template tuning alone cannot repair the stock, since summaries regenerate only on demand; and blind bulk regeneration would reintroduce exactly the drift measured here, so regeneration is only meaningful judged against the full entry and the rubric — run in batches with tuning between them, the per-batch failure rate becomes the tuning's measurement on real data. The dispatch acting on this is its own entry. Inner-lens verification of the contract's eventual implementation stays open for a later run.
