SDD lacks a durable graph-change subscription extension point that decouples downstream concerns from the write side. Christopher raised the need for SDD and its hosts to plug in independent consumers, such as search index maintenance and notifications, with changes fanning out to each despite crashes or downtime. Inspection of `pkg/application/graphstore.go` and `pkg/application/transition.go` found post-apply callbacks with recorded outcomes, but no subscription delivery mechanism.
