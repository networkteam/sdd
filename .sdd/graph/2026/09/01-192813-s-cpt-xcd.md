The difficulty of placing an entry among activity, plan, and pending directive is structural: the three are presented as sibling categories but do not share one axis. Activity and pending directive differ in what the entry binds, an act versus a shape the project must take, while plan differs from both in degree, how far the outcome is fixed in advance. A category choice and a degree choice folded into one pick is why placement took dialogue rounds even for the framework's designer, the episode recorded in the gap this reading stands beside (20260901-191943-s-prc-fwo).

This graph's usage bears the reading out: 249 directives, 102 plans, 28 activities. The discrimination knowledge rules that ambiguous cases stay directives, because a why lost crossing into a dispatch cannot be recovered (20260818-120000-s-prc-dsc), and that rule predicts exactly this distribution: directive absorbs the boundary, so the placement cost is paid at every capture while activity's discriminating benefit rarely cashes in.

The system has resolved this shape once before: the contract kind was retired into directives with guiding intent, a parameter absorbing what a sibling kind carried (20260812-180000-s-prc-typ). Exploration, not commitment: if placement still takes dialogue after the binding axis lands in the kind knowledge, the taxonomy rather than its documentation is the problem, and the deeper cut, plan and activity as directive-family variants under a binding parameter, becomes worth weighing against its migration cost across hundreds of entries, tooling, and base facts; the plan kind's steady use argues it earns its keep either way.

The reading is held for the territory question the gap leaves open, deliberately not acted on until the axis fix is in and its effect observed, which is also what would firm this synthesis beyond one lived episode and a usage count.
