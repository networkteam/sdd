Branch-correct vector search still requires reading attachment contents to verify indexed entry versions, even when synchronization is disabled. Review of 20260905-112352-d-tac-nhu exposed this remaining cost for remotely stored attachments. The desired behavior is incomplete results under eventual consistency, confined to the selected branch snapshot, without fetching attachment contents during search. The snapshot and index currently lack sufficient version information to establish that match without hashing those contents.
