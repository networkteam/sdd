Entry-version indexing, shared embedding batching and SDD-owned search coverage were merged into main and pushed in adbd49ba, completing 20260906-121218-d-tac-ccm.

PR #9 reviewed commits 0af4e1d7, 07383c65 and ad7cacbd. Discovery streams whole-project or selected entry requirements with continuation bound to revision, configuration and selection. Indexing reads exact retained sources and atomically publishes complete entry versions, including zero-chunk entries. Unpublished rows remain unsearchable; completed siblings survive retries and interruption.

Search selects authorized home and dependency snapshots before ApplicationOptions.PrepareSearch lets the consumer schedule, index or wait. SDD then derives published coverage and retrieves against those same sources. Ordinary wait expiry makes no completeness claim, and preparation and lazy read failures propagate. Shared batching validates all inputs before incremental admission, isolates cancellation and shutdown, and composes existing query routing, provider deadlines and usage observation.

AppliedMutation.AffectedEntryIDs supplies selections for write-triggered discovery, including document deletions and attachment owners. Consumers establish durable exact-source retention before scheduling, potentially using a finalized Git revision rather than the workspace revision. Scoped discovery rejects malformed IDs, skips valid absent or ineligible entries and surfaces unreadable selected sources. Snapshot acquisition preserves the authorized branch.

The [validation record](./06-165355-s-tac-hjq/entry-indexing-validation.md) maps every acceptance criterion to evidence, including executable local/external-consumer composition, publication and batching regressions, checkpoint-cost measurements and persistent real-provider interruption tests. Provider settings remain provisional. Consumer adoption guidance is in `pkg/application/doc.go` and its Go examples; validation evidence is retained with this entry.
