The `sdd` Homebrew cask declares its macOS quarantine-removal hook as a legacy `postflight` Ruby block, which Homebrew has deprecated. `brew update` prints `Warning: Calling postflight is deprecated! Use postflight_steps instead.` for the cask, as reported in PR #25.

A cask in the tap should install without deprecation warnings and keep working once Homebrew drops the legacy form. Homebrew's Cask Cookbook keeps legacy flight blocks only temporarily for third-party taps and expects the declarative `postflight_steps` stanza.

GoReleaser writes the block from the `hooks.post.install` field in `.goreleaser.yaml`, deprecated since GoReleaser v2.19 in favor of `hooks.post.install_steps`. The hook has been in the cask config since the release pipeline shipped (20260418-101837-s-tac-r8l).
