Two mechanical validator quirks around actor supersede force needless overrides during identity capture. First, superseding an actor entry to correct it is blocked by an actor-canonical-reused finding: the write-once-across-chains check does not recognize that a supersede stays within the same identity chain, so a legitimate same-canonical correction cannot pass without override. Second, after an actor is superseded, the role-refs-missing-head check demands the stale original actor head rather than the current active head, so every role bound to a corrected actor fails validation until overridden. Both fired repeatedly in a live brownfield run, each requiring a user override and compounding the actor/role capture friction. Fix direction: treat a supersede as staying in-chain for the canonical-reuse check, and resolve the required role head to the active head of the actor's supersede chain. Surfaced by the outer evaluation of the bootstrap port (20260722-140955-s-tac-lgc).
