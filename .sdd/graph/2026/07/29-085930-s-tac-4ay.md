The directive-closes-any-decision rule is implemented in commit `209c8e3` and proven on the live capture that needed it: 20260729-085604-d-tac-wgc, closing two active directives at once, wrote cleanly with no pre-flight findings, where the identical capture had been blocked by two high findings before the change.

**What changed.** `model.validateCloses` no longer restricts which decision kinds a `kind: directive` decision may close, and the remaining warning is re-aimed at the closer rather than the target: a plan or activity closing a decision now reads "only a kind: directive decision may close another decision — use supersedes instead". The doc comment above the function, which stated the narrow rule as though it were the contract, is corrected to match. The settled-directive guard and the signal-side restrictions are untouched — a settled directive still cannot be closed at all, and non-`done` signals still cannot close except a fact or insight dissolving a question. Tests moved with the rule: directive→directive and directive→plan are now valid cases, plan→contract still rejects under the new message, and the existing plan→procedure rejection in the procedure suite still holds unchanged.

**Every surface the directive named is delivered.** Pre-flight's `unusual_close` rubric gains directive-closes-any-decision as a standard pattern, with the old stable-kind line split so the signal case (fact, insight) stays distinct, and its no-finding enumeration follows. `framework-concepts` widens the retirement-rationale requirement past stable kinds and states the distinction the rule turns on — a done signal says what was delivered, a retiring directive says why nothing will be. The groom playbook's stale-entry instruction widens the same way. Installed skill renders for both agents carry the new text, refreshed separately in `aad5dc8`.

**Verification.** `devbox run build`, `go vet ./...`, `go test ./...` and `golangci-lint run ./...` (0 issues) are clean, and `sdd lint` against this graph reports no issues across 1130 entries — so widening the rule flagged nothing already recorded. The load-bearing evidence is not any of those: it is the live capture, run against a rebuilt binary on a fresh server, which is the confrontation 20260726-204112-s-prc-ogp requires rather than a constructed declaration. What makes it evidence rather than a demonstration is that the same capture, with the same fields, had genuinely failed minutes earlier — and the two directives it retires now derive `closed-by` correctly, so status derivation followed the widened rule without further change.

**Not verified, deliberately.** The pre-flight LLM calibration for the widened rationale requirement was not exercised: the eval suite is slow and paid, and was not run. The mechanical gate is proven and the rubric text is updated, but whether the LLM judges retirement rationale well on a directive→directive close is untested — the first real instance of that judgment will be its own evidence.

The retirement rationale for closing the directive is simply that its commitment is met: it demanded exactly this alignment, named its own surfaces, and every one of them shipped, so it is closed by delivery rather than retired unbuilt.
