Does log-only material suffice to faithfully resume a parked dialogue? The session log was designed as trajectory evidence and forensic record, not as a dialogue record: on resume the agent receives the fold — drafted state fields, step position, verbatim chooser words — but none of the reasoning between turns, which lived only in the previous agent's context. For capture-shaped procedures this is probably survivable, since the drafted body carries most of the material; for interview-shaped procedures the gap between what the log holds and what the dialogue built will be wider. The premise that parked dialogues should be inspected and finished (20260703-002223-s-tac-kd2 shipped exactly that resume path) rests on this holding. Empirical, not designable in advance: it needs a real inspect-and-finish attempt on a genuinely parked dialogue — one with substance, not a false start — during the dogfooding the v1 workflow engine plan runs (20260702-220449-d-tac-ry0).
