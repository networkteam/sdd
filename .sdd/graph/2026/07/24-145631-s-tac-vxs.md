Changing an established `repo_id` from A to B leaves machine-global sessions under A undiscoverable because no stable rendezvous records the previously active store. Implementation review of 20260722-112853-d-tac-ln1 showed that the confirmed in-tree and `local/<stable-root-hash>` transitions remain detectable from their computable old location, while sessions created directly under repository-ID key A have no equivalent breadcrumb once configuration selects B. The A store remains intact and new B sessions are non-destructive, so this does not block the scoped session-locality slice; however, detection, user-facing availability behavior, and any future A-to-B migration policy remain unsettled and require their own dialogue rather than an implementation-time registry, mutation block, or generalized rekey state machine.
