Engine-mode dogfooding shows the widen-depth problem is not capture-specific: in the first live catch-up run the main agent composed the briefing and a drill answer entirely from injected lanes and two search results — zero show calls in the whole session — and the explore worker's evidence was accepted the instant it was reported: inspect, compress, and completed landed in the same moment, with seven inspectedIds and a four-iteration widenReport that no log can corroborate, since free reads are untracked and the worker's context is discarded. Evidence is currently recorded, not verified — resolution-checked at most. This widens the widen-depth fabrication gap (20260703-142356-s-cpt-be9) in place, from capture's assemble step to all engine-mode composition, and sharpens its candidate fixes: injection makes the anchor surface unskippable, but everything beyond injection still rides trust; verification would need the shell to track reads per instance or gate on citations of served material.
