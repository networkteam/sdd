The trace simultaneously validates two Slice 5 shipments (20260719-015148-s-tac-khq) in live outer evaluation of the session-model plan (20260718-180550-d-tac-e3x): the door payload (19,096 chars) fit under the foreign host's budget, confirming the 25KB contract's calibration, and the fullReplay escape was exercised in the wild — the agent recovered the full position and composed a faithful briefing. A harness without that capability cannot recover this way, because the replay payload is as oversized as the serve it recovers — small-by-construction beats recoverable-by-cleverness.
