Slice 1 of the session-subsystem rebuild (20260730-093011-d-tac-k4q) is delivered in commit `210087a9`, and with it most of slice 2 — the two were inseparable, since both stores were built on the containment apparatus being removed. Sessions now resolve across the known locations and append where resolved, behind one filesystem store, one `os.OpenRoot` containment and one publish helper; the relocation machinery the plan lists for deletion is gone entirely. Suite, vet, lint and build clean.

Two deliberate deviations, both revising 20260729-082254-d-cpt-4tx rather than drifting from it. The compatibility standard is raised: every log format sdd has released must stay readable in every location it has written, carried by a test, where the directive had scoped permanent read-compatibility to graph entries and left session logs best-effort. Its separate acceptance — a session under a superseded identity key is simply not found — stands. The mechanism is replaced: the retirement table shipped in 20260727-091833-s-tac-d9n, its strict decoder and `RetiredSessionMetadataFields` are deleted rather than wired into the read path, which never reached them; lenient decoding with per-line format dispatch carries both directions instead, needing no registry kept in step with the model. That also retired file-level format classification and made a pre-0.16 log appendable in place, where it previously returned a migration error.

One defect fixed on the way: `ListWorkflowSessions` aborted the whole listing on the first session it could not decode, so a single log from a newer binary would have hidden every session.

The plan is not closed. Outstanding: slices 3 and 4, the branch-precedence consolidation, port enumeration and deletion with the conformance suite over them, and on the acceptance bar the live binary swap and the collection run against a real store. Reads are proven against this machine's real accumulated store; both legacy locations are empty here because relocation already ran, so released-format coverage rests on `local/session_compat_test.go`.
