Example dialogues are produced by live-driving real sessions with scripted personas — script the hypothesis, run the actual stack, feed the persona turns in reaction to the agent's real output — with fabrication demoted to a gap-filling fallback under strict fidelity rules. The pilot proved the need the hard way: four review rounds hardened a fully fabricated bootstrap dialogue (script-binding, provenance, a blind audit), and it still read unauthentic — authorial omniscience writes characters that draw on knowledge they cannot have (two leaks reached human review; a story-blind audit caught a third in an entry summary), and even leak-free fabrication lacks the texture only the real machinery produces. Christopher then drove the real thing — a genuine bootstrap session against a scratch project, feeding the fabricated dialogue's persona lines largely verbatim where they fit and adapting live where they did not — and the split verdict is the method's justification: the persona script survived contact (the playbook half works as drive material), while the decisive authenticity came from the agent side that fabrication had failed to imitate — pre-flight friction leaving a real supersede chain in the graph, the skill's own plain-metaphor formulations doing their job, and an honest loose-ends handoff. The method, in production order: (1) Scenario playbook — script what we think the dialogue could look like from the personas and setting (knowledge limits, input modality, time pressure) with the beats the docs need; the script is a hypothesis for the persona side, never agent lines. (2) Real-run production — drive the actual agent + skill/engine + CLI stack, supplying persona turns one at a time from the script, composed or adapted in character against the agent's actual previous output; the dialogue and the graph slice both come out real, so agent-side script-binding and provenance hold by construction. Until bootstrap lands in engine mode (20260716-144333-s-prc-7ku), bootstrap-family scenarios run through the /sdd skill flow and adapt to engine sessions once ported — accepting a temporary register mismatch with the engine-first docs stance (20260715-175731-d-cpt-p3k). (3) Curation — condense the raw transcript into the docs transcript format (turns plus typed action blocks); condensing is allowed, inventing turns is not; product miscalibrations a run surfaces are captured as signals and preferably fixed at the source rather than cosmetically trimmed. Real-run graphs stamp production time; where a scenario's fiction needs story-time, re-stamping is a mechanical post-pass (rename plus ref remap). (4) Blind verification — a story-blind context audits the curated artifact against only legitimate in-session sources, since curation is where leaks and drift re-enter. Where fabrication remains necessary, the pilot's rules bind it: every agent turn traces to a concrete step of the skill or procedure demonstrated (per-turn trace annotations), every agent-used fact has an in-session source, and captured entries must be derivable from the dialogue. This instantiates the validation-separation contract (20260412-004028-d-cpt-vt1) in content production — the acting author cannot self-certify freedom from leaks — and refines the example-world production directive (20260715-183740-d-cpt-jrt): its fabricate-deliberately route inverts to real-runs-first, while its fidelity invariant, story-time coherence, and validation-by-real-tool commitments stand.
