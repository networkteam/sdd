The relocation directive chain is retired unbuilt: both the acknowledged offline session-store migration and its broadened identity-transition trigger are withdrawn, because 20260729-082254-d-cpt-4tx replaces relocation with read-side resolution and nothing moves any more.

The retired pair committed a mechanism rather than an outcome: an acknowledgement prompt gating an aggregate move, an identity rewrite performed in the same offline pass, a tombstone marking the abandoned location, and — in the refinement — one offline mechanism covering both transition shapes, a bounded routing hold for the declined case, and resumability from a durable manifest. Every one of those obligations presupposes that session material must arrive somewhere new intact. Under the read-side model it does not: sessions are found where they already are, events append to the log that was resolved, and a session that cannot be found is an accepted cost of the transition rather than a failure to repair. With nothing to move there is nothing to acknowledge, nothing to rewrite in a pass, nothing to tombstone, and no routing to hold.

They are closed rather than superseded because no successor occupies their scope. Supersession would imply a replacement mechanism at the same tactical altitude; what replaced them is a conceptual decision that removes the mechanism's reason to exist. Their delivered code is deleted by that rebuild rather than carried forward in a new shape, so there is no continuing commitment for a successor to hold.

Both are closed in a single entry deliberately. The second refines the first, so retiring the first alone would leave an active refinement anchored to a retired target — the stale-refinement condition 20260628-154636-d-prc-rec exists to prevent, and which it insists be resolved deliberately rather than by automatic sweep or automatic re-anchoring. That rule is written for the supersession case, so it applies here by extension rather than by letter; closing strands a refinement just as supersession does. Neither of its two remedies fits: there is no successor to fold the refinement into, and re-anchoring it would carry forward a question — which transition shapes trigger an acknowledgement — that has no counterpart in a design with no acknowledgement. So both are retired together, with the rationale stated once.

This entry is settled on arrival. Its only work is the retirement; the work that replaces the retired mechanism is already committed in 20260729-082254-d-cpt-4tx and will be planned, delivered and closed on that line, so nothing further follows from this decision itself.
