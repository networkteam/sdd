Catch-up should be restructured from one monolithic pushed lane injection into a two-layer shape — a pushed brief lead sheet plus perspective-driven pulled expansion from composable view building blocks — making serve size bounded by construction and the briefing adaptive to who is being briefed. Today the compose step injects six uncapped lanes as the agent's entire input (~50KB in live measurement, middle-truncated by Codex's tool-output budget — the gap this answers, 20260719-122103-s-tac-jom); a harness-independent fix cannot rely on recovery cleverness, since the Codex fact (20260719-122547-s-tac-40d) establishes ~10K tokens as a realistic per-response floor and marks replay-sized payloads as equally degradable. The two layers: (1) the engine pushes a lead sheet — the most interesting leads, brief-rendered and hard-capped, guaranteed to survive any host budget by construction; (2) the agent expands through served, named view building blocks — composable layout queries it runs itself via the view tool, with freedom to choose and combine them — gaining robustness (each response individually small) and flexibility (agency over which threads to deepen). Deliberately rejected: engine-side verification that the agent ran specific queries — no micro-management machinery; the user junction that ends every catch-up remains the quality gate. The perspective dimension is the design's core rather than an add-on: catch-up must cater to different actors, roles, and perspectives on a project — a business owner's check-in is not a developer's six lanes — so the agent sets a perspective at the start (naturally defaulting from the participant's role in the graph) and composes the building blocks to serve it, which is how catch-up begins to realize the non-developer-reach aspiration (20260422-122523-d-stg-x0l). The design is host-neutral by construction: building blocks are served queries against the engine's view capability, exactly the operation shape the host-neutrality directive requires (20260707-134311-d-cpt-476), and their usefulness leans on the view tool's agent-facing usability line (20260717-095714-s-tac-lwi). It replaces the current compose contract of the catch-up procedure (20260703-194500-d-prc-cat) — "the lanes below are your entire input" — with "these building blocks are your input space", preserving snapshot grounding while dropping the size cliff. Open design work: what "most interesting leads" means per perspective (needs its own dialogue), where building blocks live (procedure-spec lanes vs. base facts), and how the lead sheet stays capped as the graph grows. The interim mitigation — `brief` plus `maxBytes` caps on the existing lanes — is recorded with the gap and is compatible with, not a substitute for, this restructure.
