The base-fact index test suite (20260721-152753-s-tac-31o) leaves a handful of load-bearing behaviors unpinned and couples upper layers to exact error prose, even though its overall strategy is sound and behaviorally anchored at the right layers. The unpinned edges: `Graph.IndexedFacts` checks lifecycle status before validating enrollment, so a malformed index on a superseded or closed fact is silently skipped — no test pins that ordering, and a refactor swapping it would start hard-failing on retired malformed facts unseen; the intentional divergence between the structural `indexed` view filter and the validated shell index has no test documenting it, so a well-meaning "consistency fix" to either side would pass CI; the optional-collect-field commit path in `Answer` and the `topicRaw` round-trip branch for unparseable topics are unexercised. The coupling: the exact validation message ("control or line-separator") is asserted in five test files across model, engine, application, and mcpapp — defensible defense-in-depth per seam, but every wording tweak becomes a five-file edit; upper layers should assert that rejection happens, not the model's phrasing. The first two edges are partially dissolved by the remediation direction recorded on the strictness-split finding (20260722-120425-s-tac-u22) — valid-enrollment selection removes the divergence — so whichever lands first should carry the tests. Surfaced by the testing-strategy lens of the inner evaluation (20260722-115503-s-tac-a0o).
