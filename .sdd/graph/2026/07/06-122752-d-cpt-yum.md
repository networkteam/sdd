On done signals, inline `topics:` denote **work-shape** — the kind of work that concluded (`implementation/frontend`, `architecture/database`, `evaluation/outer/user-feedback`) — not subject-matter, which a done recovers from the entries its refs point at. Work-shape reuses the existing topic mechanism (hierarchical, multivalued, extensible, stable labels, component-prefix matching); no new frontmatter field is added. The move is sound because a done is semantically distinct — it records concluded work, and its subject is derivable from its refs — so its inline topic slot is free to carry the work axis. The evaluation lens rides this convention as `evaluation/<lens>[/specifics]`: `evaluation/inner` (verification — sound under the project's guidelines) and `evaluation/outer` (validation — the right thing, works in use). Evaluation coverage then becomes computable — "has this work been outer-evaluated, by anyone?" is a component-prefix match over the done signals referencing it plus a ref walk — sharpening the abstract "work × axis" of the evaluation-as-done model (s-cpt-yfp) and supplying the inner/outer review-marker convention the evaluation-capture gap flagged as missing (s-prc-10m). It is complementary to the derived-done-topic rule (d-cpt-5nn), which governs derived subject topics from the `closes` edge, not inline topics. One edge is deferred: a done that closes nothing — an evaluation-done builds-on the work it judges — must have its subject derived by following the references of the appropriate kind, not the `closes` edge; noted for whenever done-subject derivation is built, not decided here. The convention feeds both Slice B procedures: evaluate proposes `evaluation/<lens>` on the evaluation-done, implementation proposes `implementation/<...>` on its closing done — so it is decided once here rather than living in one procedure's prose. Alternatives weighed (a dedicated field; a ref-based sibling-linking convention) and the treatment of existing dones' inline topics are in the attached note.
