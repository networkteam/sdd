Transport-level connection events disappear from the durable session log: the log records participant acts on the dialogue only, keeping the session handle as the sole correlation between an agent conversation and an sdd session. The session-model contract (20260718-131626-d-cpt-9of) demoted the MCP connection to transport, yet the cause vocabulary it shipped kept narrating that transport into the session record: `AttachmentCause` mixes acts on the dialogue (`conclude`, `abandon`, `claim`) with connection events (`disconnect`, `shutdown`, `switch`) that the server appends when a pipe drops or the process stops and that nothing reads. The slice-2/3 delivery (20260731-080621-s-tac-jkw) surfaced the cost concretely: defining "ended" required an explicit cause filter, and on a real accumulated store most sessions' last attachment record is a socket closing — connection lifecycle sitting inside the dialogue record, re-coupling one append at a time what the contract decoupled.

The commitment: nothing transport-shaped is ever appended to a session. A dropped connection may clear the live attachment stamp so the session stops looking held — deleting live coordination state, a promptness optimization — but it writes no history. "Ended" becomes the presence of a terminal participant act, conclude or abandon, with no cause filter. Displacement is derived by comparing a caller's attachment against the current live stamp rather than read from a `claim` record. With the transport causes gone and displacement derived live, the append-only attachment history has no reader left and goes entirely: the durable record of a session is its dialogue substance plus one terminal ended-record, and the live stamp (client, recency, the user's authorizing words) remains ephemeral coordination state that never enters the durable narrative.

Serving sdd remotely is what makes this a principle rather than tidiness. Over HTTP there is often no disconnect event at all — half-open TCP, load-balancer idle resets — which is exactly why liveness is derived from activity recency; an event the design must already work without cannot be load-bearing, so recording it is pure narration. A hosted dialogue spans many reconnects, so appending them turns the session log into a correlation table of client identities and connection lifetimes; a server redeploy would stamp `shutdown` into every tenant's open session at once. Operational narrative belongs in the server's logs, and a composition that needs an access trail keeps it at its own boundary, where user identity is actually known. This re-scopes the contract's cause-auditability expectation — "every lifecycle change records its specific cause" — to acts on the dialogue, and it is what the scaffolding calibration (20260727-224047-d-cpt-u8o) already implies: an append-only history of past attachment stamps, kept indefinitely and read by nothing, is record-grade bookkeeping about scaffolding.

Slice 4 of the rebuild plan (20260730-093011-d-tac-k4q) implements against this directive, which sharpens its "ended" definition in place — no cause filter, just the terminal record. Folding the vocabulary change silently into the slice was rejected because the vocabulary belongs to the session-model contract, not the plan.
