Converting an already-articulated evaluation finding into a captured entry took ~11 minutes and ~10 tool calls in the third dogfooding run — the user had named the finding in one sentence, and the path to playback ran through two full grounding ceremonies and two rejected starts. The evaluate move's assess step demands a widen pass (searches plus full inspection recorded as evidence), and the capture it then spawns demands its own re-grounding widen at assemble — two widen passes for one finding whose substance was already on the table. The rejected starts include a concrete spec defect: engage takes its anchor as a mid-move report field and rejects it as a start param, while evaluate requires anchor as a start param — adjacent moves with opposite contracts for the same concept. Candidate directions: hand the parent move's grounding evidence through to a spawned capture instead of demanding a fresh widen, align the anchor contract across moves, and a lighter path for findings articulated at a junction.
