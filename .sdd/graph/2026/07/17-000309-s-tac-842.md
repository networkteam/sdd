The connected-store force-repair gap is closed — and had in fact been fixed for a week without a closing record: `sdd index --repo <id>` / `--all-repos` with `--force` rebuilds member repos rather than silently downgrading to lazy-fill. BuildConnectedIndexesCmd carries a Force field (internal/command/index.go), the CLI threads --force through to it, and TestBuildConnectedIndexes_ForceRebuildsMembers passes on main. The fix landed in commit 5d30e8c (2026-07-09, the cross-repo release-readiness code batch) but the gap stayed open; the omission came to light during slice 2 of the index repair (20260717-000200-s-tac-eyc), whose amendment had scoped this fix as a work item that turned out to be already delivered. Closure is by verification of the existing commit and test — no new work was needed.
