After a session concludes, the engine serves no path forward: `start_session` on the same connection re-serves the just-concluded session — status `completed`, no pending step, no instruction — while the one call that does work, starting a move, silently returns that session to `running` and is named nowhere.

**The episode.** A session concluded normally, with three entries landed and two small graph-hygiene items still owed. Asked to do them, the agent called the door twice; both calls returned the handle it had just concluded, `completed`, with no pending step and nothing indicating what to do next. It read the connection as stranded, declined to write into a concluded session — the correct instinct on that information — and reported that a fresh agent session was required. The user's own guess, "can't you start a new procedure maybe?", unstuck it: starting a move on the completed session succeeded and flipped it back to `running`.

**Two defects, and the second is the worse one.** First, the door did not open fresh. Door-always-opens-fresh is the first of the four lifecycle rules settled in 20260704-000621-d-tac-bfc and carried forward explicitly by the live session-model contract 20260718-131626-d-cpt-9of; a connection whose session has just concluded is precisely where that rule earns its keep. That same contract's first invariant fixes a dialogue's identity to its durable handle and demotes the MCP connection to transport plus payload cache — yet what the door answered with here was the connection's spent binding, which is the connection acting as the dialogue's identity.

Second, `completed` is not the terminal state it presents itself as, and no serve says which reading holds. A move revives it. Either the state means the session is over — and then the door must open a new one — or it does not, and then the serve must carry the continuation. Today it means neither, so an agent reading the response correctly has no basis for the recovery that actually works and must guess. This is a served-instruction gap as much as a behavioural one: the conclude path of 20260704-100000-d-prc-dlg is the deliberate way out of a session, and it says nothing about what follows it.

**The fix must not be shaped as a reset.** Door-opens-fresh governs the no-handle case only — a door call carrying no session opens a new one — and must never be widened into a rule that re-entry re-initializes. Attaching to a session by its handle carries the opposite obligation: 20260719-124405-d-cpt-0tm requires durable, intent-bearing procedure state to project faithfully into the re-entry serve, so a pending step must come back at its current position with the state and schema needed to continue it, never restarted and never cleared. Three cases must therefore be distinguished, and only two of them are settled today: a door call with no handle opens fresh, an attach by handle resumes in place, and a move started on a concluded session — the behaviour observed here — is neither, which is precisely why it needs deciding rather than falling out of an implementation.

**The cost is friction, not lost work.** Nothing was destroyed — the session's three entries were already committed, and the two owed captures were still owed afterwards. What it cost was a stall in the middle of ordinary work: the agent stopped, declared the write path unreachable, reported that a fresh agent session was needed, and restated the two owed items as prose for whoever picked them up next — and getting moving again took the user's guess rather than anything served. 20260727-224047-d-cpt-u8o requires session scaffolding to fail cleanly and always reach a defined end state without human archaeology; here the end state was reached and then quietly was not, and the convergence that followed came from outside the engine.

**Where this belongs.** 20260729-082254-d-cpt-4tx rebuilds the session subsystem and already makes conclude a retention trigger — collect ended sessions together with their staged state — so the post-conclude lifecycle is a question that rebuild must settle rather than inherit: what `completed` means, whether a move may revive it, and what the door does for a connection holding one. The revival path is itself in tension with reclamation, since a collectable ended session that a move can bring back to life is a lifecycle worth deciding deliberately rather than discovering. The minimum this gap asks is that one of the two be true and served — the door opens fresh on a concluded session, or the concluded serve carries the way on — without either answer costing a pending step its state.
