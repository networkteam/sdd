Ran the throw-away goal-driving working-mode spike end to end and recorded its findings. A skill-free agent, driven only by a stateful stdio MCP server that holds an SDD capture mode and hands one goal at a time, completed the real capture discipline with no skill content across three worker postures (compliant, corner-cutting, skip-everything). Friction matched the s-cpt-yfo prediction: the deterministic hot path stayed sub-5ms while a single LLM check at the capture transition carried the only real cost, and the agent's own turns dominated wall-clock. Structural sequencing held where instruction discipline had eroded — a skip-prone worker was refused until it produced well-formed report-backs — but a human-driven run exposed that the loop, lacking a confirmation goal, drove straight to capture around the user. Closes the enforcement spike (d-tac-tjw); the loop it instrumented is s-cpt-yfo and the guest-surface principle it tested is s-cpt-1dz. See the [findings](./02-141108-s-tac-du6/findings.md).
