The implementation plan (20260712-234500-d-tac-x1m) also requires a temporary external-module release smoke without a local replace and a published consumable version, but the external example and evaluation both consume the worktree through a replace. Completion therefore needs the real end-to-end identity/mutation proof, a published no-replace smoke, and an honest reconciliation of the missing slice evidence before closure can be claimed.
