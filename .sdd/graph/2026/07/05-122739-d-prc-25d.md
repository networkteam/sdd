While the workflow engine's base procedures are under active design, shipped procedure entries are revised in place — same entry ID, updated body and frontmatter in the embedded source — rather than superseded; supersession is reserved for released procedures, and there only when a change goes beyond simple wording. During the design phase the binary is the single source and no project has customized a chain yet, so supersession chains would add ceremony to a surface still finding its shape; after release, when projects may hold customizations, the retirement table's supersede path applies so downstream chains stay traceable. Slice A set the precedent by revising four embedded specs in place; this directive makes the convention deliberate rather than accidental.
