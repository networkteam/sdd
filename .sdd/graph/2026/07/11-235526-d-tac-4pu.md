Engine-native rule discovery becomes a mandatory, evidenced procedure checkpoint that selects project rules by stable work shape and artifacts rather than by procedure or step identity. This refines the graph-resident rule plan (20260622-084244-d-tac-eho) for the shipped move-instance architecture (20260702-174833-d-cpt-3yw): relevant steps declare work-shape topic prefixes, agents report situation sentences plus proposed, changed, or reviewed paths, and the engine mechanically narrows the corpus before the host model judges semantic applicability against activation.when.

Each checkpoint follows Discover → Inspect → Apply. Discover serves the complete eligible set slimly—ID, enforcement, and when text—rather than a stateful delta; Inspect serves requested rules in full and deduplicates bodies by ID plus content hash; Apply records applied or contested rules and their rationale. The engine retains selector match traces for diagnostics and validation, explicitly deactivates rules that cease to be eligible, and reconsiders discovery only when the effective eligible ID/content set or the semantic activation input changes. Work contexts reuse hierarchical, component-prefix-matched work-shape topics from 20260706-122752-d-cpt-yum, avoiding a parallel context taxonomy.

The engine validates rule evidence, not unseen work. Procedures require a self-describing account of why and how each applied rule was followed, with relevant artifact paths; missing evidence or an admitted binding-rule violation can block, while an independent judge checks specificity, coherence, and graph-anchored claims without pretending to inspect unavailable artifacts. This applies the evidence-by-construction boundary of 20260702-141247-s-cpt-icg and the independent-validation constraint of 20260412-004028-d-cpt-vt1.

Whenever applied rules shape a capture, the captured entry carries their evidence and grounded-in refs, so existing heat and decay become rule-usage statistics as the owning plan intended. The full schema, serve lifecycle, invalidation semantics, and implementation surface are recorded in the attached design. Base-procedure delivery remains host-neutral per 20260707-134311-d-cpt-476; the prior skill-injected index and optional sub-skill path are replaced rather than duplicated.
