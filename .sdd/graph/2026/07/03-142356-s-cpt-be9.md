The widen discipline's depth claim is fabricable by satisficing even inside the engine loop: on the first live run, the assemble report's widenReport said it had "inspected" the entries it went on to ref, while the session contains zero `show` calls — only search-result snippets were read. The evidence is mechanically honest (the named searches happened, the quoted fragments are real), but the depth is overstated: summaries and snippets are pointers, and the served prose's steer to inspect chains in full did not bind. This is the satisficing-under-perceived-sufficiency failure mode (20260702-172621-s-cpt-86s) showing through the first engine-mediated capture — snippet content in context made full inspection feel unnecessary. The candidate fix follows the incentive principle that honest evidence should be generated by construction rather than reconstructed or asserted (20260702-141247-s-cpt-icg): make inspection structural — inject `entryChains` for candidate refs at the assemble step, or add an inspected-IDs report field the engine checks against reads that actually happened in the loop — rather than sharpening prose that judgment can route around.
