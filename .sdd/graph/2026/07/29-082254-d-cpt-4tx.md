The engine's session subsystem is rebuilt from the base on a read-side compatibility model: nothing relocates, nothing is migrated, reclamation replaces migration, and the care formerly spent guaranteeing session state is redirected to the graph write path.

**Why a rebuild and not the proposed refactor.** 20260726-195959-s-tac-wg5 measured the migration and relocation core at four to five times its warranted weight and proposed deletion and consolidation with the decided behaviour left unchanged. The behaviour is the problem. 20260727-224047-d-cpt-u8o classifies sessions as scaffolding whose life is that of an agent conversation, and 20260727-230506-d-cpt-msg withdraws the very primitives the machinery is built from — the recovery state machine, rollback, whole-graph compare-and-set, atomic multi-document apply. Refactoring toward a design whose primitives no longer exist is incoherent; the excess is not decoration on a sound structure. Concretely, `local_sessionrelocate.go` (3,025 lines) and `local_sessionmigration.go` (2,453) are roughly 5,500 of that ~6,900, and both exist to preserve session data across a store move — record-grade apparatus wrapped around something explicitly declared not to be a record. The seven items of 20260726-195959-s-tac-wg5 are not dropped; they land inside the rebuild instead of ahead of it.

**Compatibility is a read property, and it is best-effort.** Sessions are found where they already are: resolution looks across known locations, and events append to the log that was resolved — which needs no home policy, because writing where you read leaves nothing to reconcile. Older on-disk shapes are tolerated and converted through the versioned retirement table shipped in 20260727-091833-s-tac-d9n, which becomes the whole of compatibility rather than one part of a migration. If an old session is not found, it is not found: during the transition a session may appear absent, and that is an accepted cost rather than a defect to engineer against. This is what answers 20260724-145631-s-tac-vxs — sessions under a superseded identity key are not discovered, by acceptance rather than by breadcrumb, registry or rekey machinery. Read-compatibility remains permanent where it was ever promised: for graph entries, not for session logs.

**Nothing moves, so the moving machinery goes.** This retires the mechanism of 20260723-192701-d-tac-g7r — the acknowledgement prompt, the aggregate move, the identity rewrite in the same pass, the tombstone — and with it the broadened trigger and bounded routing hold of 20260724-123026-d-tac-vhk. Deleted with them: the two independent transaction engines, the per-payload state machine, quarantine-then-finalize source deletion, rollback of already-byte-verified publishes, the resumable manifest, the symlink-rebinding ancestor-pinning apparatus, the parallel rooted and path-based store implementations, the Windows-only build files, and the store-routing policy engine living in `cmd`. Both retired directives are active and intent `pending`, and the second actively refines the first, so their formal retirement is a deliberate act performed together — not a sweep, and not left implicit in this entry.

**Reclamation replaces migration.** 20260728-004924-s-tac-gtx records that nothing reclaims session logs or staged-blob retentions and that the port cannot express a sweep at all — Stage, Stat, Open, Retain, Release offer neither enumeration nor deletion. A rebuild fixes that for free by designing the port correctly rather than bolting a sweeper onto one that cannot carry it. One janitor does two jobs at one defined trigger: collect ended sessions with their staged state, and sweep pending declarations toward convergence as 20260727-230506-d-cpt-msg requires. A session with a pending declaration is not collectable — the single point where the two subsystems genuinely touch, and already anticipated by that contract's rule that grooming skips whatever an in-flight declaration still claims. Retention is a decision and is settled here rather than left to implementation: collect on conclude, plus the catch-up sweep for sessions that die without concluding, with no grace window and no archive unless a concrete need is named. Reclamation is also what makes old locations drain — the same mechanism, no special case for the transition.

**What survives, and where the care goes.** The semantics delivered by 20260722-112853-d-tac-ln1 stand: the declared session branch binding, and the machine-global per-project store keyed by repository identity. What does not survive is its relocation mechanism. The redirected care lands on the graph write path, which is the thing that must never lose a confirmed entry: the convergence contract of 20260727-230506-d-cpt-msg, implemented on the single command-and-handler write path that 20260721-151122-s-tac-spe shows does not yet exist — a dependency, since encoding those preconditions into either of today's two parallel surfaces would build them twice.

**Alternatives weighed and rejected.** Behaviour-preserving simplification, for the reason above. Eager acknowledged migration kept in some reduced form: rejected on blast radius — an all-at-once sweep gives a single unreadable payload the power to stop the entire run, which is what happened against the real store and what left the per-payload resilience clause of 20260727-091833-s-tac-d9n open, whereas reading only the session actually being resolved confines that failure to that one session. The invisible acknowledgement prompt from the same episode is deliberately *not* cited as evidence here: it was a terminal rendering defect that would have appeared in any design carrying a prompt, and attributing it to eager migration would be the find-something reasoning this entry commits to resisting. Copy-on-resume: rejected because copying while an older binary may still be appending is the one way to fragment a single session's log. A fixed-home policy: considered and discarded as redundant, since appending to the log you resolved achieves it without a rule. A breadcrumb or registry for superseded identity keys: rejected in favour of the accepted cost.

**The discipline this rewrite owes.** A greenfield has no test pressure, which is exactly where the compounding recorded in 20260726-200257-s-prc-cbl re-enters: individually reasonable defensive additions accreting into machinery nobody decided to build. Every defensive addition in this rebuild is accepted only against an explicitly stated threat model and a proportionate size estimate — the check whose absence produced the mass being deleted. The plan that decomposes this must state its criteria as outcomes observable from outside, so that its own claims can meet reality rather than only review.
