Base-fact discovery indexes are derived from active fact entries carrying nested `index` metadata, never from fact IDs hard-coded into the session shell. This refines the base-facts pull-layer directive (d-cpt-dtv) and addresses the first-call discovery failure (s-prc-e2v): adding, superseding, or project-overriding a fact updates the opening index through ordinary graph resolution, while the shell remains a small pointer surface and never duplicates fact bodies.

The frontmatter shape is:

```yaml
topics:
  - engine/base-facts
  - cli/view

index:
  title: "How to compose graph views (view tool): layout grammar, filters, ranking, quoting, and examples"
  topic: cli/view
```

`index` is valid only on `kind: fact`. Both keys are required. `title` must stand alone as an agent retrieval cue—enough to understand when pulling the fact will help, not merely a terse name. `topic` selects exactly one primary future grouping and must also appear in the entry's ordinary `topics`; it is a presentation selector, not duplicate classification. Presence of the object enrolls the fact.

The shell derives the list after active and supersession resolution and renders each item with its full canonical entry ID and title, avoiding ambiguity in this machine-facing handoff. The initial list may remain flat; the single index topic preserves deterministic grouping later without duplicating entries. `sdd show` exposes the metadata. Special rendering in `sdd view`, catch-up, and skill surfaces is explicitly outside this directive unless later evidence establishes a need.
