Should cross-repo entry resolution accept short or unprefixed IDs and automatically search all declared dependency repos, or stay strictly prefixed? Surfaced by the outer validation 20260709-160634-s-tac-tt8, where `sdd show`/`sdd view` traversed and listed cross-repo refs and resolved a foreign repo's entry by its full prefixed ID, but a short ID (or an unprefixed full ID) resolved only against the local graph — a foreign short ID did not resolve. Auto-searching declared dependencies would improve ergonomics (no need to know which repo an ID lives in), but trades against the explicitness prefixing buys under the cross-repo design (20260606-003344-d-cpt-s6q), needs a cross-repo collision/ambiguity rule for when the same short ID exists in more than one repo, and adds a multi-repo lookup cost. Open question, not a defect — recorded so the resolution-ergonomics decision is made deliberately rather than by omission.
