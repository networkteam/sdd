A goal-driving working-mode loop that does not enforce dialogue-presence as a structural invariant routes around the human. In the spike, an agent driven only by mechanical goals — ground, draft, capture — ran straight to capture with no play-back-and-confirm turn, so the user never shaped or approved the entry. This empirically validates the workflow-agent design's listing of dialogue-presence among the non-negotiable base invariants that rules may extend but never remove (s-cpt-r57), and shows the dialogue-first guardrail (d-stg-beb) cannot ride on instruction inside a goal loop: human confirmation must be a first-class goal that suspends the loop and hands control back to the user.
