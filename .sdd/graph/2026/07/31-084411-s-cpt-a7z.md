How should the graph draw the boundary between meta-observation about SDD and work on SDD as a product, so the distinction becomes operative rather than merely noticed? The structural cause of the circling is recorded (20260731-083147-s-cpt-j2w), but recording a cause changes nothing about how the two mix, and no mechanism for separating them exists.

The problem has two halves and they appear to need different answers. **Distinguishability** — nothing in the record marks which side an entry sits on, so meta-observation reads as product work and is indistinguishable in any view, count, or briefing. **Friction** — in an ordinary project, meta-work is a digression and that cost is what stops it becoming the work; here it is legitimately in scope, and nothing supplies an equivalent brake.

Only the first half has even a partial candidate. Topics can carry an axis other than subject-matter: on done signals, inline topics denote work-shape (20260706-122752-d-cpt-yum). But that convention rests on a done's subject being recoverable from its refs, which frees the slot — reasoning that does not transfer to a gap or an insight, whose subject is not derivable. What could transfer is weaker: topics are multivalued, so a meta label could sit alongside subject labels on any kind. Whether a label nobody is compelled to apply constitutes a boundary at all is precisely the uncertainty.

The friction half has no candidate. It is also unclear whether it needs one, or whether making the split visible — a countable ratio of meta to product output over a period — supplies enough feedback on its own. That is a hypothesis, not a design, and it is the first thing worth testing.
