SDD's evaluation model treats the two lenses as **generic postures** whose concrete meaning comes from the project, not the framework: **inner is verification** — is the work sound, judged against the project's own guidelines — and **outer is validation** — is it the *right thing*, working in use and serving the people it's for. What each lens concretely checks depends on the work type (code, docs, design, stories), which the project defines through its own guidance (`AGENTS.md`/`CLAUDE.md`) and, later, rules — the framework bakes in no code-specific assumption. Evaluation is **real work** done between reading the chain and recording: the agent inspects and runs (host work) and gathers human feedback where judgment is needed, part-automated and part-human, with a human attestation counting as evidence. It is **per-lens and partial** — an evaluation may cover one lens or both, and different participants or runs cover the rest — so two-lens completeness ("a pass on one is not a pass") is a **coverage property computed across the graph**, not a gate inside a single run. This develops the evaluation-as-done model (s-cpt-yfp), whose per-lens coverage is made computable by the work-shape encoding decided separately (d-cpt-yum), and resolves the inner/outer-review-structure and human-attested-verification facets of the evaluation-capture gap (s-prc-10m). It is realized by the reshaped engine evaluate procedure (scope then carry-out then record) and by correcting `evaluation.md`, whose current definition is code-leaning and frames inner as "sound on its own terms" — the self-contained reading this model rejects. Rejected: forcing both lenses in one evaluation (collapses the coverage axis and the partial case), and baking code-specific lens meaning into the framework (SDD is a generic process).
