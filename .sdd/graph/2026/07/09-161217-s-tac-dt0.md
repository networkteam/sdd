`sdd lint` should verify that every cross-repo reference prefix in the graph resolves to a declared dependency, turning resolve-or-block from a capture-time gate into a standing graph invariant. Surfaced by the outer validation 20260709-160634-s-tac-tt8. The resolve-or-block invariant (20260628-130400-d-cpt-uh0) is enforced only at capture, so it cannot catch violations arising afterward: entries captured before the declared-dependency precondition shipped, a hand-edited `dependencies` list, config drift, or a dependency dropped out from under existing refs (the dependency-lifecycle gap 20260709-161133-s-cpt-3wt). A check that collects the distinct target repo-ids across all entries' cross-repo refs and confirms each is present in `dependencies` closes that loop — the third enforcement leg alongside the capture-time gate and the ref-safety guard on the redefined `repo remove`. Cheap, local, near-shippable; it slots into `sdd lint` beside the existing index-fingerprint check.
