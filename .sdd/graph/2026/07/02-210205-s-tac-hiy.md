The `sdd index` and search lazy-fill progress bar does not honestly track indexing: it reaches 100% before the work is done, gives no indication of what is being processed during the wait, and can land on the wrong total — presenting as a hang once the bar fills.

**Advances on batch start, not completion.** The counter advances at batch start (`OnBatchStart` → `reporter.Add`, `handler_index.go:240-242`), before the synchronous `EmbedDocuments` round-trip (`:288`) and the following upsert / manifest save; nothing advances it on completion, and the footer (spinner + bar + count) carries no label naming the current work. So the bar fills the moment the final batch's embedding begins, then sits at 100% with no feedback for the whole call — worst when all pending entries fit one embedder batch, where it jumps straight to 100% at once. There is no deadlock (the program waits for the work goroutine before quitting), so this is a perceived hang caused by progress being both decoupled from completion and silent about what is in flight.

**The total can be wrong.** The bar total comes from `manifest.PendingCount` (fingerprint-based) while the indexer's skip check also considers the content hash (`handler_index.go:161`), so the sum of increments can fall short of the total (bar never reaches 100%) or overshoot it (`Ratio()` clamps to 1 early).

**What good looks like:** the bar advances as batches *complete*, the footer names what is being embedded right now, and the total reflects the work actually performed. Live-status granularity is bounded by the embedder — a batch is one atomic `EmbedDocuments` call returning all vectors together, so status is available at batch granularity (the IDs handed to `OnBatchStart`) but not per-entry mid-call without embedding one entry at a time and losing batch throughput.
