Engine serves are oversized and the cost lands on the model turn, not the wire: in the first catch-up dogfooding run the compose serve measured 44.9KB (six lanes injected as full entry lines with ref sub-lines) and produced a ~98-second model turn before the briefing, the framing block measured 21KB, and a single drill pulled 26.5KB of search results for another ~34-second turn — while every server round-trip stayed under 0.4 seconds. The perceived slowness of engine mode is context ingestion of injected payloads, not transport. Candidate directions: a payload budget per serve, leaner lane rendering for the compose injection (tighter counts, compact line format, fewer sub-lines), a trimmed framing layout, and paging or summarizing large injections.
