Move-anchor bare-ID resolution is served by the free show read, not by loosening the engine's strict anchor field type. The bare-ID resolution decision (20260709-171105-d-cpt-ete) names move anchor resolution as one of three surfaces where a bare ID (short or unprefixed-full) must resolve, alongside the sdd show CLI command and the show MCP tool. Those two read surfaces now resolve a bare ID against the flat union of the local graph and its declared dependencies. The anchor surface reaches the same outcome without any engine change: the agent resolves a bare ID through the free show read — always available, never gated — and anchors the move on the returned full canonical ID.

The engine keeps its anchor field strictly typed as a full, local entry ID. That field type is shared with the closes and supersedes lifecycle fields, which must stay full-ID and within-graph — a lifecycle edge never crosses the repo boundary — so it cannot simply be loosened. Making the anchor field itself accept bare or foreign IDs would have required a distinct field type, a relaxed validation schema, a normalization hook to canonicalize the value on report, and edits to the embedded base-procedure specs: a high-blast-radius change to the engine core that drives every session, traded for a convenience whose workaround is a single free read. Keeping the field strict also keeps ID-resolution logic in one place — the union resolver behind the read tools — rather than duplicating it into the engine's field layer. The served procedure and skill guidance therefore directs the agent to resolve a bare anchor through show before anchoring, rather than promising the engine will accept it directly.
