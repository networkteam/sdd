Supersede-aware ranking shipped in commit 377af716 on branch `worktree-heat-supersede`: `sdd view` now resolves every inbound reference to the live head of its supersede chain, damping each hop's heat contribution by 0.8, so a superseding entry inherits the attention its predecessor earned instead of starting at zero. `NewGraph` builds an `InboundRefs` index alongside `RefsTo` — the same inbound edges resolved through `Graph.ResolveRef`, each carrying its hop distance. `HeatScore` reads it and multiplies by `SupersedeHopFactor^hops`; `InDegreeScore` reads the same index undamped, because a fractional count has no meaning; coldness and the `mult`/`add`/`log` composites inherit both through in-degree with no rules of their own. This closes 20260729-111657-d-cpt-x6z by delivering it, not by retiring it — every clause of the rule is implemented, including the parts that fall out rather than needing code.

**One implementation-shape call, made at build time.** The directive named "the `RefsTo` keying in `NewGraph`" as a site, but `RefsTo` has three non-ranking consumers — annotation topic derivation, downstream-neighbour collection, and the show tree — that all need the literal keying. Re-keying the map in place would have silently changed `sdd show` output and topic membership, well outside a ranking directive's scope. The resolved index is therefore built beside `RefsTo` at the same construction site and read only by ranking. It reuses the live-head primitive shipped in 20260608-102734-s-tac-4ly rather than duplicating the walk, which is exactly what the directive asked for.

**Evidence, not just tests.** Six model tests cover hop compounding across zero to three hops, zero-hop parity, undamped in-degree, coldness demotion of a fresh successor, composite inheritance, and closure-does-not-inherit; `go vet`, `go test`, and `golangci-lint` are clean. The directive was explicit that arithmetic tests are necessary and not sufficient, so the check ran on the live graph: an unrelated real pair moved 0.293 → 0.698, which is exactly `0.293 + 0.8 × 0.506` where 0.506 was its predecessor's former score. The three downstream presentation surfaces were checked rather than assumed — focus-block stalled classification is unchanged, topic-count heat rows rose modestly where predecessors exist with counts untouched, and the catch-up active-and-hot and open-loops lanes are byte-identical between builds, which is the directive's "nothing changes for the ordinary case" property holding in practice.

**The named acceptance instance could not be used, and correcting that produced a second entry.** The directive required that a specific re-kinded rule appear in the guiding-directive lane afterwards. It could not: that entry declared its supersession in body and summary but carried no `supersedes` frontmatter field, so no edge existed and nothing could be inherited — the original still derived as an active `contract` holding 8.176 heat across nine inbound references, more than the top of the lane, unreachable by any active lane. The closed gap's diagnosis was partly a misreading of that: the predecessor was invisible because of its kind, not its supersession. Rather than substituting comparative findings, the graph was corrected first: 20260730-171311-d-cpt-0cv supersedes both the original and the prose-only re-kind, leaving one live copy on a real chain. Closing the re-kind instead of superseding it was considered and rejected — closure deliberately does not inherit, so it would have stranded that entry's own attention exactly as the original's was stranded, and would have recorded a genuine successor as a retirement without replacement. With the edge written, the acceptance check passes in its sharpest form: that entry scores 0.000 under the pre-change build and 7.991 under the new one, leading the lane.

**One thing stays open, deliberately.** Writing the correction exposed 20260731-083311-s-tac-4vx: the engine's capture procedure takes `supersedes` as a single entry ID, so the two-target supersede the correction required had to be written through the CLI instead. That gap is captured on its own line and is not part of this delivery. Separately, this ships the hop factor for heat only — whether it also multiplies the weighted in-degree of 20260705-130245-d-tac-cad is that plan's call to state rather than inherit silently, exactly as the directive specified, and the 0.8 factor remains the provisional calibration guess it was declared to be, due for revision against comparative findings rather than argument.
