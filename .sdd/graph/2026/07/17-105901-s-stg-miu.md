A settled positioning one-liner for SDD's public pitch: "SDD is agent-native memory for your project's decisions and the why behind them. Your agents get exactly the project context they need — what was noticed, discussed, and decided — and new knowledge is captured in dialogue with the people who create it, as the work happens." The line enters through the category prospects already search for (agent-native memory), then immediately re-differentiates on the reasoning-not-recording axis (s-stg-ob9): the payload is decisions and their why, not bulk-recorded knowledge. "Knowledge" appears only in the capture clause, where after the first sentence's sharp scoping it deliberately covers everything dialogue produces — observations, insights, evaluations, decisions — restoring the "curated new knowledge during the work" idea from the first draft; "curated" itself stays implicit because "captured in dialogue with the people who create it" states the curation mechanism concretely rather than claiming the adjective. The triad "noticed, discussed, and decided" traces signal → dialogue → decision in plain words, putting dialogue — the differentiator — inside the sentence; "exactly the context they need" makes a fit claim rather than a superlative one ("perfect context" was rejected as puffery that readers discount, bare "actual reasoning" as underselling that the graph carries the project's whole background and state). "In dialogue with the people who create it" carries the provenance story and keeps humans visible as authors — the distinction sharpened by the Honcho analysis (s-cpt-ue6), where a competitor markets "reasoning-first" but means automated inference, the opposite mechanism. Earlier wording choices weighed in dialogue: project-scoped "your project's decisions" over "you" (reads as a personal notes tool, contradicting the team/collaboration setting), over "your team" (excludes solo use), and over passive "what has been decided" (erases the human authors — the differentiator itself); the plural, project-scoped wording also pre-answers the shared-vs-private first-contact question observed at the lightning talk (s-stg-erj), though the full boundary/federation story that insight calls for still needs its own treatment in longer-form material. The line stays honest in autonomous agentic-loop settings, which were never ruled out of scope: under the intake model (d-cpt-fbi) autonomous agents capture candidate entries, but promotion to decisions passes through dialogue — autonomous where it can be, dialogue-gated where it matters. Natural first use is the landing headline for the v1 docs site (d-tac-c7b), whose "The idea" section leads with people, dialogue, and knowledge curation.
