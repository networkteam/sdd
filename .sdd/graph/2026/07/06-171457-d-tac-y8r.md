The engine's interview procedure runs alignment-building dialogue as a researched question loop: the agent takes a journalist's posture — researching the story, treating the user as the expert — asks exactly one pressure-tested question per turn with balanced background, grounds each answer back in the graph before the next question, and closes with a synthesis whose crystallized outcomes become seeded captures.

**Posture.** A journalist researching a story: homework first — the graph searched from several angles, chains read when anchored, a written list of assumptions to check and tensions spotted — and from it *the one question* that best advances the story. Questions are open, short, and background-balanced: two or three sentences of context, a concrete example when the matter is abstract, never option menus (menu-shaped offers and yes/no-as-essence were rejected in the May alignment architecture; the option-prompt experience in this slice's own design interview is fresh evidence). One question at a time is strict; each answer shapes the next. This settles the batching variant the May worked example carried — one-at-a-time is the word, as the evidence-by-construction insight (s-cpt-qs2) already assumed.

**Grounding loop per answer.** After each answer, the agent grounds it back in the graph before formulating the next question: search whether it is already captured somewhere (to connect it) and whether it strains standing entries (to push back). Each cycle does its homework until enough information is gathered and alignment feels sufficient. Pushback is graph-grounded or perspective-grounded and sharpens rather than confronts — anti-parrot; framings are pressure-tested from opposing angles before they enter the story (push-pull).

**Trajectory as evidence and resume material.** A running transcript accumulates per cycle: the question, the user's answer in their words, the grounding result, what shifted, and where the story heads next. The record is the alignment evidence by construction (s-cpt-qs2), and it deliberately carries the between-turns reasoning whose loss the resume-fidelity question names (s-cpt-m6m) — dogfooding this procedure is meant to answer that question's interview-shaped half.

**Shape.** frame (goal plus research record, seeded when dispatched from another move) → ask (the loop: one question per cycle, agent advances with next or saturated) → synthesize (articulate the built understanding back — the story, what crystallized, what pushback settled) → junction (user chooser: record dispatches seeded captures for the crystallized outcomes, each confirmed normally; conclude ends with alignment as the only outcome, legitimate since the trajectory lives in the session log).

Realizes the interactivity standard (d-prc-e2y) and supplies the alignment architecture's heavy tool (d-prc-1ob) as the interview move spec of Slice B (d-tac-dbk).
