The migration/relocation core on branch worktree-session-branch-binding is roughly four to five times larger than its committed scope warrants — about 6,900 lines of production code (excluding tests) where an estimated 1,200–1,600 would serve; the code is well-covered by tests, so the excess is maintenance surface, not risk — and needs a simplification refactoring that deletes and consolidates parallel machinery without changing the decided behavior. The disproportion was surfaced by the merge-readiness inner evaluation (20260726-195621-s-tac-0hg): each adversarial review round during implementation minted defensive findings that were individually accepted as "implementation fixes requiring no decision," and they compounded into transaction machinery nobody decided to build. The behavior itself is correct, decided, and pinned by the test suite — acknowledged move-if-absent migration, decline routing-hold, tombstone-dominates-material routing, identity rewrite, resumable manifest, crash-safe copies — so the refactoring target is deletion and consolidation under green tests, not redesign. The work, ordered by payoff: (1) replace trusted_store.go plus the rooted_regular_* and directory-sync os.Root/SameFile ancestor-pinning machinery (~530 lines defending against a symlink-rebinding adversary racing a single-user offline CLI in its own state dir) with a single os.OpenRoot for containment; (2) collapse the parallel rooted/path-based session and blob store implementations (~570 lines) to one — production only constructs the rooted variant, leaving the path-based one dead, against the single-path rule; (3) merge the two independent transaction engines (local_sessionrelocate.go, 3,025 lines, and local_sessionmigration.go, grown from 275 to ~2,450) onto one shared crash-safe publish helper (temp write, fsync, rename-no-clobber, dir fsync), dropping the per-payload four-state machine, the quarantine-then-finalize source deletion, and the ~200-line rollback of already-byte-verified publishes; (4) delete the Windows-only build files (~169 lines — goreleaser ships darwin and linux only); (5) collapse the three-state transition marker to two states (routing only ever distinguishes held vs moved) and trim the six-field marker re-derivation on the read path; (6) move the store-routing policy engine out of cmd/sdd/storepaths.go (358 lines plus 721 test lines in package main, operating solely on local-package types) into local/, consolidating the path-confinement rule currently defined in both places; (7) consolidate effective-branch precedence, currently spread across readTarget and captureMutationTarget with an asymmetric workBranch honor, into one shared resolution. Full findings with per-item file citations are attached to the evaluation done.
