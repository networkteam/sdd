Nothing reclaims session-lifetime scaffolding — neither session logs nor the staged-blob retentions they own — and the `StagedBlobStore` port cannot express a sweep even if a caller wanted one. Its five methods are Stage, Stat, Open, Retain and Release (application/blobstore.go:25-31): there is no way to enumerate owners or retentions and no way to delete a blob, so a sweeper is not merely unwritten but unwritable against the current contract. A repository-wide search for sweep, prune, GC or purge functions finds only agent-skill pruning in `sdd init`.

The gap rests on that absence, not on any growth measurement. One observation confirms accumulation is real rather than theoretical: the live machine-global store currently holds 48 session logs, ten staging directories, and 4.7M of its 5.4M total. That is a single snapshot of one machine and one project, so it establishes only that nothing is ever reclaimed — no rate can be read from it, and none is needed, since an unbounded store with no reclamation path is a gap at any rate above zero.

This is a gap rather than deliberate conservatism only because sessions are now classified as scaffolding (20260727-224047-d-cpt-u8o): durability belongs to captured entries, and a session's useful life is roughly that of the agent conversation. Under that calibration, retaining every log forever is not caution about losing a record — it is the absence of a lifecycle for something explicitly declared not to be a record. The convergence contract (20260727-230506-d-cpt-msg) already names the attachment half as an accepted consequence of ordered delivery, with a sweeper as its intended answer, and this entry records that the answer does not exist and cannot currently be built at the port.

Two distinct sources feed the retention side. A mutation whose finalizers succeeded but whose terminal never landed keeps its staged-blob retention permanently, since the only release paths are the applied path and the terminal. And a crash between attachment materialization and the entry write leaves orphan attachment files, the case the contract accepts by name. Neither is data loss; both accumulate silently.

The placement decisions make the growth harder to notice rather than harder to justify. Staged-blob sweeping was deliberately assigned outside the SDD-facing surface as composition-side operations work (20260712-194427-d-tac-wjq), and no composition has implemented it; the store then moved machine-global under the XDG state root (20260722-112853-d-tac-ln1), correctly, since sessions are not derivable and a cache root is deletable by contract — but that also puts the growth outside every working tree, where no `git status` or repository cleanup will ever surface it.

What the mechanism should be — age-based, session-state-based, an explicit command, an automatic pass, and whether the port grows enumeration and deletion or the reclamation stays entirely inside the local composition — is left to a separate dialogue.
