The recovery projection's `State` field answers three unrelated questions at once — is the write delivered, what is still owed if not, and did a human recovery verb touch it — and the resulting seven-value vocabulary reports every ordinary successful write as `recovered`. Observed live on the real store: `sdd recover --history` labels 80-plus normal captures `recovered`, including entries captured minutes earlier by healthy sessions, because the ordinary write path passes `RecoveryApply` as its terminal verb (application/transition.go:107) and the projection's terminal branch maps every verb except discard and abandon-unknown to `RecoveryRecovered`. Nothing was recovered. The label is answering the provenance question in the field reserved for the delivery question, because provenance has nowhere else to live.

That conflation is the same defect shape this session hit twice already in the work that surfaced it (20260728-005407-s-tac-1kr): the original stranded-notice bug branched on a terminal's presence instead of reading the recorded outcome, and the first fix branched on a derived string instead of the underlying evidence. Both were judgments baked into a label and then re-read as if they were facts. A field carrying three questions guarantees more of them.

Measured against the standard the projection is meant to express, the excess is explicit rather than aesthetic: 20260727-230506-d-cpt-msg commits to exactly two durable conditions, pending delivery and delivered, with transient failures as diagnostics rather than states. The implementation has seven, one of which (`applied-finalized`) was added during this session precisely because no existing value could say "delivered without a terminal" — a symptom of a state space being asked to encode combinations rather than conditions.

The direction is settled in dialogue and separates the three questions into three fields. `State` answers only delivery, with three values — delivered, pending, abandoned — where actionability is `pending` itself rather than a parallel boolean that can disagree with it. A reason field carries what is owed for a pending item and which verb closed an abandoned one. A provenance flag records that a recovery verb touched the item, derivable from the presence of a recovery attempt, which is what `recovered` was trying to express. Abandoned survives as a third value because it is a human decision rather than a delivery outcome, not because delivery has three states.

The change is contained. Outside the projection itself the production consumers are two lines in cmd/sdd/recover.go — an equality check and a verb-selecting switch, both of which key on the reason afterwards — plus a third that casts an `ApplyState` into a `RecoveryState` and manufactures values outside the declared set, which disappears. Roughly ten test assertions and the documented state list follow. `RecoveryState` is exported from the public application package, so its values change for embedders; under 20260712-194427-d-tac-wjq's versioning stance v1 carries no migration obligation, and the visible cost is that `sdd recover --history` output changes.

Rejected: keeping `recovered` alongside the new values. It reads as additive and safe while preserving exactly the conflation that produced this.
