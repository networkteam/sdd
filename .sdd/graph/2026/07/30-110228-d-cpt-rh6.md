The three knowledge homes — facts, step instructions, and the session shell serve — stay non-overlapping in *authority*, not in every word: a teaser may carry a taste of what it points at, must carry why and when to reach for it, and must never read as complete. This sharpens the non-overlap condition of the base-facts pull-layer directive (20260707-140544-d-cpt-dtv), which as written reads as a zero-duplication rule. Zero duplication is the wrong target, because a bare pointer cannot create the pull it exists to create. The real-agent evaluation proved this concretely (20260718-091255-s-prc-e2v): the agent had to know enough about view syntax to infer that a view was needed before the reference became findable at all, so the breadcrumb arrived too late to bootstrap the very call it was meant to enable. A teaser that names a fact without conveying what reaching for it would buy is indistinguishable, in effect, from no teaser.

The contract is already committed for one surface, and this generalizes it. The derived-index directive requires a fact's index title to stand alone as a retrieval cue — "enough to understand when pulling the fact will help, not merely a terse name" (20260719-133529-d-cpt-qhn). That is exactly the teaser rule, applied to the shell's pointer surface. Extended to step instructions: a step may say what a fact covers and why this moment calls for it, and may borrow a phrase to make the need legible, but the definitions, tables, and calibration detail live in the fact and only there. Authority is the thing that must not be duplicated — not vocabulary, not framing, not a sentence of orientation.

The failure mode this guards against is a second surface a reader could mistake for the whole — e.g. a compressed restatement of an entry-kind vocabulary, or a definition table, sitting alongside the fact that owns it with each reading as complete — not a surface that merely whets an appetite. Duplicated authority drifts, and the drift is silent: nothing marks which copy is current, so a reader who lands on the wrong one gets no signal that another exists. That silence is the cost, and it is why the boundary is drawn at authority rather than at wording. A corollary follows for anything the implementation already knows: reference content derivable from code renders at load rather than being re-written as prose, so the fact tracks the code with no manual sync, and hand-written prose is reserved for judgment the code cannot express. The line between an adequate taste and an accidental second authority stays a judgment calibrated in practice rather than specified up front — hence medium confidence.
