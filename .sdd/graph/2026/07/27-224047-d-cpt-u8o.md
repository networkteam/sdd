Engine sessions are scaffolding whose lifetime is bound to an agent conversation — including its compactions, handoffs and resumes — while the graph is the project's only durable record, so durability is earned by a captured entry and never by session bookkeeping. This is the standard by which session machinery is calibrated: sessions must be robust, but robustness and durability are different requirements, and conflating them produces apparatus out of proportion to what sessions are worth.

Robust means a session fails cleanly and converges: it does not corrupt its own log, does not silently lose the work a user has collected mid-dialogue, and always reaches a defined end state without human archaeology. Durable would mean the session is itself a record worth preserving indefinitely and reconstructing exactly. Only the second justifies record-grade guarantee apparatus — multi-state projections of uncertainty, specialized recovery verbs, authorized audit events, legacy binding paths. Sessions warrant the first.

The practical consequence is that a small number of clearly defined and understood failure modes is acceptable for session state, provided each has a path that converges. A session whose bookkeeping is left incomplete may be reported plainly and discarded; what must never be lost or duplicated is the entry the dialogue confirmed. This re-scopes the durable-transition requirement (20260712-194427-d-tac-wjq): recording intent before apply is sound where it protects the graph write across two independent stores, but the requirement was elaborated as though session and graph deserved symmetric guarantees, and the Slice 5 delivery (20260713-005437-s-tac-paq) built reconciliation, fencing and retention machinery on that footing. Each piece was individually defensible; the calibration underneath them was wrong.

The stranded-notice gap (20260727-102820-s-tac-9op) is the evidence. Thirty-four pending-write notices, every underlying graph write successful, twenty-nine unclearable by any available verb, heading every orientation serve with an action nobody can take. The record that mattered was never at risk — the entries are present and committed. Only the scaffolding's bookkeeping was incomplete, and treating that bookkeeping as durable state requiring authorized recovery is precisely what turned benign residue into a permanent user-facing defect. That gap closed by asking for the recovery contract to be reduced to what is necessary; this directive supplies the standard for the reduction.

It grounds in the single-record aspiration (20260422-123237-d-stg-3k0). If the graph is the sole system of record, then everything else — including the engine's own session store — is a working surface, and preserving a working surface with record-grade guarantees is a parallel record by another name, bought at the cost of machinery that obscures the primary case it exists to serve.

It also bounds, without weakening, the handover-fidelity invariant (20260719-124405-d-cpt-0tm). That invariant demands intent-bearing procedure state project faithfully into a re-entry serve, and it stands unchanged: fidelity across a handoff is exactly the robustness sessions must have, because an agent that cannot re-establish the work discards effort the user already spent. What it must not be read to mean is that session state is a durable record. Its scope is continuity within the session's life, not preservation beyond it — and nothing in it obliges the engine to keep session bookkeeping recoverable once the dialogue it served is over.

The concrete delivery contract this standard implies — how a confirmed write converges, and which conditions remain durable — is being settled separately; this entry fixes only the calibration those mechanics must answer to.
