Slice 1 of the coordinator-lifecycle directive (20260723-114725-d-tac-z6r) is implemented, reviewed, and committed on branch worktree-coordinator-lifecycle (commits a56372a, 316c28f): the transient output coordinator now owns an explicit dormant/armed/live/done lifecycle as a bubbletea-free slog-routing handler in internal/cliout/coordinator.go, closing the clone-path full-height scroll race (20260723-112802-s-tac-bnw) and the fast-path escape leak plus raw Ctrl-C error (20260709-161249-s-tac-bg7).
