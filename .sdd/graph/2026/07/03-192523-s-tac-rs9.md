Slice 5 of the v1 workflow engine plan shipped (commit dbb92b8): the engage and explore procedures are embedded base entries with per-procedure table tests, the shell attaches open-threads blocks at junction payloads only, and start_procedure carries a parent link for sub-move lineage. Engage runs the universal entry move with the anchor's (and companion targets') chains injected at the brief step — inspection by construction — and ends at a user move junction, dispatching follow-up moves as their own procedures rather than containing them. Explore is a compression mission for a disposable context: targets and goal travel as params across the context boundary, the worker's full-read discipline is evidenced by graph-resolving inspected IDs, and the briefing contract keeps full IDs with enough context to act without re-reading. This makes chain-reading structural for both new procedures — partially addressing the widen-depth fabrication gap (20260703-142356-s-cpt-be9), whose capture-side assemble hole stays open. The capture playback unit gained re-serve guidance — delta after adjust, always the full body before a confirm that follows body edits — closing the prose gap (20260703-142414-s-tac-3ug), and the junction open-threads directive (20260703-142447-d-tac-nqo) closes with blocks on session entry, completion, abandon, and resume, never mid-procedure. Table tests and MCP integration tests gate the mechanics; parity in use for both procedures awaits side-by-side dogfooding, the plan's remaining AC6 bar. The design dialogue's reasoning — the dispatch boundary, compression ownership, why explore is a procedure at all — is preserved in the attached [design record](./03-192523-s-tac-rs9/design-record.md).
