Move dispatch and start share one seeding contract across all engine procedures: params seed state fields, gates evaluate on entry, and a step whose conditions are already satisfied auto-advances. On this primitive, two mechanics resolve the evaluate→capture friction measured in the third dogfooding run.

**Declared evidence handoff.** A dispatching junction declares what it seeds into the spawned move: the parent's grounding evidence (its widenReport) and the anchor by default, overridable per dispatch when a finding centers elsewhere. The seeded evidence lands in the child's own state, so the child's grounding gate is satisfied on entry, parked-and-resumed instances keep their grounding record, and the serve instructs extending the evidence only when the draft reaches beyond it. The alternative — relaxing the child's gate via a cross-instance predicate — was rejected: it leaves the child without an evidence record, breaks resume fidelity, and couples transition evaluation across instances. A cold start without a parent is unchanged: the gate is only ever satisfied by evidence, inherited or fresh, never waived.

**Uniform anchor contract, split by procedure class.** Every user-facing anchored move takes an optional `anchor` (entry-id) plus `anchorHint` (the user's pointer in their words); a resolver first step gates on a resolved anchor, auto-advances when seeded, and otherwise resolves the hint through widening plus the user's pick. Recency heuristics ("the last thing we shipped") are excluded — they break in collaborative graphs; ambiguity resolves in dialogue, where the user is. The procedure class vocabulary gains **`task`** alongside move and shell: procedures a move delegates work to — dispatched with required resolved params, no user choosers, excluded from the shell's move enumeration and junction offers, and preferring a disposable context (fork-hinted to the harness, inline as paid fallback). Explore is reclassified from move to task, the first member of its class rather than a special case; resolution for tasks belongs to the dispatching dialogue — the same principle applied where no user is present. The names "tool" and "sub-move" were rejected: the former collides with MCP tool vocabulary, the latter with the lineage sense of nested moves, which is orthogonal to class. A task's result flows back to its dispatcher textually — the parent cites the task instance's evidence in its own report; structuring this up-edge is deliberately deferred until dogfooding shows the textual form failing, consistent with hard gates guarding only the write path (20260629-204630-s-cpt-1dz).

This revises the shipped capture, engage, evaluate, and explore specs and is the template groom, implementation, and interview are specced against under the engine plan (20260702-220449-d-tac-ry0). The third candidate from the gap — seeding junction-articulated findings as draft material — rides the same handoff mechanism and is deferred.
