The spike fixed the direction — self-describing evidence, incentive over verification, judgment advisory — but not the form: which reusable evidence primitives (verbatim specifics, cross-coherence, alignment trajectory) a playbook move or rule declares per work surface, and how much is enough to push faking-cost above doing-cost before the demand becomes friction. This is the open design question a next spike would target.
