The `/sdd-catchup` interpretation guidance for inactive-ref sub-lines (shipped in s-prc-u5p) biases the briefing agent into reading a currently-inactive reference as a recent, causal unblock. In one fresh catch-up session the briefing claimed d-tac-jqq's blocker "just closed with" the expand(refs) ship; in fact its only inactive ref is the `sdd view` foundation d-tac-uww, closed 2026-05-07 (three weeks earlier) and not a dependency of the shipped work. The agent held the claim confidently and corrected only when challenged within the same session — the confident-until-pushback half of s-prc-uq8. The render is correct (present state only, no timestamp, no causality); the guidance ("a blocker that just closed is likely now unblocked") supplied the recency, causation, and "blocker" relationship the data lacks, and the generic `refs` unknown-kind verb signals no relationship at all. Broader signal: interpretive prompt guidance is a strong bias lever — the initial evaluation write-up itself overstated the evidence as "two independent sessions" when it was one session self-correcting, the same over-claim reproduced in the evaluating agent. No true-positive case existed on the current graph, so the feature's reader-facing payoff stays unobserved while the false-positive risk landed on the first run. Fix direction: strip the interpretive suggestion from the catch-up note, describing the sub-lines and their limits without proposing a conclusion.
