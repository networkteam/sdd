Skill updates for the augment-plan pattern landed in commit ae6359c, closing d-prc-9ti's two operative commitments.

First, `Transition to implementation` now includes a new step 7 requiring `sdd show <entry-id> --downstream` before starting work, with the union of original ACs and downstream commitments treated as the implicit acceptance contract. Step 11 was extended to close the original entry plus any augmenting directives in the same done signal via `--closes`. Step 10 (design-choice handling) gained a branch for narrow refinements, redirecting them to the augmentation pattern instead of always treating them as missing decisions.

Second, a new `Augment Plan Playbook` section after the Grooming Playbook documents the move end-to-end: the ceremony spectrum (mechanical fix / augment / supersede), distinguishing tests, how to capture an augmenting directive (description shape, refs, layer, kind, confidence), and how the closing done signal handles distributed acceptance. The trade-off (acceptance contract distributed across original entry plus downstream) is named explicitly per the directive's intent.

Third, `Modes of working` gained a brief `Augment plan` entry pointing to the playbook, sitting between `Act/Implement` and `Groom`.

The bundle source under `internal/bundledskills/claude/sdd/SKILL.md` and the installed copy under `.claude/skills/sdd/SKILL.md` were committed together (the latter regenerated via `./bin/sdd init --scope project`), folded into a single commit. No augmenting directives existed on d-prc-9ti at close time, so the original directive's commitments are the full contract addressed here.
