For converting screencasts to README-quality GIFs, capturing via macOS native screen recording and converting with Gifski (open source) at lowest quality, 5 FPS, and 838px width produces ~1.12Mb output suitable for embedding. An earlier attempt using browser capture via gifcap.dev combined with the getsitecontrol.com GIF compressor degraded quality at the compression stage and was rejected. Refines the recipe summarized in s-cpt-6tu by naming the working tool chain.
