The activity-vs-plan-vs-directive kind tests in `framework-concepts.md` read ambiguously between "shape of the work" and "shape of the outputs," allowing agents and the pre-flight validator to disqualify activity on the wrong axis. Observed live during capture of d-prc-iqw: when drafting an activity that dispatches follow-up engage sessions to dispose synthesis-named candidates, Claude defaulted to directive on the reasoning that "the shape isn't known up front" — meaning the outputs (which moves emerge, what form each takes) are emergent. The activity test currently reads *"specifies work whose shape is already known from context (parent plan, refs, or self-evident narrative)"* — but the *work* here (dialogue / engage sessions) has a well-known shape; only the outputs are emergent. Pre-flight then independently hit the same trap on the captured activity, raising a `[medium] activity-shape-ambiguity` finding that suggested `kind: plan` with ACs or directive, repeating the conflation.

Fix shape: sharpen the kind tests — explicit guidance that activity validates against work-shape, not output-shape, and that emergent outputs are compatible with activity when the work form is known. Probably also adjusting the activity-vs-directive boundary test (currently leans on "choice against alternatives" for directive but doesn't push back on directive when no choice is being made) and the pre-flight rubric for activity-shape findings.
