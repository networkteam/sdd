Refines AC 14 of the `sdd view` plan (d-tac-uww) to scope slice 7's coverage to the explicit `name(string)` modifier branch only, deferring the "otherwise auto-derived from rank+decay" branch as a separable follow-up. Slice 7 implements name() as a last-write-wins modifier that sets `query.SectionResult.Name`, rendered by RenderView as a `## <title>` line before the section body. Auto-derivation — synthesizing a section title from the configured rank algorithm and decay (e.g. "Top by heat (exp-14d)") when no name() is supplied — needs section-pipeline introspection at render time and adds noise to the FlatList/Grouped/FocusBlock shapes that don't otherwise need to know their rank context.

Dialogued at slice 7 implementation. Three options were considered: (a) implement auto-derive in slice 7, threading rank+decay metadata through SectionResult and FlatList; (b) defer until the closing slice with explicit deferral; (c) drop auto-derive from the plan entirely. Option (b) was chosen — auto-derive is genuinely useful for catch-up readability ("Top by heat (exp-14d)" is more informative than nothing) but isn't structurally required for slice 7's core contribution. Slice 8 either implements it as a small pre-render synthesis pass or captures a separate deferral directive based on observed need from the comparative findings work.

The closing done signal at AC 23 should either: (1) confirm auto-derive shipped in slice 8 and AC 14 fully met, or (2) document an explicit re-deferral with rationale (e.g. user-supplied name() coverage proved sufficient, auto-derive judged premature). Either resolution is valid; this directive only commits to making the choice explicit at closing rather than leaving the partial-coverage state ambiguous.
