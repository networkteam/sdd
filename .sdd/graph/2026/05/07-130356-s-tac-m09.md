Six read-side queries (`LintQuery`, `ListQuery`, `SearchQuery`, `ShowQuery`, `StatusQuery`, `ViewQuery`) carry `Graph *model.Graph` as a struct field, and `WIPListQuery` carries `GraphDir string`. The shell loads the graph once and hands it to the finder per call; the finder consumes it as if it were declarative input. This conflates *what to read* (declarative intent) with *the resolved data the read works against* (a dependency the finder should own).

The structural consequence runs against the CQRS layering in `d-cpt-ah1`: the finder is reduced to a transformer over shell-provided data, and the query slot doubles as a courier for the resolved dependency. A purely declarative query would carry only intent — the data and disk locations the finder needs to do its work would be finder-side dependencies wired at the composition root.

Surfaced during slice 8 of `d-tac-uww` (sdd view) when adding `source(wip)` made the leak structurally obvious: the natural extension was to add `WIPMarkers []*model.WIPMarker` to `ViewQuery`, pre-resolved data through the query slot. That move was rejected in dialogue, which exposed that the same shape already exists across the six queries above. The exact resolution — where the deps live (Options, constructor, separate dependency type), whether to migrate all read queries together or incrementally, what to do with `WIPListQuery` and other `*Dir`-carrying queries — is open and needs its own decision.
