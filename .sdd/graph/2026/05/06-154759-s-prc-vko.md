Pre-flight validation templates do not recognize the augment-plan pattern (`d-prc-9ti`), rejecting otherwise-valid augmenting directives with findings that argue against the pattern's core mechanics. Two capture attempts at a Plan 1 augmenting directive (resolving the topic-filter ownership ambiguity in `d-tac-gvn`) were both blocked at high severity; capturing was completed via `--skip-preflight`.

Observed misalignments include: asking for the augment-plan pattern itself to be cited as a ref (attempt 1, contradicting the skill's "don't cite graph-mechanics rules" guidance); asking for the immutable plan to be edited to backward-ref the augmenting directive (attempt 2, contradicting plan immutability and downstream traversal as the visibility mechanism); and asking for AC supersession or directive weakening as the only resolution paths (attempt 2, missing that the augment-plan pattern exists exactly to refine ACs without supersession).

This sits within `s-prc-vvd`'s broader concern about pre-flight oscillation on supersession-shape rubrics. Resolution direction: pre-flight templates should recognize the augmenting-directive shape (directive refs an active plan, refines AC contract, scoped to the plan, closed by the plan's done signal) and treat AC-refinement-via-directive as a first-class resolution path alongside supersession.
