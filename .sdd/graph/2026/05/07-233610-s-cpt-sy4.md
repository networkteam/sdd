The catch-up's "Other open" drill section currently relies on the agent inferring 3–5 area names from the input — a synthesis step that costs wall-clock time and gets harder as graph size grows; a `sdd view` aggregation that returns top topics with counts (e.g. `as-counts`) plus topic backfill on existing entries would replace inference with mechanical lookup.

The type-system 7+7 expansion (d-cpt-ni0) added topics via inline frontmatter and annotation entries, and the topic-drill mode is already directed (d-tac-qom). What's missing is two pieces:

1. **Topics actually assigned to existing entries** — none have been so far. Backfill could be partial (top-N hot entries first) rather than full-graph; partial is enough to make the catch-up surface useful.

2. **A `sdd view` aggregation render that returns top topics with counts and aggregate heat without enumerating member entries** — e.g. `as-counts` or similar. The dream output is a few lines like `catch-up-scaling — 12 entries, heat 8.3` per topic, no entries listed underneath.

With both pieces, the drill section becomes mechanical lookup of top-N topic labels, removing the per-run inference cost and scaling cleanly. The agent prints the names; no synthesis required.

Independent of the heat-quality gap (s-cpt-zsd), which addresses a different ranking concern (per-ref qualifiers via s-cpt-k7z). Surfaced during catch-up sub-skill design dialogue (d-tac-k4l) when comparing the agent's introspection on cost ("at 30 warm items I'd need a harder selection pass") against the user's structural proposal for mechanical aggregation.
