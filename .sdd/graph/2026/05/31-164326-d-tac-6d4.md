Implement the eight-kind principle-based ref-kind vocabulary across the model, pre-flight, and skill surfaces, realizing the redefinition decided in d-cpt-s9j. Renames `grounds` -> `grounded-in` and merges `evidence` into it through a read-layer parse transform (the same mechanism as legacy bare-string -> `unknown`, no history rewrite), adds `required-by` as the forward inverse of `depends-on`, and generalizes `addresses` to cover realizing a decision's commitment. Threads each referenced entry's derived status into the pre-flight ref-meta check so it can distinguish grounded-in / builds-on / refines (the active/closed/basis split d-cpt-s9j flagged as a separate fix, pulled into scope here), and validates the rewritten rubric's calibration end-to-end against the live runner so correct choices stop producing debatable medium findings. Rendering follows the parse layer for free, so legacy refs surface under the new names with no verb-map change. The rubric and skill rewrites stay consistent with the active selection-and-calibration directive (d-prc-2is); label-aware heat weighting (d-tac-cpw) is unbuilt and simply inherits the clean vocabulary. See the [implementation plan](./31-164326-d-tac-6d4/plan.md) for the surfaces map, mechanism notes, and carve-outs.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The capturable ref-kind set is the eight principle-based kinds (`grounded-in, builds-on, refines, addresses, surfaces, depends-on, required-by, related`); a new capture using `grounds` or `evidence` is rejected.
- [ ] On-disk `grounds` and `evidence` refs parse as `grounded-in` through a read-layer transform with no entry files rewritten, and `sdd lint` reports zero issues on the existing graph.
- [ ] `sdd show` and `sdd view` `expand(refs)` render pre-existing `grounds`/`evidence` refs as `grounded-in`, and render `required-by` for a new entry that carries it.
- [ ] Every pre-flight template defines the vocabulary by principle -- generalized `addresses` (act on a signal or realize a decision's commitment), `grounded-in` absorbing empirical citation, forward-class `surfaces`/`required-by` with no back-ref, `related` as the floor -- and no template still lists `grounds` or `evidence`.
- [ ] The pre-flight ref-meta check receives each referenced entry's derived status (active / closed-by / superseded-by) and uses it to separate `grounded-in`, `builds-on`, and `refines`, while summary-prompt hashes stay unchanged (no summary regeneration is triggered).
- [ ] Ref-kind calibration is validated end-to-end against the live pre-flight runner (extending `internal/llm/preflight_eval_test.go`): one scenario per row of the reference note's scenario->kind table, plus the known-debatable cases (`addresses` realizing a decision, `grounded-in` vs `builds-on` by target status, `related`-floor vs a sharper kind). Each asserts a genuine wrong-kind produces a `high` finding and a correct or defensible choice produces no finding -- not a spurious `medium` -- pinning the calibration the rubric rewrite must hold.
- [ ] `framework-concepts.md` and `SKILL.md` document the principle-based vocabulary -- the rename/merge/addition, backward-vs-forward direction and capture-order, the no-back-ref point, and the completeness principle for future growth -- consistent with d-prc-2is.
- [ ] Installed skills are rebuilt and re-stamped from the updated bundle via `sdd init --scope project`, with bundle source and installed copies committed together.
- [ ] `go vet ./...`, `go test ./...`, and `golangci-lint run ./...` are clean.
