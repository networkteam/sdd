First-real-use evaluation of `sdd view --layout='top(N)'` surfaced that standing entries (contracts, aspirations) accumulate refs *because tactical work cites them as grounding* — correct citation behaviour. The heat algorithm treats grounding refs the same as refs that extend or close: a `refs:` from a tactical plan to a contract pulls the contract toward "hot" with the same weight as a `closes:` from a done signal pulling a plan toward "actively resolving."

The structural foundation for fixing this already exists as an open conceptual gap: `s-cpt-k7z` proposes optional per-ref qualifiers (a 'why' annotation per relationship). With qualifiers in place, heat could weight by qualifier kind — grounding-citations decay or contribute less, extension/resolution citations contribute fully — turning ref accumulation into a richer signal than raw count.

Resolution depends on `s-cpt-k7z`'s structural change landing first; this gap names the heat-weighting consequence and the application path. Companion to a separately-captured filter-shape concern (`s-tac-x1n`) — the same evaluation surfaced both, but their resolutions are independent: filtering can ship without ref-qualifier metadata; heat-weighting needs that structural change first.
