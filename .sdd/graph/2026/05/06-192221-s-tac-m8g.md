Shipped augment-aware pre-flight: `decision_refs.tmpl` now recognizes the augmenting-directive shape and treats AC-refinement-via-directive as a first-class resolution path, unblocking the contradictory pre-flight findings recorded in s-prc-vko. Code in commit a10be53.

End-to-end validation: capturing `d-tac-k7v` (a directive refining `d-tac-ghm`'s closure protocol — same structural shape as the original blocked attempts on `d-tac-gvn`) ran cleanly without `--skip-preflight`, producing no high findings. The original blocker pattern is gone.

## AC walk

1. **Augmenting-directive shape defined and named.** New "Augmenting directives" section in `decision_refs.tmpl` (commit a10be53) enumerates all four discriminants explicitly: (a) `kind: directive` decision, (b) refs an active plan, (c) has no `supersedes` and no `closes` on the plan, (d) refines or clarifies one of the plan's acceptance criteria. The section also names the consequences: plan stays immutable, directive joins the plan's implicit AC chain, closure happens via the plan's done signal listing both.

2. **AC tension is resolved by refinement, not supersession.** The section explicitly states: "AC tension is resolved by refinement, not supersession. The directive sharpens, narrows, or clarifies the plan's AC contract while the plan stays untouched. Do not propose plan amendment, AC supersession, or directive weakening as findings on this basis." Eval evidence: `TestPreflightEval_AugmentingDirective_CleanRefinement` produces empty findings; `TestPreflightEval_AugmentingDirective_TopicFilterReconstruction` produces empty findings. Real-world `d-tac-k7v` capture: zero `scope-creep` / `scope-smuggling` / `amend-AC` findings — exactly the family of reflexive blockers the original attempts hit.

3. **No backward-ref demand on the plan.** Section explicitly states: "Refs are one-way. The directive refs the plan; the plan does not back-ref the directive ... Do not ask the ref'd plan to be amended to add a backward ref." `d-tac-k7v` capture produced no `missing-ref-to-directive-target` finding (the high-severity blocker on attempt 2 in s-prc-vko's attachment).

4. **Graph-mechanics meta-pattern names in prose are not refs.** Section explicitly states: "Graph-mechanics meta-patterns are not refs. Names appearing in prose to identify the entry's shape (e.g. citing the augment-plan pattern itself) are framework-level rules, not logical dependencies for this individual entry. Do not ask for them as refs." This is the rule the template now enforces — independent of whether any specific subsequent entry happens to invoke a meta-pattern by ID.

5. **Lifecycle recognized.** Section explicitly states: "Lifecycle is split. The directive remains open across the plan's implementation window and is closed by the plan's done signal alongside the plan. Do not flag this as dangling commitment or closure-sequencing ambiguity." No `closure-sequencing-ambiguity` finding on `d-tac-k7v` (one of the original mediums in s-prc-vko's attachment).

6. **Eval test fixtures: positive and negative both present and behaving.** Three cases under `internal/llm/preflight_eval_test.go`:
   - `TestPreflightEval_AugmentingDirective_CleanRefinement` (positive) — directive refines a latency-target AC on an elasticsearch migration plan. Result across runs: empty findings. Passes.
   - `TestPreflightEval_AugmentingDirective_GenuineSupersessionFlagged` (negative) — directive asserts two ACs are wrong (replacement shape). Across runs the LLM **always produces a finding** identifying the directive as replacement-shaped and naming `supersedes` as the better-fit operation; categories observed include `ac-replacement-not-refinement` (at high severity) and `ac-assertion-vs-augmentation` (at medium severity). The finding is consistent; only the severity drifts. The test asserts on finding presence via the `mentionsSupersession()` helper rather than severity, because the boundary between medium and high is genuinely ambiguous per `verdict.tmpl`'s calibration ("clear wrong usage" vs "worth naming for dialogue") and both readings are defensible. AC 6's "should still be flagged" is satisfied — the negative case is always flagged.
   - `TestPreflightEval_AugmentingDirective_TopicFilterReconstruction` — directive resolves topic-filter ownership across two active plans (`d-tac-gvn` and `d-tac-uww`). Result: empty findings.

7. **Reconstruction produces no high tied to augment-plan pattern.** Topic-filter reconstruction case is empty across runs. The fixture's plan1 entry mirrors `d-tac-gvn`'s relevant AC text verbatim — including the original ambiguity-bearing wording "reuses `topic(L)` filter primitive from Plan 2's shared internals" — and plan2 mirrors `d-tac-uww`'s "Pipeline primitives in shared internal packages, callable from other CLI commands (`sdd list --topic` consumes `topic(L)` filter)" — so the test exercises the exact AC inconsistency that triggered `s-prc-vko`'s capture. Real-world reinforcement: `d-tac-k7v` (different content, same augmenting-directive shape) cleared pre-flight without `--skip-preflight`.
