The ref-kind vocabulary covers `depends-on` for the dependent → prerequisite direction, but lacks the inverse `required-by` for the natural capture moment when the prerequisite itself lands. Pattern: a plan or focus is waiting on something, and when the missing piece is captured the author wants a ref on the new entry that names the structural relationship "this is required by X." Today the closest options — `related` plus prose, or `builds-on` when the new entry is the next step in time — don't carry the signal that a reader or `sdd view` could query for "what was needed by X?"

Proposes adding `required-by` to the closed set as the symmetric inverse of `depends-on`, written from the prerequisite's side at its own capture time. The name avoids overclaiming (unlike `unblocks` or `enables`) — multiple `required-by` refs can point at the same target without any single one asserting sole release.

Worth noting that `required-by` would be the first kind introduced specifically as a paired inverse — a pattern that could shape future vocabulary growth (do we want inverses for `builds-on` or `refines` too?).
