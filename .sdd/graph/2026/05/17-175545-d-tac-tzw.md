Sharpens AC 8 and AC 9 of the per-ref kind qualifiers plan (d-tac-cs0) by specifying the LLM-check's operational scope and naming the code seams for both pre-flight layers, capturing pre-flight observations from the plan's capture session as implementer guidance.

**AC 9 — LLM consistency check, operational scope.** The check flags **direct contradictions** between a ref's `desc` and the entry body: cases where the desc characterizes the relationship in a way the body affirmatively refutes. Three categories explicitly do *not* count as inconsistency: (a) topical drift — desc and body emphasize different aspects of the same relationship, (b) granularity differences — desc more or less specific than the body, (c) tonal or wording variations with equivalent meaning. The check is skipped when desc is absent. Findings cite the ref ID and the contradicting body excerpt.

**AC 8 — mechanical validation seam.** Vocabulary validation lives in `internal/model` (kind values checked at unmarshal and/or `Entry.Validate`). The pre-flight runner in `internal/llm/preflight` invokes the model validator and converts returned errors to high-severity pre-flight findings, alongside its LLM-based findings. Structural rules stay with the model; pre-flight orchestrates and renders. This maintains the CQRS layering — domain validity is not pushed into the pre-flight LLM call.

**AC 9 — LLM check integration point.** The desc-vs-body check extends the existing pre-flight LLM prompt in `internal/llm/preflight`, with refs (id, kind, desc) added to the prompt context. Output uses the existing finding format (severity, message, citing entry IDs).

These refinements do not change the plan's scope — they sharpen what the implementing agent needs to know about *how* and *where* to wire the two pre-flight layers.
