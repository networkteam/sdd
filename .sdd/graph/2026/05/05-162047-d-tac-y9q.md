Refines `sdd show` depth-0 rendering to indent the Summary section's text under its label and insert a horizontal rule (`---`) before the body description, separating the derived-summary block from the canonical entry text. Preserves the inline summary that s-tac-233 shipped — the depth-0 surfacing makes summary/body drift visible during review — while addressing the read-flow friction where adjacent prose paragraphs blurred together with only a blank line between them. Considered alternatives: reordering body-before-summary (rejected — breaks the scan-then-read pattern shared with `sdd status` and `sdd list`); a `Description:` label without a rule (rejected in favor of the rule, which carries cleaner visual hand-off for prose).
