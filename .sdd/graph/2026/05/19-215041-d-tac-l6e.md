Adds `refines` to the closed-set ref-kind vocabulary established by d-tac-cs0, growing the set from 7 to 8 kinds and giving the augmenting-directive pattern (d-prc-9ti) its canonical structural marker. `refines` names a directive-or-decision ref that sharpens, narrows, or clarifies an active plan's (or directive's) commitments in place, without superseding — the augmenting-directive shape, where the augmenting entry's lifecycle is split and closure happens alongside the target via the target's done signal.

**Distinguishing test against `builds-on`.** Both kinds describe a forward relationship to a prior entry; the test is whether the target is still active and whether the new entry refines in place or continues in time:

- `refines` — the target is **active**; no `supersedes` or `closes` on the target; the body sharpens, narrows, or clarifies the target's existing commitments. The target stays active during the refinement window and is closed by its own done signal (which also closes the refining entry).
- `builds-on` — extends a **closed** entry (the next decision in the chain), or extends an active one in a forward "next step" continuation sense rather than in-place refinement. The new entry adds work *after* the target, not within its commitment scope.

**Calibration impact (LLM ref-meta consistency check, per d-tac-olh).**

- `high` — Directive refs an active plan with body framed as "narrows AC X" or "applies the plan's commitment to scope Y" but `kind: builds-on` (or vice versa: forward-continuation framing with `kind: refines`). The kind misrepresents the relationship the body uses.
- `medium` — Prose could read either way; the distinguishing test above is the dialogue prompt for sharpening before capture.

**Migration.** Existing `builds-on` augmenting directives (d-tac-tzw, d-tac-olh, d-tac-4ub) stay as historical state, parallel to the legacy bare-string ref treatment established in d-tac-4ub. No retroactive backfill; prospectively, new augmenting directives use `refines`. Lint does not flag the historical entries; the documentation just names `refines` as the prospective canonical choice for the pattern.

**Scope of work.** Adds one constant to `internal/model/ref.go` (`RefKindRefines`) and threads it through `refKinds` plus `RefKindValues`. Updates the kind table in `cli-reference.md` with the new entry and the distinguishing test against `builds-on`. Updates the ref-kind paragraph in `internal/bundledskills/claude/sdd/SKILL.md`. No changes to the YAML schema, parser, or mechanical pre-flight — the closed-set check already accepts any value the model exposes. Closes alongside d-tac-cs0 via the slice-2 done signal.
