Sharpens AC 9's calibration of the LLM ref-vs-body consistency check (extending d-tac-tzw) along two axes — severity levels for ref-metadata mismatches, and treatment of softer divergences as low-medium signals rather than ignored.

**Direct contradiction → `high`.** When a ref's metadata materially misrepresents how the body frames the reference, pre-flight blocks. Two flavors:

- *Desc contradiction*: the body affirmatively refutes the relationship the desc claims. Example: ref `(kind: grounds, desc: "extends the predecessor contract")` on a contract entry; body says "this directive retires the predecessor contract." Both can't be true.
- *Wrong kind*: the chosen kind misrepresents the body's framing — another kind in the closed set would name the relationship correctly. Examples: ref `(kind: grounds)` on a gap signal whose body says "addresses the temporal-blur gap" — `addresses` is correct (gap signals aren't standing structure); ref `(kind: evidence)` on a plan whose body says "depends on the per-ref kind taxonomy landing first" — `depends-on` is correct (functional prerequisite, not citation); ref `(kind: builds-on)` on a contract the entry leans on without extending — `grounds` is correct.

Finding cites the ref ID and the contradicting body excerpt so the author can resolve unambiguously.

**Softer divergences → `low` or `medium`.** Topical drift, granularity differences, and wording variations — explicitly excluded from the rubric in d-tac-tzw — return as flag-worthy observations, ranged by reader-confusion risk:

- `medium`: the divergence is significant enough that a future reader would reasonably misread the relationship from the desc alone (or from the kind alone, when no desc is present). Example: desc "anchors to dialogue-first principle"; body never names dialogue-first and frames the connection through a different principle.
- `low`: mild variation the reader can reconcile from context. Example: desc names a sub-claim ("extends the type-system kind taxonomy"); body uses the broader frame ("builds on the d-cpt-ydf framework"). Same axis, different focal length.

**No finding** when desc is consistent with the body's framing and kind matches how the body uses the reference. The check is skipped entirely when no ref carries a `desc` *and* every kind reads correctly against the body.

This refines AC 9's calibration without expanding AC 9's scope — the check remains "ref-metadata consistency with body framing, in the pre-flight LLM prompt." d-tac-tzw's AC 8 mechanical-seam guidance and AC 9 integration-point guidance (refs in prompt context via `internal/llm/preflight`) are unchanged.
