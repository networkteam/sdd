Commits to label-aware heat weighting in `sdd view`'s rank algorithm, multiplying each ref's contribution by a per-kind weight before applying decay and summing, so grounding citations decay properly and resolution citations weight fully — closing the heat-conflation gap (s-cpt-zsd) and depending on the per-ref kind structural foundation (d-tac-cs0).

**Weights.** Three buckets:

- `grounds` — 0.25 (light; standing structure shouldn't rank hot from anchoring refs)
- `related` — 0.5 (softer than lineage)
- `builds-on`, `addresses`, `surfaces`, `evidence`, `depends-on` — 1.0 (active engagement)
- `unknown` — 1.0 (legacy parity so existing rankings stay roughly stable at launch)

These are starting values grounded in dialogue intuition and the empirical distribution from cs0's 33-ref sample. The closing done signal validates or revises them via comparative findings.

**Algorithm.** Per-ref contribution becomes `weight × decay(age)`; summed across incoming refs as today. The decay function (exp-14d default, others configurable) stays unchanged. The only change is the weight multiplier in the inner term.

**Dependency.** Implementation gates on d-tac-cs0 shipping. Until then, the algorithm has no `kind` to read — the structural foundation must land first.

**Comparative findings at closure.** The closing done signal carries a findings attachment comparing the heat layout on the live graph with and without label-aware weighting, plus weight-bucket sensitivity (e.g., grounds at 0.1 vs 0.25 vs 0.5; related at 0.25 vs 0.5 vs 0.75). Methodology mirrors d-tac-uww's slice-8 findings.

**Scope boundary.** This directive covers heat weighting only — not filter primitives keyed on kind, not search ranking, not other consumers. Each is its own follow-up if pursued.
