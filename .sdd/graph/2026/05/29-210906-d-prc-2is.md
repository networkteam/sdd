Commit to reducing ref-kind misfires and the non-blocking noise they generate (closes the selection/calibration gap) through two coordinated changes. (1) Body-derived kind selection: choose the kind *after* the body is written, tagging the strongest relational axis the body actually asserts, with `related` reserved as a true last resort gated by a forcing question ("why does no other kind fit?"). Because pre-flight judges kind against the body, keying selection off the body collapses most of the disagreement at its source. (2) One-notch pre-flight recalibration: demote the "defensible but a sharper kind merely exists" case from medium to low (or no finding); keep medium only for genuine undersell where the body asserts a functional axis the kind drops or softens; keep high for outright contradiction. The repo evidence motivates this directly — of 12 `related` refs, 7 sit in the demoted band and 0 contradict, so the medium currently fires on defensible choices and catches no real error. The sharpened rule ideally lives in a single canonical home shared by skill and pre-flight rather than two hand-synced places — the shared-reference work tracked in s-tac-uer; the two interlock, but this directive ships as a stopgap in both places rather than blocking on it. Considered and rejected: removing `related` entirely (too blunt — ~5 of the 12 are genuine, correctly-tagged siblings). Full design, the concrete severity mapping, the build/embed wrinkle, and open questions are in the [attached design note](./29-210906-d-prc-2is/design.md); decomposition into a plan with acceptance criteria is the next step.
