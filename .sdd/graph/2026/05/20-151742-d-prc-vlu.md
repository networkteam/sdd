SDD commits to integrating pre-draft grounding discipline into capture flow — playbook moves and skill-text changes that lead the agent to ground checkable claims before drafting an entry, and to surface ungroundable claims for dialogue rather than echoing them as fact. This addresses the pre-draft grounding gap (20260520-103250-s-prc-6ll), grounded in the dialogue-first aspiration (20260422-122136-d-stg-beb): captured entries should reflect verified claims, not echoed phrasing.

The committed surfaces are playbook moves and skill-text changes — text-instruction enforcement at capture time, with the known failure mode that agents may drift from text rules. A second potential enforcement layer — pre-flight validation that demands grounding for claims in the entry body — stays open as a downstream design question. Both paths share a calibration constraint: the discipline must trigger on claims that genuinely need grounding without expanding to every dialogue claim, or capture becomes ceremony. The unresolved challenge for pre-flight specifically is how to encode that calibration narrowly enough to fire on claims that need grounding without over-firing on background context, framework-internal terms, or claims already grounded by prior graph entries. Calibration resolution is the prerequisite to adopting pre-flight as a second enforcement layer.

The directive deliberately leaves two related conceptual questions open: where verified groundings land as `kind: fact` entries (20260520-133234-s-cpt-sxn fact economics), and how the graph resists echo-chamber drift overall (20260520-132944-s-cpt-psu). Both are graph-hygiene concerns this directive narrows around capture-time discipline only.
