Make pre-flight `decision_refs.tmpl` augment-aware so directives that refine an active plan's AC without superseding stop getting blocked by reflexive supersede/amend findings.

Pre-flight produced contradictory blocking findings on a directive that conformed to the augment-plan pattern (per `s-prc-vko`'s capture). Root cause: the pattern shipped as skill guidance only — `decision_refs.tmpl` knows "supersede or amend" as resolution paths for AC tensions but has no positive frame for "directive refines AC contract while plan stays active." An augmenting directive (decision, refs an active plan, no supersedes, no closes) routes through `decision_refs.tmpl` and gets evaluated against a frame that excludes its actual shape.

This plan teaches `decision_refs.tmpl` to recognize the augmenting-directive shape and treat AC-refinement-via-directive as a first-class resolution path alongside supersession. Scope is local to that template (with calibration adjustments in `verdict.tmpl` if the verdict-level reflex needs softening too). Broader template-vs-skill knowledge asymmetry — every pattern that ships in the skill should also ship template-side awareness — is out of scope; logged in `s-prc-vvd`'s family.

Alternative considered: structural fix making pattern-shipping symmetric. Rejected for now — too broad, would defer the immediate unblock. The structural angle remains visible via `s-prc-vvd`.

See [plan](./06-172854-d-tac-ghm/plan.md) for the dispatch analysis, the per-finding root-cause walk, and the design notes for the template change.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `decision_refs.tmpl` defines and names the "augmenting-directive shape": a directive that refs an active plan, has no `supersedes`, has no `closes` on the plan, and refines/clarifies an AC of the ref'd plan.
- [ ] When the proposed entry matches the augmenting-directive shape, AC tension between the directive and the ref'd plan does not reflexively trigger supersede or amend-AC findings; AC-refinement-via-directive is enumerated as a permitted resolution path.
- [ ] When the proposed entry matches the augmenting-directive shape, the template does not ask for the plan to be amended to backward-ref the directive — one-way relationship and `sdd show --downstream` visibility are explicit.
- [ ] When the prose names the augment-plan pattern by ID (`d-prc-9ti`), the template does not ask for it as a ref; graph-mechanics meta-patterns are not refable per-entry.
- [ ] The augmenting-directive lifecycle (open across plan implementation, closed by the plan's done signal alongside the plan itself) is recognized — not flagged as dangling commitment or closure-sequencing-ambiguity.
- [ ] LLM rubric tests under `internal/llm/preflight_eval_test.go` cover the augmenting-directive shape with a positive case (clean augment, no high findings) and a negative case (genuine supersession that should still be flagged).
- [ ] Re-running pre-flight on a reconstructed augmenting-directive matching the original blocked attempt (resolving topic-filter ownership in `d-tac-gvn`) produces no `[high]` findings tied to the augment-plan pattern.
