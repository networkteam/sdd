Holds the catch-up playbook sweep that follows once Plan 2 (`d-tac-uww`) ships `sdd view` and Plan 1 (`d-tac-gvn`) ships the `kind: focus` and `kind: annotation` infrastructure. Scope: rewrite `references/playbook-catchup.md` to use the new pipeline-based CLI, define light / full / drill catch-up moves with concrete `sdd view --layout=...` invocations, integrate topic clustering and focus-narrative rendering per the new kinds, and update skill-level routing to call `sdd view` from these moves. The existing `sdd status` behavior is unchanged by this activity; deprecation of `sdd status` is a separate decision deferred to a future directive once `sdd view` is well-adopted.

Trigger: both Plan 1 and Plan 2 done signals have landed. The activity does not begin until the underlying infrastructure is in place; agents and humans continue using the existing `sdd status`-driven catch-up until then.

The closing done signal will close this activity, `d-tac-kv5` (quick catch-up directive — operationalized when light mode lands in the playbook), and `d-tac-qom` (topic-drill directive — operationalized when drill mode lands in the playbook).
