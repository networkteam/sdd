Shipped the `not(<filter>)` negation primitive in `sdd view` per the plan d-tac-e1s, scoped to negating `kind`, `layer`, and `topic` filters. All eight acceptance criteria land with test coverage; live smoke against the project graph confirms the practical win surfaced by s-tac-x1n — `top(20):not(kind(contract,aspiration))` removes the structural anchors that previously dominated the rankings and replaces them with actionable signals and recent done events. One small specification clarification crystallised during implementation: multiple `not(kind(...))` calls union their exclusion sets (asymmetric to the positive side's intersection of disjunctions, which is the right semantic for negation but worth naming since the plan AC was silent on the multi-call case).

## AC coverage

- AC 1 — `not(kind(K1, K2, ...))` excludes those kinds, composes with positive `kind()` under intersection. Covered by `TestView_NotKind_ExcludesKinds` and `TestView_NotKind_ComposesWithPositiveKind`.
- AC 2 — `not(layer(L))` excludes that layer. `TestView_NotLayer_ExcludesLayer`.
- AC 3 — `not(topic(L))` excludes by component-wise prefix. `TestView_NotTopic_ExcludesTopic`. Implementation reuses the existing `TopicFilter` type with a new `ExcludeEntries` method paralleling `FilterEntries`.
- AC 4 — unsupported inner errors with listed-supported-set message. `TestView_Not_RejectsUnsupportedInner` covers `not(active)`, `not(since(...))`, and nested `not(not(...))`. The bare-ident path (`not(active)` parses as ident, not function) was wired to surface the same supported-inner list as the function-mismatch path so the error reads consistently regardless of which shape the user tried.
- AC 5 — arity errors. `TestView_Not_ArityErrors` covers zero and multi-arg cases.
- AC 6 — `not` registered in `knownFunctions`. `TestView_Not_RegisteredInKnownFunctions` confirms misspellings like `nott` produce the listed-valid-set error including `not`.
- AC 7 — `cli-reference.md` adds one Filters-table row and one worked example (`top(20):not(kind(contract,aspiration))`), no expanded rationale or alternatives commentary. Source-of-truth and installed copy in sync.
- AC 8 — eight executor tests, three positive plus three error classes (rejection, arity, shadow). All passing on `go test ./...`.

## Specification clarification — multi-call semantics

The plan AC for `not(kind(...))` named composition with positive `kind(...)` ("positive includes ∩ negative excludes") but did not specify multiple `not(kind(...))` calls. Implementation chose **union** for the negation side: `not(kind(A,B)):not(kind(C))` drops entries whose kind is in `{A, B, C}`. This is the natural inverse of the positive side's intersection — multiple negative filters each independently exclude, so combining them widens the exclusion. Storage is a flat `excludeKinds []model.Kind` rather than the slice-of-slices shape the positive `kindFilters` uses, since unioning at append time has no observable difference from unioning at apply time.

`not(layer(...))` and `not(topic(...))` follow last-write-wins per their positive counterparts, since both produce a single-value field. Multiple calls of either are accepted but only the last one binds — consistent with the positive side and unsurprising.
