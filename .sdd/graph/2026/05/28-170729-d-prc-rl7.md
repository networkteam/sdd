This sharpens d-prc-nkw's commitments in place by constraining the shape of the eventual implementation, leaving the architecture itself intact. Procedural shape removes the interpretation latitude that principle shape leaves open — that is the hypothesis. The directional learning is medium confidence: principle text demonstrably did not close the gap, but procedural text is not yet proven to. This constraint binds d-prc-nkw's follow-up implementation plan, which should also integrate d-prc-vlu's capture-time grounding commitment as one of the procedural moments rather than shipping it through a separate stream — both directives share implementation surface at the capture moment and would otherwise duplicate or contradict.
