Single-level summaries don't fit every consumer. The current summary (~3–5 sentences) is right for `sdd status`, `sdd list`, and `sdd show`'s primary surface where reader bandwidth is wider; but compact mechanical entry representation is missing — there's no short identity-form for a referenced entry to render inside another entry's row, for grouped roll-ups, for topic-counts aggregations, or for any future surface where many entries appear as nested or compact items.

Proposes a `short_summary` field (~50–80 chars, single phrase) alongside the existing `summary`, generated via the same LLM path with a tighter prompt and regenerated together on entry capture or `sdd summarize`. Surface conventions: existing summary renders unchanged; short_summary becomes available wherever density matters.

Beneficiaries form the consumer side of a small constellation. `expand(refs)` (`d-tac-yls`, blocked on `d-tac-cs0`) would adopt short_summary as a trailing clause `— <short_summary>` for fully-loaded per-ref context. Together with `d-tac-cs0`'s per-ref `kind` (relationship semantics) and `desc` (per-ref narrative) and `d-tac-yls`'s derived status, short_summary completes the four primitives an agent needs to thread refs correctly: identity, relationship+narrative, current state, referenced-entry compact form. Other consumers: `s-cpt-sy4`'s topic-counts aggregation render would gain a per-entry phrase in counts output; future cross-section dedup or topic-rollup surfaces would have a tighter unit to work with.

Sibling concern to summary-body drift (`s-cpt-tdp`) — both are about rendered text not reflecting current truth, addressed at different layers. Orthogonal to per-ref `desc` from `d-tac-cs0`: `desc` is per-ref narrative ("why entry A references entry B"); short_summary describes the referenced entry's own compact form ("what entry B is about").

Low confidence — capture as forward direction. Friction in the current single-level summary is observable (catch-up's per-entry summary lines are full sentences, eating context budget across 15+ open-and-warm entries) but not yet a sharp pain point. Resolution likely a small structural plan: add the field to the model, extend the summarization prompt, render-side adoption via small directives per consumer.
