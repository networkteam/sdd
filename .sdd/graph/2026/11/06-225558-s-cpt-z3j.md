Should SDD give entries a draft state, so that an entry written without dialogue stays open until a dialogue takes it up, with intake (20260603-172628-d-cpt-fbi) as the case for items arriving from outside?

The question arose in a meeting between Christopher and Benno on 2026-11-06 and a follow-up dialogue. Three cases want an entry that is written but not yet settled:

- A team member is fairly sure of a decision but wants it checked before it becomes one. Benno noted that for people not used to deciding, a draft they can still align feels different from a decision.
- Background agents running procedures prepare captures with no person in the dialogue. 20261106-174926-d-cpt-kx0 leaves such captures open as a case, and 20260410-205013-s-cpt-bn1 already wants mined candidates reviewed before they count.
- Items from issue trackers, email or chat, which d-cpt-fbi captures as intake signals: open until deliberately closed, held out of catch-up, counted for triage.

In the meeting Christopher questioned intake as a classification by how an item arrived and whether it was processed, not by what it is in the project ("eher technisch als semantisch"), and proposed that such an item could be a point of view (20261106-225324-s-cpt-d5x). In the follow-up he kept intake: a separate entry type for what comes in from the outside world has benefits, and a draft state could build on it.

Candidate answer, unsettled: the demand comes from the skipped dialogue, not from the entry's kind. An entry written without dialogue, whether from a channel, by a background agent or as a draft someone wants checked, stays on the attention surface until a dialogue entry references, closes or settles it, while its kind stays semantic. Open within that answer: whether the state is derived from provenance or set as an attribute, as directive intent is, and whether intake then stays a kind or becomes the draft state of an outside item. Capturing directly, without a draft, stays possible.

The intake kind is not built yet, so the answer should come before it is. The meeting excerpts are attached: [meeting excerpts](./06-225558-s-cpt-z3j/draft-state-meeting-excerpts.md).
